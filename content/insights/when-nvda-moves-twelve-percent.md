The week that closed yesterday left a useful public tape for anyone raising into a US listing path with China in the story. Major US indices finished higher: S&P 500 about +2.5%, Nasdaq about +2.3%, Dow about +2.1%. On the same delayed watchlists, Nvidia fell about 12.75%, and the China ADR basket averaged roughly −7.7%, with names like NIO and Alibaba among the sharpest declines. That split matters more than any single headline. The broad market can look calm while the names that set the mood for China tech and AI policy look violent.

Founders sometimes treat a week like this as a signal to rewrite the entire narrative overnight. That is usually the wrong move. The tape did not invent your manufacturing map, your customer mix, or your counsel’s China-exposure page. It changed how quickly a partner who just scrolled past Nvidia and China ADR red will ask the same questions you already knew were coming. Your job is to meet those questions with paper that was ready before the week began, not with a slide that chases the close.

Start with language, not with price targets. If your deck still leans on “AI infrastructure” metaphors without saying where compute, tooling, and supply sit, a week when Nvidia is the loudest ticker will drag every vague metaphor into a policy conversation you did not schedule. Say what you buy, from whom, under which export or end-use constraints you already track, and what you do if a licence or a vendor path narrows. Investors who own public mega-cap tech will not expect you to forecast Nvidia. They will expect you to survive a room that spent the week watching it.

Separate your company’s China operating exposure from the ADR complex. Soft China ADR weeks train US readers to compress every China-touching private company into the same risk bucket. Push back with a diagram, not with optimism. Customers, people, cash, IP, data, permissions — each on a line. Then say which of those lines would move if listing sentiment for China names stayed soft for another quarter. Soft sentiment is a calendar and pricing problem. It is not automatically a product problem. Mixing the two makes both harder to underwrite.

Use the week’s delayed data as a diligence warm-up, not as investment advice. Ask yourself the questions a banker will ask on Monday: if China ADRs keep printing weak closes while the S&P holds up, how do you talk about timing for a US process? Do you have a bridge endgame, a Hong Kong parallel path, or a strategic that does not depend on a hot China ADR tape? Write the answer in one paragraph. If the paragraph needs three different audiences to agree before it is true, you do not yet have an answer.

Keep biotech and hardware founders honest about second-order effects. A loud Nvidia week can pull attention toward chip-export politics even when your company never touches GPUs. Assay equipment, cloud spend, and CRO timelines can all get asked about through an export-control lens once that lens is open. Put the boring truth in the folder: what you import, what you license, what stays on-prem, what a US reader can verify without a phone call to Shenzhen at midnight. Boring truth ages better than a rewrite tuned to one Friday close.

Do not let the index rally fool you into silence. Broad indices up and China ADRs down is exactly the pattern that produces mismatched rooms: someone cites the S&P as proof risk is fine, someone else cites Nvidia and Alibaba as proof China tech is un-investable this month. Your meeting will contain both people. Prepare one sentence that acknowledges the week’s public moves without pretending they are your forecast, then return to your gates — data room, counsel note, milestone calendar. The week gave you free rehearsal material. Use it. Do not trade it, and do not let it write your strategy.

昨收的這一週，給任何走美國上市路徑、故事裏有中國的人，留下一份有用的公開行情。美股主要指數收高：標普 500 約升 2.5%，納斯達克約升 2.3%，道瓊斯約升 2.1%。同一批延遲觀察名單上，英偉達約跌 12.75%，中概股籃子平均約跌 7.7%，蔚來、阿里巴巴等跌幅居前。這個分裂比任何單一標題更重要。大市可以看起來平靜，定調中國科技與人工智能政策氣氛的名字卻可以很劇烈。

創辦人有時把這樣的一週當成一夜之間重寫整套敘事的信號——那通常是錯的。行情沒有發明你的製造地圖、客戶結構，或律師的中國風險一頁。它改變的是：剛滑過英偉達與中概股紅色的合夥人，會多快問出那些你本來就知道會來的問題。你的工作是用這週開始前就準備好的紙去接問題，而不是用追逐收市的投影片。

先從語言入手，不要從目標價入手。若簡報仍靠「人工智能基建」隱喻，卻不說算力、工具與供應落在哪，英偉達成為最響代碼的一週，會把每個含糊隱喻拖進你沒排程的政策對話。說清楚你買甚麼、向誰買、你已在追蹤哪些出口或最終用途限制，以及牌照或供應商路徑收窄時你做甚麼。持有公開大型科技股的投資者不會指望你預測英偉達；他們會指望你活過一個剛看了一整週英偉達的會議室。

把公司的中國營運敞口與中概股複合體分開。中概股偏氣氛偏軟的一週，會訓練美國讀者把每家沾中國的私人公司壓進同一個風險桶。用圖推回去，不要用樂觀。客戶、人、現金、知識產權、數據、許可——各佔一行。然後說：若中國名字的上市情緒再軟一個季度，哪些行會動。軟情緒是日曆與定價問題，不自動等於產品問題。兩者混在一起，兩邊都更難看穿。

把這週的延遲數據當盡調熱身，不要當投資建議。問自己銀行家星期一會問的：若中概股繼續弱收、標普仍撐住，你如何談美國流程的時機？你有沒有過橋結局、香港平行路徑，或不依賴中概股熱行情的戰略交易？用一段話寫答案。若這段話要三個不同聽眾先同意才算真，你還沒有答案。

生技與硬體創辦人也要對二階效應誠實。英偉達很響的一週，即使你的公司從不碰圖形處理器，也會把注意力拉向芯片出口政治。檢測設備、雲支出、合約研究組織時間表，一旦出口管制鏡頭打開，都可能被這樣問。把無聊的真相放進資料夾：你進口甚麼、授權甚麼、甚麼留在本地、美國讀者不用半夜打去深圳也能核實甚麼。無聊的真相比按某個週五收市改寫的版本更耐放。

不要讓指數上漲騙你沉默。大盤升、中概跌，正好製造錯位的會議室：有人用標普證明風險沒問題，有人用英偉達與阿里證明這個月中國科技不可投。你的會議兩種人都會有。準備一句話承認這週的公開波動，但不要假裝那是你的預測，然後回到你的關口——資料室、律師備註、里程碑日曆。這週給了你免費的彩排材料。用它。不要交易它，也不要讓它替你寫策略。
