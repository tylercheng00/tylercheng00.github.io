The meeting after the meeting is the one that counts算數的是會議之後的那場會

You leave thinking it went well. The meeting that decides anything happens after you have gone, with people who did not hear your tone.你離開時覺得很好。真正做決定的會在你走了之後，跟一些沒聽到你語氣的人。

The meeting you prepared for lasts forty-five minutes. The questions were friendly. Someone said the science was interesting. Then you leave, and the meeting that matters happens with a note, a deck and ten minutes.

Within a day, send a recap the person you met can paste to their partners. Two sentences on what you do. The point they seemed to doubt, answered without a speech. What you are asking for. What you will send next, and on which day. No new adjectives. If your recap is longer than a page, someone else will summarise it, and you will not choose the summary. Write it for the partner who missed the meeting. They are the audience.

People are polite in the room and blunter in the corridor. If you sensed a worry, the structure, the single customer, the unfinished method, put the plain version in the recap, with the fact. Don't wait for them to formulate it badly while you are gone. A worry you name is smaller in that second meeting than a worry someone invents from a vague sentence on page nine.

Decide before you leave who on your side answers the next email. If three founders all reply, the thread forks and the fund sees a company that has not picked a voice. One voice, copying the others, is enough.

"What happens on your side now, and when?" is a fair question at the end of a good meeting. The answer tells you whether there is a committee, a date, a partner who still needs to see you, or a person who enjoyed the conversation. Enjoyment does not survive the second meeting unless someone will spend political capital to put you on an agenda. If they will not tell you the process, assume there isn't one yet, and don't count the meeting as a live round.

You did the performance. Then write the note that has to perform without you. That note is the one with a vote attached, whether or not anyone calls it that.

你準備的那場會大概四十五分鐘。問題客氣。有人說科學有趣。然後你走了。要緊的那場會，是一份紀錄、一份簡報和十分鐘。

一天之內送一段他們可以貼給合夥人的回顧。兩句你做甚麼。他們似乎懷疑的那一點，不用演講就答了。你要甚麼。下一步送甚麼、哪一天。不要新的形容詞。回顧長過一頁，別人會摘要，摘要不會由你選。寫給缺席的那位合夥人。他才是讀者。

人在房間裡客氣，在走廊裡直接。你感覺到一個擔心，架構、單一客戶、沒做完的方法，就把平實的版本和事實放進回顧。不要等他們在你不在時把它講得很差。你點了名的擔心，在第二場會裡，比別人從第九頁一句含糊的話編出來的擔心更小。

離開之前決定下一封郵件誰回。三位創辦人都回，討論會分叉，基金看到的是一家還沒選好聲音的公司。一個聲音，抄送其他人，就夠。

「你們那邊現在會發生甚麼，何時？」在一場不錯的會結束時是公平的問題。答案告訴你有沒有委員會、有沒有日期、有沒有一位還要見你的合夥人，或者只是一個人覺得這次聊天不錯。聊天的愉快活不過第二場，除非有人願意花自己的政治資本把你放上議程。他們不願講流程，就假設目前沒有，不要把這次會算成一輪活着的融資。

你做了那場表演。然後寫那份必須在沒有你的時候表演的紀錄。那份紀錄才帶着一票，無論有沒有人這樣叫它。
