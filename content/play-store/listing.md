## Data safety form

- Does the app collect or share user data? **No.** Answers are stored only on the device.
- The app opens the website for booking (Cal.com) and WhatsApp; those are covered by the website's privacy policy.
- Visit counts use GoatCounter (no cookies, no personal data, no IP storage). If Play Console asks, declare "App activity → page views", not linked to the user, not shared, for analytics.

## Why the app does not sell the full report

Google Play requires Play Billing for digital content bought inside an app. The app is therefore free with no payments; the full report is sold only on the website. The app's own page (`app.html`) hides the Stripe button, and so does every page opened from inside the app.
