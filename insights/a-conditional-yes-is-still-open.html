A conditional yes is still open work附條件的「可以」：為何仍不算融資談成

Mid-November filings pressure and chip-politics headlines sharpen the "if". Treat every conditional yes like a deliverable with an owner.峰會前氣氛再熱，投資人說「可以，如果……」時，創辦人仍要把條件當成未完成的功課。

US capital markets美國資本市場16 Nov 20262026年11月16日7 min read閱讀約 7 分鐘

On 16 November 2026 from Hong Kong, the feed is already in pre-APEC Shenzhen week mode. Leaders and business forums are being previewed; US–China capital tension sits in every second briefing; Jensen Huang and Nvidia chip-sale politics are already a standing news item, not a future surprise. Separately, mid-month China-linked filing calendars are busy: counsel redlines, risk-factor pages, and data-room packs that must be true before anyone books a post-summit roadshow. In that weather, investors still say yes with a hinge. Founders hear the yes. The room should hear the if.

A conditional yes is useful. It tells you what would make capital move under a noisy tape. It is also a trap if you mark the name as closed because the summit week “should clarify sentiment.” Macro clarity is not your close. The condition is now your job. Put an owner on it. Put a date that ignores the APEC photo schedule. Put the evidence you will send — audit comfort, customer reference, China-exposure paragraph counsel has actually read. If you cannot name those three, you do not have a path. You have a compliment timed to a busy news week.

I keep conditional yeses in the same list as open diligence requests. Same column. Same follow-up rhythm. The psychology is different for the founder — it feels warmer than a no, especially when everyone is talking about Asia-Pacific opportunity — but the work is the same. Someone still has to produce a file, a call, or a clean answer that survives a partner who just skimmed another chip-export headline.

Watch which conditions proliferate this week. “Yes if the China map is clearer” is not a soft ask when US readers are pricing listing risk off diplomacy and export-control coverage. “Yes after counsel is comfortable” means counsel must see the paragraphs that mention supply chains, related parties, compute, or bay-area manufacturing before you reuse them at a Shenzhen breakfast. “Yes after the next customer call” still needs a date that is not “after APEC.” Write the gate in plain language. If the gate only exists as summit atmosphere, you do not have a conditional yes. You have a pause dressed as progress.

When the condition clears, confirm in writing that the yes still stands. People change seats between pre-summit briefings and post-summit travel. Memories soften. A week of silence after you send the audit comfort letter is not a close. It is another open item — and in a mid-month filings window, open items compound. The buyer who liked you on Monday may be deep in another China diligence pack by Friday.

Practical hygiene for this calendar:

1. List every “yes, if” with owner, evidence, and a date that is not a summit day.
2. Send the feared paragraphs to counsel before you send the warm follow-up to the investor.
3. Update the board with cleared conditions and remaining conditions — not with how optimistic the APEC week “feels.”
4. Refuse to spend as if conditional money were wired. Runway in weeks still rules.

Celebrate when money moves. Until then, treat every “yes, if” as unfinished work with a name on it. Pre-APEC buildup, US–China capital tension, and chip politics will keep moving the mood. Your conditions only move when someone delivers the file.

This is operating discipline for a noisy mid-November, not investment advice and not a forecast of summit outcomes.

以香港時間計，2026年11月16日，版面已進入深圳亞太經合組織（APEC）週前的預熱：領袖與工商論壇預告佔版，美中資本氣氛反覆，黃仁勳與英偉達芯片銷售爭議也成了常駐新聞。另一邊，月中與中國相關的上市申報窗口很擠——律師紅字、風險因素頁、資料室材料，都要在任何人預訂峰會後路演之前站得住。這種天氣裏，投資人仍習慣帶着前提說「可以」。不少創辦人只聽見「可以」；會議室真正該聽見的，是那個「如果」。

附條件的同意並非沒有用。它至少告訴你：在嘈雜行情下，資金要動起來，還差哪一塊。問題在於，一旦把名字標成「已談成」，只因為峰會週「應該會讓情緒變清楚」，這句話就變成陷阱。宏觀敘事清不清，替代不了你的交割。條件現在是你的工作：誰負責、哪天交、要交什麼證據——審計安心函、客戶推薦、律師真正讀過的中國風險段落。三樣說不出來，你就沒有路徑，只有一句趕在忙碌新聞週送出的客氣話。

較務實的做法，是把「可以，如果……」和未完成的盡職調查——也就是核查文件、數字與關聯安排的過程——放進同一份清單、同一欄、同一套跟進節奏。對創辦人來說心理不同：它比直接拒絕溫暖，尤其當人人都在談亞太機會。但工作一樣。仍要有人交出檔案、打通一通電話，或給出一個乾淨答案，而且要經得起剛掃過又一則芯片出口標題的合夥人追問。

留意這週會增生哪些條件。市場更關心的是：「中國地圖講清楚才可以」——在美國讀者正按外交與出口管制報道替上市風險定價時，這不是軟要求。「律師覺得可以才可以」——意思是供應鏈、關聯方、算力或灣區製造等段落，要在深圳早餐再講之前，先給律師看。「下一個客戶電話之後才可以」——仍需要一個不是「APEC之後」的日期。用白話把門檻寫出來。若門檻只存在於峰會氣氛裏，你就沒有附條件的同意，只有停頓扮成進度。

條件清掉之後，書面確認那份同意仍然成立。從峰會前簡報到峰會後出行，座位會換人，記憶會變軟。審計安心函寄出後一週沉默，不是交割，是另一個未完成項；而在月中申報窗口，未完成項會疊加。星期一喜歡你的買家，星期五可能已埋在另一份中國盡職材料裏。

這個日曆下較穩妥的衛生習慣：

1. 列出每一個「可以，如果……」，附負責人、證據，以及不是峰會日的日期。
2. 先把害怕的段落寄給律師，再寄溫熱的跟進給投資人。
3. 向董事會報告已清與未清的條件，不要報告APEC週「感覺」多樂觀。
4. 拒絕像有條件的錢已到賬一樣花錢；以星期計的現金跑道仍作主。

錢動了再慶祝。在那之前，把每一個「可以，如果……」當成掛着名字的未完工作。APEC前預熱、美中資本緊張與芯片政治會繼續拉動情緒；你的條件，只有在有人交出檔案時才會動。

以上是嘈雜十一月中旬的營運紀律，不是投資建議，也不是對峰會結果的預測。
