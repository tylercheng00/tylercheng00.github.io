::: en
I recently helped a company raise USD 5 million in 45 days. The client has asked to stay anonymous, so I will not talk about who they are. But the experience changed how I think about speed in fundraising, and that part is worth sharing.

The short version: a fast round is rarely about luck or about one great meeting. Most of the speed is decided before you meet anyone.

### The story has to fit on one page

If the founders cannot explain, on a single page, what the company does, why now, and what the money is for, no deck will save them. Investors forward that page internally. If it is vague, the forwarding stops.

### "Use of funds" should read like a plan, not a wish

"Expansion and working capital" tells an investor nothing. "Two hires, one production line, eighteen months of runway to the next milestone" tells them exactly what they are buying. Specific numbers make it easier to say yes.

### Fewer investors, better chosen

It is tempting to send the deck to everyone. In my view that slows things down. A shorter list of investors who have already done similar deals means fewer first meetings that go nowhere, and more second meetings that go somewhere.

### Have the answers before the questions

Every serious investor asks for the same things: financials, cap table, contracts, IP. If those are sitting in an organised data room on day one, a question asked on Monday gets answered on Monday. When answers take a week, so does every decision.

### Rhythm and a real deadline

Momentum is fragile. A steady follow-up rhythm, and a closing date that everyone believes, keeps a round moving. Without a deadline, "we're interested" can stay "we're interested" for months.

None of this is clever. It is mostly preparation and discipline. That is exactly why it works.

::: zh
我最近協助一家企業在 45 日內完成 500 萬美元融資。客戶希望保持匿名，所以我不會透露是誰。但這次經歷改變了我對「融資速度」的看法，這部分值得分享。

簡單來說：一輪融資之所以快，很少是因為運氣，也不是因為某一次精彩的會面。大部分的速度，在見任何人之前已經決定。

### 故事要寫得進一頁紙

如果創辦人不能在一頁紙內說清楚公司做甚麼、為甚麼是現在、錢用來做甚麼，再漂亮的簡報也救不了。投資者會把這一頁轉發給內部同事。內容含糊，轉發就到此為止。

### 「資金用途」要像計劃，而不是願望

「擴張業務及營運資金」對投資者來說等於沒說。「聘請兩個人、建一條生產線、足夠支撐十八個月直到下一個里程碑」才讓他們清楚知道自己在買甚麼。具體的數字，令人更容易說「好」。

### 投資者不在多，在於選得準

把簡報發給所有人很吸引，但我認為這反而會拖慢進度。名單短一點、集中在做過類似交易的投資者，第一次會面白費的機會就少，能走到第二次會面的就多。

### 問題未來，答案已經準備好

認真的投資者都會要同一批資料：財務、股權結構、合約、知識產權。如果第一天就整理好放在資料室，星期一問的問題，星期一就有答案。答覆要一星期，每個決定也就要一星期。

### 節奏和一個真正的期限

勢頭很容易散。穩定的跟進節奏，加上一個大家都相信的截止日期，融資才會一直向前。沒有期限，「我們有興趣」可以一直停留在「我們有興趣」好幾個月。

這些都不是甚麼妙計，主要是準備和紀律。正因如此，才有效。
