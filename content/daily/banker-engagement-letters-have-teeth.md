::: en
Engagement letters arrive when momentum feels good. Slow down for the economics and the exit. Success fees, retainer, what happens if you pause, what happens if you hire another advisor. Those lines matter more than the pitch meeting.

Have counsel read it. A week of delay is cheaper than a year of a misaligned mandate.

::: zh
聘用函常在動能正好時到來。為經濟條款和退出放慢。成功費、顧問費、你暫停怎麼辦、另請顧問怎麼辦。這些行比簡報會重要。

請律師讀。延遲一週，便宜過錯配一年的委託。
