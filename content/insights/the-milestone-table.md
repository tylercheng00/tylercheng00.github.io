::: en
Most milestone tables are calendars. Complete the formulation. Hire the team. File the patent. All of those can be ticked while an outsider still couldn't name a risk that got smaller.

I ask a rude question of each row. If this is done, what are you no longer afraid of?

Four things, and then stop. The risk, in one sentence. The evidence that would count. What it costs. How long it takes if the answer is bad, not only if it is good. A row with a plan only for success is a hope with a budget. Skip the column called strategic importance. If the risk sentence is clear, the importance is obvious. If it isn't, an adjective will not save it.

"Positive data" is not evidence. Three independent batches inside a specification, made by someone other than the inventor, is evidence. Write the evidence as a thing that can sit in a folder, not as a feeling the founder will report. The scientist should be able to draft the row and the investor should be able to read it. That is the point of having the table at all.

If every row must succeed or the company dies, you don't have milestones. You have one bet, chopped into chapters. Sometimes that is the truth, and you should say so. More often the plan pretends the steps are independent and the appendix shows they aren't. Show the dependency. A board can fund a dependent plan. It cannot fund one that pretends not to be.

You will slip. When you do, don't just move the date and leave the sentence alone, as if the same risk changed quarters. Sometimes the slip taught you something, and the row should change. A table that only ever moves right, words untouched, stops being a tool. It becomes a record of optimism.

Use the same table with investors and with yourselves. If you need two, one of them is for show.

::: zh
多數里程碑表其實是日曆。完成製劑。聘人。交專利。這些都可以打勾，而外人仍然講不出哪個風險變小了。

我對每一行問一句不大禮貌的話。做完了，你不再怕甚麼？

四樣，然後停。風險，一句。怎樣的證據才算數。多少錢。如果結果是壞的，要多久，不只是好的時候。只有成功計劃的那一行，是一個有預算的希望。不要「策略重要性」那一欄。風險那句清楚，重要性就明顯。不清楚，形容詞救不了。

「正面數據」不是證據。三批獨立、落在標準裡、而且不是發明人自己做的，才比較像。證據要是可以放進資料夾的東西，不是創辦人會報告的感覺。科學家寫得出、投資者讀得懂。不然要這張表幹嘛。

如果每一行都必須成功公司才活得了，你沒有里程碑。你有一個賭注，拆成幾章。有時這就是實情，應該這樣說。更常見的是計劃假裝各步獨立，附錄又露出它們不是。把依賴畫出來。董事會可以為有依賴的計劃出錢。它不能為一個假裝沒有依賴的出錢。

你會延誤。延誤的時候，不要只改日期、句子原封不動，好像同一個風險換了季度。有時延誤教了你一件事，那一行該改。一張只會往右移、字永不改的表，不再是工具。它是樂觀的記錄。

給投資者看的和你們自己用的，用同一張。要兩張的話，其中一張是演出。
