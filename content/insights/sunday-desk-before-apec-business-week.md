::: en
Sunday in Hong Kong is the last soft edge before an APEC business week. Leaders and ministers will not fill every hour of your calendar, but they will fill the information diet of the people you meet. US investors, bankers, and counsel will arrive on Monday already primed by preview pieces about Shenzhen, trade, investment facilitation, and the usual secondary storylines about major-power meetings. Your desk work today is to decide what of that noise enters your company voice, and what stays outside it.

Start with a coverage roster for the next five working days. Who owns inbound diligence email. Who can approve a data-room unlock. Who joins a 8 a.m. New York call if the CEO is in Shenzhen. Who has authority to say “we will come back tomorrow” without sounding evasive. Put mobile numbers and time zones on one page. Summit weeks break ordinary escalation paths. Broken escalation paths turn small asks into delayed answers, and delayed answers read as disorganisation when the news is already loud.

Second, freeze the narrative pack. One China-operating map. One compute-and-tools paragraph if AI or cross-border tooling appears in the deck. One timing paragraph that separates your gates from market weather. One related-party schedule. Do not open a redesign of the whole pitch on Sunday night. Redesigns under anticipation produce slides that age in forty-eight hours. Frozen packs produce consistent answers across three time zones.

Third, pre-write the Monday openers. For a US fund: acknowledge APEC week in one clause, then state what you want from the call — feedback on the file, a process next step, or a clear no. For a banker: ask which readiness gaps still block a kickoff, given last week’s public tape of firm indices beside soft China ADRs and a sharp Nvidia move. For a strategic: ask what decision forum they need before year-end, and what you must deliver into that forum. Openers that chase summit commentary waste the only scarce resource in a busy week: attention.

Fourth, set a news diet for the executive team. Two people may skim geopolitics; everyone else stays on customers, lab, and file hygiene. Founders who personally refresh summit coverage all day become slower on diligence. Slowness is visible. Assign the skim, schedule a fifteen-minute debrief at 6 p.m., and protect the rest of the day for work that still matters when the cameras leave.

Fifth, decide the announcement blackout. Soft fundraising updates, speculative dual-track talk, and “we might meet someone around APEC” messages belong offline this week unless they are closed facts. Closed facts can still ship: signed documents, cleared audits, finished studies. Atmosphere cannot. Atmosphere is already oversupplied by the summit itself.

Biotech desks should add a Sunday check on site and CRO reachability. Confirm who can host a quality or method-transfer call if a US diligence thread opens midweek. Confirm which batch records are already PDF-stable in the room. Summit travel makes physical sites harder to visit; remote readiness becomes the difference between a one-day answer and a one-week stumble.

Close the Sunday desk with a single email to the board: roster attached, narrative pack frozen, blackout rules stated, and the two meetings that matter most this week named. Boards do not need your geopolitical essay. They need proof the company will sound the same on Tuesday as on Thursday. That sameness is what APEC business weeks reward. Noise is free. Consistency is the work you can still finish today.

香港的星期日，是 APEC 商務週前最後一道軟邊界。領袖與部長不會填滿你日曆的每一小時，但會填滿你要見的人的信息飲食。美國投資者、銀行家與律師星期一出現時，已被深圳、貿易、投資便利，以及大國會面等次要故事線的預告稿預熱。你今天的枱頭工作，是決定哪些噪音進入公司聲音，哪些留在外面。

先做未來五個工作天的值班名單：誰負責進站盡調電郵；誰能批准打開資料室；若行政總裁在深圳，誰上紐約早上八時的電話；誰有權說「我們明天回覆」而不顯得回避。把手機與時區放在一頁。峰會週會打斷平常升級路徑；打斷的升級路徑把小請求變成延遲答案——新聞已很響時，延遲讀起來就像失調。

第二，凍結敘事包：一張中國營運圖；若簡報出現人工智能或跨境工具，一段算力與工具說明；一段把你的關口與市場天氣分開的時機文字；一份關聯方表。星期日晚上不要打開整份簡報重設計。在預期下重設計，會產出四十八小時就過時的投影片；凍結的包，能在三個時區給出一致答案。

第三，預寫星期一開場。對美國基金：用一個子句承認 APEC 週，然後說你要這通電話給甚麼——對檔案的意見、流程下一步，或清楚的婉拒。對銀行家：問在上週公開行情——指數偏強、中概偏軟、英偉達大幅波動——之下，哪些就緒缺口仍擋住開場。對戰略方：問年底前他們需要哪個決策論壇，你必須交進那個論壇甚麼。追逐峰會評論的開場，浪費忙碌一週裏唯一稀缺的資源：注意力。

第四，為管理層設定新聞飲食。兩個人可以掃好地緣政治；其餘人留在客戶、實驗室與檔案衛生。整天親自刷峰會報道的創辦人，盡調會變慢——慢是看得見的。指定掃讀的人，傍晚六時排十五分鐘匯報，其餘時間保護仍會在鏡頭離開後重要的工作。

第五，決定公告靜默。軟性融資更新、投機的雙軌說法、「我們也許在 APEC 期間見某人」這類信息，本週除非是已敲定的事實，否則留在線下。已敲定的事實仍可發布：簽了的文件、過關的審計、做完的研究。氣氛不行——氣氛已被峰會本身供過於求。

生技枱頭應在星期日加一項場地與合約研究組織可達性檢查。確認若美國盡調線在週中打開，誰能主持質量或方法轉移電話；確認哪些批次記錄已在資料室裏是穩定檔案。峰會出行讓實地探訪更難；遠程就緒，是一日回答與一週踉蹌的差別。

用一封給董事會的電郵結束星期日枱頭：附件值班名單、敘事包已凍結、靜默規則已寫明，並點名本週最重要的兩個會議。董事會不需要你的地緣政治論文；他們需要證明公司星期二與星期四聽起來一樣。這種一樣，是 APEC 商務週獎勵的東西。噪音免費；一致性是你今天仍能做完的工作。
