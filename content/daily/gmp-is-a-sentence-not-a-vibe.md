::: en
Slides that say GMP-ready without a site, a partner, or a scope invite a hard follow-up. Be precise. Clinical material made under which quality system. Which steps are still research grade. Which vendor holds the license.

If you are not there yet, say the plan and the date. Precision here is not pessimism. It is how quality claims survive a buyer who has seen soft language before.

::: zh
投影片寫 GMP-ready 卻沒有場地、夥伴或範圍，會引來很難的追問。寫準。臨床物料在哪個質量體系下做。哪些步驟仍是研究級。牌照在哪個供應商。

若還沒到，就說計劃和日期。這裏的精確不是悲觀。而是質量主張如何活過看過軟語言的買家。
