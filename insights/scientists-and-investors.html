Scientists and investors aren't speaking the same language科學家和投資者說的不是同一種語言

Working on both sides — building a science company and helping companies raise capital — I spend a lot of time translating. Here is the dictionary I use.同時站在兩邊——創辦科研公司，又協助企業融資——我花很多時間做「翻譯」。這是我用的字典。

Biotech生物科技US capital markets美國資本市場4 Nov 20262026年11月4日4 min read閱讀約 4 分鐘

I work in two rooms. In one, people talk about particle size, binding affinity and batch consistency. In the other, people talk about runway, valuation and exit. The two rooms want the same thing — a science company that succeeds — but they often misunderstand each other.

A few translations I find myself making again and again.

"Promising results in vitro"

What the scientist means: it worked in a dish, which is an encouraging first step.
What the investor sometimes hears: a product is coming.
The honest translation: one early risk has been reduced. Many more remain, and the next ones are more expensive.

"What's the market size?"

What the investor means: is the prize big enough to justify the risk?
What the scientist sometimes hears: please exaggerate.
The honest translation: show a realistic path to a first market you can actually reach, not the biggest number you can find.

"We need more time"

To a scientist this is just how research works. To an investor, time is money that has already been committed. Both are right. The fix is to stop talking about time and start talking about milestones.

The table I wish every founder brought

The most useful thing I have seen bridge the two rooms is a simple table. Each row is a milestone. For each one: what risk it removes, what it costs, and how long it takes.

Investors can price that. Scientists can commit to it. And when something slips — it always does — everyone can see which risk is still open, rather than arguing about whether the team is "behind".

I think the best science companies are not the ones with the best science alone. They are the ones that learn to speak both languages without losing their honesty in either.

我在兩個房間工作。一個房間裏，大家談粒子大小、結合親和力和批次一致性；另一個房間裏，大家談資金能撐多久、估值和退出。兩個房間想要的其實一樣：一家成功的科研公司。但他們經常誤解對方。

以下是我經常重複做的幾個「翻譯」。

「體外實驗結果很有希望」

科學家的意思：在培養皿裏有效，是令人鼓舞的第一步。
投資者有時聽到的：產品快要出來了。
誠實的翻譯：一個早期風險降低了。後面還有很多風險，而且下一步的成本更高。

「市場有多大？」

投資者的意思：回報夠不夠大，值得承擔這個風險？
科學家有時聽到的：請誇大一點。
誠實的翻譯：展示一條實際可行的路，通往你真正能進入的第一個市場，而不是你能找到的最大數字。

「我們需要多一點時間」

對科學家來說，研究本來就是這樣。對投資者來說，時間就是已經投入的資金。兩者都沒錯。解決方法是不再談時間，而是談里程碑。

我希望每位創辦人都帶來的一張表

我見過最能連接兩個房間的東西，是一張簡單的表。每一行是一個里程碑，列出：它消除甚麼風險、需要多少錢、需要多少時間。

投資者可以為它定價，科學家可以為它承諾。當進度延誤時（一定會延誤），大家都看得到哪個風險仍未解決，而不是爭論團隊是否「落後」。

我認為最好的科研公司，不只是科學做得最好的公司，而是學會說兩種語言、而在兩種語言裏都不失誠實的公司。
