The risk factor you keep rewriting is the real one你不斷重寫的那項風險，才是真的那項

The generic paragraphs are furniture. The one your team argues about for a week is the company.那些通用段落是家具。你們吵了一個星期的那一段，才是這家公司。

US capital markets美國資本市場11 Nov 20262026年11月11日3 min read閱讀約 3 分鐘

Every listing document has risk factors that could belong to almost anyone in the industry. Competition. Key people. Regulation may change. Necessary, and almost nobody remembers them. Then there is the paragraph the team keeps rewriting. I pay more attention to the argument around it than to the final adjectives.

A risk factor that is easy to write is usually borrowed. One that produces four drafts and a tense call is touching something true. A customer who can leave. A permit that is not quite what the slide implied. A step in the technology you do not control yet. Don't sand it until a stranger couldn't tell it was yours. Specific is what makes it useful. It is also what makes it quotable. That trade is real. Take it on purpose.

The weak version says the company may be adversely affected. The useful version says what is true today and what follows if it gets worse. Two customers are most of the revenue, and if either leaves, the forecast you just showed does not survive. A reader can do something with that. "Adverse effects" gives them nothing.

Don't bury the sharp paragraph between twelve dull ones. A careful reader hunts for the proper nouns. Put the real risks where a person can find them, in sentences a founder will say out loud. If you cannot say it out loud, you are not done arguing, and the document is ahead of the company.

The week you spend on that paragraph is information. Why is this sentence so hard? Because the fact is not resolved. Sometimes the right response is not better prose. It is a second customer, a signed assignment, a specification you will stand behind. Then the paragraph gets short. Until the fact moves, expect the draft to keep coming back. That is the document telling the truth early.

每份上市文件都有一些風險因素，幾乎可以屬於行內任何一家。競爭。關鍵的人。法規可能改。必要，而且幾乎沒人記得。然後有一段是團隊一直在改的。我更注意圍繞它的爭吵，多過最後的形容詞。

容易寫的，通常是借來的。引起四稿和一通緊張電話的，碰着了真的事。一個可以走的客戶。一個跟投影片不完全一樣的許可。技術裡你還沒控制的一步。不要把它磨到陌生人看不出是你的。具體才有用。也因此可以被引用。這個交換是真的。有意識地接受。

弱的版本說公司可能受到不利影響。有用的版本講今天甚麼是真的，變差了會跟着發生甚麼。兩個客戶佔了大部分收入，任何一個離開，你剛展示的預測就活不了。讀者拿這個做得到事。「不利影響」他們拿不到。

不要把尖的那一段埋在十二段悶的中間。謹慎的讀者會去找有專有名詞的。真正的風險放在找得到的地方，用創辦人願意大聲講的句子。大聲講不了，你們就還沒吵完，文件已經跑在公司前面。

花在那一段上的一星期是資訊。為甚麼這句這麼難？因為事實還沒解決。有時對的回應不是更好的文字。是第二個客戶、一份簽了的轉讓、一個你願意撐的標準。然後那段會變短。事實沒動之前，預期草稿會再回來。那是文件提前說了真話。

— 鄭迪珉 Tyler Tik Man Cheng
