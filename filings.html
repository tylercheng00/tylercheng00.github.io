1 · How to read listed-company announcements1 · 如何讀上市公司公告

Announcements are the calendar of the company. Financials are the scoreboard. Start with the headline type, then the date, then who signed it.公告是公司的日曆，財報是計分板。先看公告類型，再看日期，再看誰簽署。

Common HKEX types港交所常見類型

Results / interim / annual業績／中期／全年

Numbers land here. Read revenue, gross profit, operating profit, net profit, EPS, and the “outlook” paragraph last — after the numbers.數字在這裏。先讀收入、毛利、經營溢利、淨利、每股盈利，最後才讀「展望」——數字之後再說故事。

Inside information / profit warning內幕消息／盈利警告

Treat as a stop sign. Ask: what changed vs last guidance? Cash or accounting? One-off or run-rate?當作停車標誌。問：相對上次指引變了什麼？現金還是會計？一次性還是常態？

Connected transactions / placings關連交易／配售

Dilution and related-party risk. Note issue price vs last close, use of proceeds, and who is buying.攤薄與關連風險。記發行價對上日收市、資金用途、誰在買。

SEC 6-K / 10-Q / 10-K / 20-FSEC 6-K／10-Q／10-K／20-F

US filers and many ADRs. 10-K / 20-F = annual depth; 10-Q = quarterly; 6-K often carries HK/China news into the US file.美股及多數 ADR。年報深度在 10-K／20-F；季報在 10-Q；6-K 常把港／中消息帶進美國檔案。

A 10-minute announcement ritual十分鐘讀公告儀式

  1. Title + type + time stamp.標題＋類型＋時間戳。
  2. Any number in the first page? Copy it into your notebook with units (HK$m / US$m).首頁有沒有數字？連單位抄進筆記（百萬港元／百萬美元）。
  3. Find “use of proceeds”, “going concern”, “material uncertainty”, or “non-IFRS / non-GAAP”.搜尋「所得款項用途」「持續經營」「重大不確定性」「非國際財務報告準則／非公認會計原則」。
  4. Only then read management’s narrative.然後才讀管理層敘事。

2 · The three financial statements2 · 三大財務報表

One company, three cameras. Balance sheet = photo at a date. Income statement = movie over a period. Cash flow = which scenes actually moved cash.同一間公司，三個鏡頭。資產負債表＝某一日的定格；損益表＝一段時間的電影；現金流量表＝哪些鏡頭真的動了現金。

Rule of thumb: if profit rises but operating cash falls for several periods, dig into receivables, inventory, and one-offs before you celebrate.經驗法則：利潤上升但經營現金連續下滑，先查應收款、存貨與一次性項目，再慶祝。

3 · Balance sheet — how to read it3 · 資產負債表——怎麼看

Assets are what the company controls. Liabilities are what it owes. Equity is the residual claim. Always check units and whether figures are consolidated.資產是公司控制的資源，負債是欠別人的，權益是剩餘權益。先確認單位，以及是否綜合報表。

Lines that matter first先看這些科目

  • Cash & equivalents — runway before you believe the story.現金及等價物——相信故事之前，先看跑道。
  • Trade receivables — growing faster than revenue? Collection risk.應收賬款——增速快過收入？收款風險。
  • Inventory — rising with flat sales can mean products stuck.存貨——銷售持平卻上升，可能貨壓倉。
  • Short-term debt vs cash — refinance pressure in the next 12 months.短期借款對現金——未來十二個月再融資壓力。
  • Goodwill / intangibles — acquisition optimism; check impairment notes.商譽／無形資產——併購樂觀；查減值附註。

Current ratio ≈ current assets ÷ current liabilities. Quick ratio removes inventory. Neither is a scorecard alone — pair with cash flow.流動比率≈流動資產÷流動負債；速動比率剔除存貨。單獨看不夠——要配現金流。

4 · Is the company making money?4 · 公司賺不賺錢？

Start with revenue quality, then margins, then whether profit is cash-backed.先看收入質量，再看利潤率，再看利潤有沒有現金撐腰。

Gross margin毛利率

(Revenue − COGS) ÷ Revenue. Falling margin with rising sales can still destroy value.（收入−銷售成本）÷收入。銷售上升但毛利率下滑，仍可能在毀價值。

Operating margin經營利潤率

After R&D, sales & marketing, G&A. Shows the machine before interest and tax games.扣研發、銷售及市場推廣、行政開支後。看利息與稅項「遊戲」之前的機器。

Net profit vs adjusted profit淨利 vs 經調整溢利

Non-IFRS / non-GAAP adds back SBC, impairments, “one-offs”. Read the bridge table every time.非準則溢利常加回股權激勵、減值、「一次性」。每次都要讀調節表。

EPS & share count每股盈利與股本

Profit up but diluted EPS flat? Check placings, convertibles, and option overhang.溢利升但攤薄每股盈利不動？查配售、可換股與期權潛在攤薄。

5 · Cash flow statement5 · 現金流量表

Operating cash flow answers “did the business generate cash?” Investing answers “what did they buy or sell?” Financing answers “debt, equity, dividends, buybacks.”經營現金流回答「生意有沒有產生現金？」投資回答「買了／賣了什麼？」融資回答「借貸、股本、股息、回購」。

  • OCF > 0 and rising with profit — healthier story.經營現金流為正且隨溢利上升——故事較健康。
  • Capex — growth vs maintenance. Heavy growth capex can be fine if returns show up later; say so with dates.資本開支——增長還是維修。高增長資本開支可以成立，但要用日期說清回報何時出現。
  • Free cash flow ≈ OCF − capex (simplified). Still read footnotes for leases and working capital.自由現金流≈經營現金流−資本開支（簡化）。仍要讀租賃與營運資金附註。

6 · Candlestick / K-line anatomy6 · K線圖結構

Each candle is one period (day, week, hour). Body = open→close. Wicks = high/low. Colour conventions vary; here green = close ≥ open, red = close < open.每根 K 線是一個時段（日／週／小時）。實體＝開→收；影線＝最高／最低。顏色慣例不一；此處綠＝收≥開，紅＝收<開。

  • Long lower wick — sellers pushed down, buyers recovered into the close.長下影——賣盤壓低後，買盤在收市前回補。
  • Long upper wick — rally failed into the close.長上影——冲高後收市回落。
  • Doji — open≈close; indecision, not a signal alone.十字星——開≈收；猶豫，單獨不是訊號。

7 · Chart toolkit (MA, volume, levels)7 · 看圖工具箱（均線、成交量、位置）

  • Moving averages (MA20 / MA50 / MA200) — context, not magic. Price far above MA200 after a vertical run is often crowded; far below after panic can be oversold — still not a trade ticket.移動平均線（20／50／200）——是語境不是魔法。價格遠高於 MA200 的直綫拉升常擁擠；恐慌後遠低於可能超賣——仍不是買賣票。
  • Volume — breakouts on thin volume are weaker than the same move on heavy volume.成交量——縮量突破弱於放量同幅走勢。
  • Support / resistance — prior swing highs/lows and round numbers. Mark them before you narrate.支持／阻力——前高前低與整數位。先標位置，再講故事。

8 · Case study · Alibaba (BABA) — filings meet the chart8 · 個案 · 阿里巴巴（BABA）——公告遇上圖表

Practice path: open the latest 20-F / 6-K or HK filings, write three numbers (revenue, operating margin, cash), then look at the live chart below. Ask whether the price move matches the filing surprise or a sector tape.練習路徑：打開最新 20-F／6-K 或港股公告，寫下三個數字（收入、經營利潤率、現金），再看下方真實圖表。問：價格波動是對齊公告驚喜，還是板塊行情？

  1. Find cloud / commerce split if disclosed — mix shift matters more than one headline.若有披露，分開雲與電商——結構變化比一個標題重要。
  2. Check buyback / dividend language vs capex and investment losses.對照回購／派息用語與資本開支、投資虧損。
  3. On the chart: mark the earnings date mentally, then see if the candle that day had unusual volume.在圖上心里標業績日，看當日 K 線成交量是否異常。

Live chart by TradingView. Delayed data possible. Education only — not a recommendation on BABA.真實圖表由 TradingView 提供，或有延遲。僅供教育——不是對阿里巴巴的推薦。

9 · Case study · NVIDIA (NVDA) — margins, cycle, K-lines9 · 個案 · 英偉達（NVDA）——利潤率、周期與 K 線

NVDA filings often hinge on data-centre revenue, gross margin, and supply commentary. Pair the 10-K / 10-Q language with the live chart: vertical runs, consolidations, and volume spikes around results.英偉達公告常圍繞數據中心收入、毛利率與供應評論。把 10-K／10-Q 用語對上真實圖表：直綫拉升、整固、業績附近的放量。

  • Track gross margin vs management guidance — small misses move this name hard.追蹤毛利率對管理層指引——輕微不及預期也會大幅波動。
  • Read customer concentration and export-control risk factors before you narrate “AI forever”.講「人工智能永遠」之前，先讀客戶集中與出口管制風險因素。
  • On K-lines: after a gap-up on earnings, study the next five sessions’ wicks — follow-through or give-back?業績跳空後，看隨後五個交易日影線——是延續還是回吐？

Live chart by TradingView. Education only — not a recommendation on NVDA.真實圖表由 TradingView 提供。僅供教育——不是對英偉達的推薦。

10 · Field checklist before you claim you “get” a company10 · 說「我看懂這間公司」之前的實戰清單

  1. Latest annual + latest interim / quarter opened; units noted.已打開最新年報＋最新中期／季度；單位已註明。
  2. Cash, short-term debt, receivables, inventory copied with dates.現金、短期債、應收、存貨已連日期抄下。
  3. Gross / operating / net margins for three periods.三個期間的毛／經營／淨利率。
  4. Operating cash vs net profit direction.經營現金與淨利方向是否一致。
  5. One announcement risk (dilution, lawsuit, going concern, related party).一項公告風險（攤薄、訴訟、持續經營、關連方）。
  6. Chart: timeframe named, one support, one resistance, volume note.圖表：時框已寫、一個支持、一個阻力、成交量一句。
  7. Still unknown: write it. Unknowns are part of diligence.仍不懂的寫下來。未知是盡調的一部分。
