Weekend coverage on 22 November 2026 kept returning to the same cluster: Huang’s personal scandal, suspected intelligence targeting, and companies such as Megaspeed that sat close to Nvidia chip flows under export-control scrutiny. Private founders who never touched those entities still inherit the question style. Who are your resellers. Who is the end customer. What do you refuse. How do you know.

Write a channel map that a lawyer can defend. Onboarding checklist. Denied-party screening cadence. Contract audit rights. Examples of refused orders with dates. If you have never refused an order, explain the controls that would trigger a refusal. “We trust our partners” is not a control. It is a toast.

Gray paths through third countries will be assumed hotter after this news cycle. If any historical revenue touched ambiguous routing, isolate it, disclose it under counsel, and show the cutoff date. Trying to hide a small gray slice is how small slices become term-sheet cancers. Early daylight is cheaper.

Product and finance should share one vocabulary. Sales cannot promise flexibility that finance cannot recognize and that counsel cannot defend. Align the three before the next customer call. Huang-week and probe-week together made misalignment look intentional.

Put the channel map in the data room index on a single line so partners see you expected the question. Expectation is a diligence advantage. Surprise is a diligence tax. Pay fewer taxes this month.

Keep the day hook in the first paragraph and the operating proof in the rest. Investors forgive topical openings; they do not forgive empty middles. Add dates, owners, and artefacts that can survive a partner forward. If a control does not yet exist, name the date it will. If a risk cannot be closed this week, name the mitigation already funded in cash runway weeks. Write as if the next associate will paste your note into an investment committee chat without you in the room.

寫一張律師辯護得了的渠道圖：准入清單、拒絕往來名單掃描節奏、合約查核權、附日期的拒單例子。若從未拒單，說明什麼控制會觸發拒絕。「我們信任夥伴」不是控制，是敬酒詞。

經第三國的灰色路徑，在這輪新聞後會被預設更熱。若歷史營收碰過含糊轉運，就隔離、在律師指导下揭露，並出示切斷日期。想藏住一小片灰色，會讓小片變成條款清單癌變；早點見光比較便宜。

產品與財務應共用詞彙。銷售不能承諾財務確認不了、律師辯護不了的彈性。下一次客戶電話前先對齊三者。黃仁勳週與調查週加在一起，會讓不對齊看起來像故意。
