::: en
Founders like to open with the valuation they have in mind. I get it. It is also the wrong first sentence. The person across the table is usually trying to work out something plainer: who owns this company, and who is allowed to sign for it.

Until that is clear, the price is attached to an object they can't describe yet.

If you are a China-based company talking to a US investor, they are looking at three places at once. Where the work happens. Where the contracts sit. Where the shares they would buy sit. If those are three different places, draw it on one page, with arrows, in the first meeting. A messy chart that is true is better than a simple sentence that is vague. By the third meeting they have either built the wrong picture or decided it is not worth building.

Owning it and being allowed to sign are not the same thing. If you need a parent, a board that has not met, or a shareholder overseas who does not answer email, that is a fact about the round. I ask a blunt version early. If we agreed on Friday, who still has to say yes before Monday, and do they already know? That answer is the timetable.

A Cayman holding company does not answer the question. It moves it. You should be able to say, without the lawyer in the room, what that company owns and through which agreements. The lawyer's job is to make the chain true. Yours is to know it.

Every structure has one awkward bit. A nominee. A relative on the register. An option pool that was promised in a chat and never written down. Say it yourself, in one sentence, and say what you have done about it. People are rarely shocked by the structure. They are shocked when the second version doesn't match the first.

創辦人喜歡一開口就講自己心裡的估值。我明白。但那通常是錯的第一句。對面的人多半在想更普通的事：這家公司是誰的，誰可以替它簽字。

這件事沒講清楚，價格就是貼在一件他們還描述不了的東西上。

中國公司跟美國投資者談，對方同時在看三個地方。事情在哪裡做。合約在哪裡。他們要買的股份在哪裡。如果是三個地方，第一次見面就用一頁紙畫出來，加箭嘴。一張很難看但是真的圖，好過一句很簡單但很含糊的話。拖到第三次，他們要麼畫錯了，要麼覺得不值得畫。

擁有，和可以簽字，不是同一件事。要父母點頭、董事會還沒開過、或者海外那個股東不回郵件，這就是這一輪的事實。我會早點問得很直接：星期五談妥的話，星期一之前還有誰要說好，他們知不知道？這個答案就是時間表。

開曼那家控股公司沒有回答問題，只是把問題挪走了。律師不在房間裡，你也應該講得出它擁有甚麼、靠哪些協議。律師負責讓那條鏈是真的。你負責知道它。

每個架構都有一處講起來彆扭。代持。名冊上的親戚。微信裡答應過、文件裡沒有的購股權。你自己先用一句話說出來，並講你做了甚麼。架構本身很少嚇到人。嚇到人的是第二個版本跟第一個對不上。
