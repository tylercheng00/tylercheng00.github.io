After Huang’s return, name your circular deals before the room does黃仁勳復職之後：先把「循環交易」的名字寫清楚

Markets cheered Nvidia’s CEO return and the AI “flywheel” narrative — diligence will still ask who funds whom, on what terms, and what breaks if the hub person steps away again.市場為輝達執行長回歸與人工智慧「飛輪」歡呼；盡調仍會問誰資助誰、條件為何、中樞人物再缺席時什麼會斷。

US capital markets美國資本市場24 Nov 20262026年11月24日7 min read閱讀約 7 分鐘

The story markets told on 23–24 November was simple: Jensen Huang is back, therefore the AI financing flywheel can keep spinning. Nvidia’s share price ripped higher; Asian tech benchmarks followed, with Taiwan’s cash market posting a historic point gain on the twenty-fourth. Supply-chain executives told local reporters that orders into 2027 still look solid, while also warning that “handshake” arrangements and circular financing will face tighter scrutiny, not looser. That second sentence is the one that belongs in your fundraising file. Celebration without naming the circular edges is how diligence surprises get born.

Circular financing — chips sold into customers who are also equity or credit counterparties, capacity prepaid by the same ecosystem that books the revenue — can be rational industrial policy for a company at Nvidia’s scale. It is radioactive for a private company that cannot show the graph. If your round depends on a strategic that also buys your product, or on a customer that also sits on your cap table, write the map before the first partner meeting. Who pays whom. Which contracts are take-or-pay. Which investments are marked, guaranteed, or contingent. Which revenues would disappear if the strategic paused for governance reasons the way Nvidia’s ecosystem briefly feared when Huang stepped down.

Huang’s eleven-day absence was a stress test of key-person gravity inside that flywheel. Public markets priced the fear, then the relief. Private processes should keep the intermediate lesson: if one executive’s calendar is load-bearing for your offtake, credit support, or introduction path, you do not have a diversified commercial story. You have a concentration story wearing a growth costume. After this week, expect investors to ask for a one-page “hub risk” note — not to insult your partners, but to see whether you have already thought about substitution. Name secondary buyers, alternate lenders, and the date you last refreshed those conversations.

Taiwan and U.S. reporting also stressed that export-control and compliance rails are unlikely to loosen because the CEO returned. Nvidia is adding board-level export oversight and a chief compliance officer search while investigations continue. If your model assumes gray-channel flexibility into restricted geographies, rewrite it. Circular deals that rely on informal routing will be the first items counsel redlines. Prefer contracts with audit rights, know-your-customer language that matches your actual resellers, and board minutes that show you reviewed concentration and end-use risk in the last two quarters.

Use the Huang headlines as a forcing function, not as a mood. Update the related-party schedule. Update the customer concentration table with names you are allowed to show under NDA. Add a footnote on any prepaid capacity or reciprocal investment. If nothing circular exists, say so in one plain sentence so the room stops hunting ghosts. If something circular does exist, bring the primary documents to the second meeting instead of another logo slide. The tape can call the flywheel “back.” Your job is to show whether your company is a spoke with documentation — or a story that only works when one hub person is smiling on stage.

Document the graph in a format counsel can reuse in the S-1 or data-room index later. Circles that feel clever in a seed deck become footnotes that slow a listing. Huang-week is a cheap time to clean the graph because everyone is already talking about the flywheel’s hub. Use the attention. Do not wait for your own outage to force the cleanup under a compressed diligence clock.

11月23日至24日，市場講的故事很簡單：黃仁勳回來了，人工智慧的融資飛輪就能繼續轉。輝達股價暴衝，亞洲科技股跟漲，台股現金市場在24日寫下史上罕見的點數狂飆。供應鏈主管對媒體說，看到2027年的訂單仍在，卻也同時警告：「握手」安排與循環融資只會被盯得更緊，不會更鬆。第二句才該進你的融資檔案。只慶祝、不把循環邊緣寫出名字，盡調的意外就會這樣長出來。

循環融資——把晶片賣給同時是股權或信貸對手的客戶，又由同一生態系預付產能、再把營收記回自己——對輝達這種量級，可以是合理的產業佈局；對講不清關係圖的私人公司，卻像放射性物質。若你的這輪融資依賴「既是客戶又是戰略投資人」的對象，或依賴同時出現在股權表上的大客戶，請在第一次合夥人會議前畫好地圖：誰付給誰、哪些合約是照付不議、哪些投資是計價、擔保還是或有、若戰略方因治理原因暫停（就像市場短暫擔心黃仁勳缺席時那樣），哪些營收會消失。

黃仁勳缺席的十一天，是對飛輪裡「關鍵人重力」的壓力測試。公開市場先定價恐懼，再定價鬆一口氣。私人流程應留下中間那課：若單一高管的行程，承載你的包銷、信用支持或引薦路徑，你就沒有分散的商業故事，只有穿著成長外衣的集中度故事。這一週之後，預期投資人會要一頁「中樞風險」說明——不是要羞辱你的夥伴，而是看你有沒有想過替代方案。寫出次要買家、備援資金方，以及你上次更新這些對話的日期。

台美報導也強調：執行長回來，不代表出口管制與法遵會鬆綁。輝達一邊讓調查繼續，一邊加董事會層級的出口監督，並尋找法遵長。若你的模型還假設能靠灰色渠道進入受限地區，現在就改寫。依賴非正式轉運的循環交易，會是律師最先畫紅線的項目。寧可要有查核權的合約、與真實經銷商一致的客戶盡職語言，以及近兩季董事會已審視集中度與最終用途風險的會議紀錄。

把黃仁勳頭條當成逼你交作業的鬧鐘，而不是情緒板。更新關聯交易表；在保密協議允許下，更新有名字的客戶集中度；凡有預付產能或互惠投資，加註腳。若根本沒有循環結構，就用一句白話寫清楚，讓房間別再找鬼；若有，第二次見面請帶主合約，而不是又一頁標誌牆。行情可以宣布飛輪「回來了」；你的工作是證明：自己的公司是有文件的輪輻——還是只在中樞人物站上舞台微笑時才成立的故事。

把關係圖做成律師之後能直接進S-1或資料室索引的格式。種子輪簡報裡顯得聰明的圓圈，到了上市階段往往變成拖慢進度的腳註。黃仁勳這一週是便宜的清理窗口，因為所有人都在談論飛輪的中樞。趁注意力還在，動手；別等自己停機，才在壓縮的盡調時鐘下被迫清理。
