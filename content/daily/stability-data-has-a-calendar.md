::: en
Stability is often on the critical path and off the fundraising slide. Real-time data takes real time. Accelerated conditions help, within rules, but they do not invent a year you did not run.

When someone asks when the next financing milestone lands, include the stability clock if it gates release or partner confidence. Cutting the study to hit a roadshow date is how you meet a partner with a hole in the file.

Put the end date of the current pull on the internal calendar next to the raise. If they conflict, change one of them early, in daylight.

::: zh
穩定性常在關鍵路徑上，卻不在融資投影片上。即時數據需要真實時間。加速條件在規則內有用，但變不出你沒跑過的一年。

有人問下一個融資里程碑何時落地時，若放行或夥伴信心被穩定性卡住，就把這座時鐘算進去。為了趕上路演去砍研究，是帶著檔案缺口去見夥伴。

把當前拉取的結束日和融資放在同一張內部日曆。若衝突，趁還在白天早改其中一個。
