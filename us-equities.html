    02 — Choosing a route選擇路線

    Routes to the US market進入美國市場的途徑

    A

    Traditional IPO傳統 IPO

    New shares sold through underwriters after SEC registration; price set by book-building.完成 SEC 註冊後，經承銷商發行新股，以簿記建檔定價。

    • Raises new capital; established process可募集新資金；流程成熟
    • Longest preparation; full underwriting cost準備時間最長；承銷成本較高

    B

    Direct listing直接上市

    Existing shares begin trading without a traditional underwritten offering.現有股份直接掛牌交易，不經傳統承銷發行。

    • Lower dilution; no lock-up by default攤薄較少；預設無禁售期
    • Suits well-known companies; less price support較適合知名企業；股價支持較少

    C

    SPAC mergerSPAC 合併

    The company merges with a listed special-purpose acquisition company and becomes public through the merger.企業與已上市的特殊目的收購公司合併，從而成為上市公司。

    • Negotiated valuation; potentially faster估值可協商；可能較快
    • Redemption risk; tighter regulatory scrutiny有贖回風險；監管審查趨嚴

    D

    M&A / reverse merger併購／反向合併

    Acquiring, merging with, or combining into an existing US-listed company.收購、合併或併入已在美國上市的公司。

    • Strategic fit; can combine growth and listing具策略協同；可結合增長與上市
    • Diligence-heavy; integration risk盡職審查繁重；有整合風險

    Exchange listing standards (financial, liquidity and governance tests) differ by venue and tier and change over time; they are assessed case by case.各交易所及板塊的上市標準（財務、流通量及管治要求）各有不同，亦會不時更新，須按個別情況評估。

      Company企業
      A Nasdaq-listed, China-based new-energy company.一家納斯達克上市的中資新能源企業。
      Structure結構
      Oct 2025: placement agreement with a non-US institutional investor (up to USD 30M), with a first tranche settled in 100 bitcoin. 12 Feb 2026: strategic financing agreement for USD 40M. Each round priced in advance per share; investors subscribed with digital assets; every step publicly announced.2025 年 10 月與非美國機構投資者簽訂私募協議（上限 3,000 萬美元），首筆以 100 枚比特幣結算。2026 年 2 月 12 日簽署 4,000 萬美元策略融資協議。每輪事先約定每股價格，投資者以數字資產認購，每一步均公告披露。
      Outcome結果
      13 Feb 2026: USD 60M completed the next day, 50% above plan. Talks on a larger expansion were announced afterwards and had not been completed.2026 年 2 月 13 日（翌日）完成 6,000 萬美元，較原計劃多 50%。其後公告洽談進一步擴大規模，尚未完成。

      NasdaqAuto aftermarket services汽車後市場服務Fixed-price placement定價私募2026

      A one-step, fixed-price placement with clear terms and predictable dilution一次過定價的私募：條款清楚、攤薄可預計

      ~USD 30Msubscription agreement私募認購協議
      USD 0.60fixed price per share每股固定價
      Company企業
      A Nasdaq-listed, China-based automotive aftermarket services company.一家納斯達克上市的中資汽車後市場服務企業。
      Structure結構
      50 million Class A ordinary shares at a fixed USD 0.60. Price and share count were fixed at signing, so a falling share price cannot trigger more shares. The company could accept cash or digital assets as payment.發行 5,000 萬股 A 類普通股，每股固定 0.60 美元。價格和股數在簽約時已定，不會因股價下跌而增發更多股份；認購款可收現金或數字資產，由公司決定。
      Outcome結果
      Agreement signed on 22 Jun 2026; the placement has since closed.2026 年 6 月 22 日簽署認購協議，其後已完成交割。
