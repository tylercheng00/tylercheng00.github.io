::: en
Founders sometimes ask counsel to quickly check a slide that has already been shown. That is backwards. The risky sentences — China exposure, IP ownership, related-party deals — should meet counsel before they meet a room of buyers.

Budget time for redlines. A clean deck that counsel has not seen is not clean. It is untested. If counsel asks for a weaker claim, that is often the claim you can defend in diligence. Strong marketing language that dies under a document request costs more than a dull accurate line.

::: zh
創辦人有時請律師快速看一下已經展示過的投影片。這是反的。危險句子——中國風險、知識產權權屬、關聯交易——應在見買家之前先見律師。

為紅字修改留時間。律師沒看過的乾淨簡報並不乾淨，只是未測試。若律師要求你把主張寫弱一點，那往往才是盡職審查裏守得住的說法。在文件要求下站不住的華麗行銷，代價高過一句無聊但準確的話。
