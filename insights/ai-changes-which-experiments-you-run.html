Insights觀點 · Biotech生物科技

AI won't replace the lab. It changes which experiments you run.AI 不會取代實驗室，但會改變你做哪些實驗

In biotech, AI is most useful as a filter. It narrows the search; the lab still decides.在生物科技裏，AI 最有用的角色是過濾器。它縮窄搜尋範圍，最後仍由實驗室決定。

Biotech生物科技4 Nov 20262026年11月4日3 min read閱讀約 3 分鐘

Every biotech pitch I see now has "AI" somewhere in it. Some of it is real. Some of it is a chart with a neural network icon on it.

Here is how I think about it.

AI is a filter, not an oracle

A nano-carrier can be designed in an enormous number of ways: the material, the size, the surface, the ligand, the payload. Nobody can test them all. Where AI genuinely helps is in narrowing that space — using existing data to predict which combinations are worth making, and which are probably not.

That does not replace experiments. It changes which experiments you run. Instead of testing a hundred designs to find three good ones, you might test twenty and find the same three. For a small team, that difference is the difference between possible and impossible.

The real bottleneck moves to data

Models are only as good as what they learn from. Biological data is noisy, often small, and frequently inconsistent between labs. A model trained on messy data gives confident, messy answers.

So I think the competitive advantage is shifting. It is less about having the cleverest model — those are increasingly available — and more about owning clean, well-labelled data from your own experiments. A modest model on excellent data beats an excellent model on poor data.

Be careful with "AI-discovered"

When a press release says a therapy was "discovered by AI", I want to know what the AI actually did. Did it propose a candidate that then went through the usual years of testing? That is useful and honest. Did it replace the testing? Then I would be worried.

Our working rule

AI narrows the search; the lab decides. It is a simple rule, but it keeps us honest about where the evidence comes from.

現在我看到的每一份生物科技簡報，總會有「AI」的字眼。有些是真材實料，有些只是一張配上神經網絡圖示的圖表。

我是這樣看的。

AI 是過濾器，不是神諭

一個納米載體可以有極多種設計：材料、尺寸、表面、配體、藥物。沒有人能全部測試。AI 真正有幫助的地方，是縮窄這個範圍：用已有的數據預測哪些組合值得做，哪些大概不值得。

這並不取代實驗，而是改變你做哪些實驗。原本要測試一百個設計才找到三個好的，現在可能測試二十個就找到同樣那三個。對小團隊來說，這個差別就是「做得到」與「做不到」的差別。

真正的瓶頸轉移到數據

模型的好壞取決於它學習的內容。生物數據噪音多、樣本往往很少，而且不同實驗室之間經常不一致。用凌亂的數據訓練出來的模型，會很有信心地給你凌亂的答案。

所以我認為競爭優勢正在轉移。重點不再是擁有最聰明的模型，因為這些模型愈來愈容易取得；而是擁有來自自己實驗、乾淨而且標註清楚的數據。普通的模型配上優質數據，勝過優秀的模型配上劣質數據。

對「由 AI 發現」要小心

當新聞稿說某療法「由 AI 發現」，我會想知道 AI 實際做了甚麼。它是否提出一個候選方案，然後經歷一般的多年測試？這既有用又誠實。它是否取代了測試？那我會擔心。

我們的工作原則

AI 縮窄搜尋範圍，實驗室作最後決定。原則很簡單，但它令我們時刻清楚證據從何而來。
