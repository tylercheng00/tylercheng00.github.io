::: en
People who offer introductions are busy. Give them four lines they can paste: who you help, the one sentence of proof, the link, and the ask. No autobiography. No five links.

A forwardable blurb respects their reputation. It also forces you to choose one story. If you cannot write it, you are not ready for the introduction you want.

::: zh
願意引薦的人很忙。給他們四行可直接貼上的話：你幫誰、一句證明、連結、請求。不要自傳。不要五個連結。

轉發得了的介紹尊重他們的名聲。也逼你選一個故事。若你寫不出來，你還沒準備好你想要的那次引薦。
