Lab notebooks are financing documents實驗筆記本為何也是融資文件

APEC week spotlights China tech ambition; US politics spotlights control. For biotech raisers, dated lab records are how ownership and method survive both.芯片管制佔新聞時，有日期的實驗記錄，仍是權屬與方法的證據。

Biotech生物科技17 Nov 20262026年11月17日7 min read閱讀約 7 分鐘

Scientists often treat notebooks as private memory. Investors and partners treat them as evidence. That gap widens this week: APEC business leaders gather in Shenzhen amid Asia-Pacific trade and investment talk about China’s economic rise and Greater Bay Area capacity, while US headlines put Jensen Huang, Nvidia, and AI chip sales into China under bipartisan pressure to tighten controls. The politics are about semiconductors. The diligence habit they reinforce — who owns what, who did what, where know-how sits — lands on every China-linked technical company seeking US capital, including biotech.

Who ran the assay. Which lot of reagent. Whether the protocol matched the one in the deck. Which entity employed the operator. Gaps here become ownership fights and reproducibility fights later. In a news cycle primed for technology-transfer anxiety, a missing dated entry is not a lab inconvenience. It is a story a sceptical partner can tell about control of the method. You do not have to like that leap. You do have to close the gap that makes the leap easy.

You do not need a perfect electronic system on day one. You need dated entries, named operators, and a way to find the run behind a chart. When a diligence team asks whether they can see the raw, the answer should be a path, not a scramble. Put notebook hygiene on the same list as assignments and patents. It is less glamorous than a Shenzhen keynote about innovation. It is often what lets the glamorous slide survive a second look from a US fund that just read about export controls over breakfast.

Build a minimum viable record pack for financing. One index of studies that support the deck’s claims, with notebook IDs or file paths. One chain-of-custody note for critical materials. One statement of which legal entity owns the IP and employs the people who generated it — mainland opco, Hong Kong holdco, US subsidiary, or a mix. Summit-week narratives about regional tech rise do not substitute for that statement. Chip-politics narratives about leakage do not invent ownership either. Paper does.

Capital implications are concrete for China-linked founders. US readers will ask whether know-how can move in ways the equity story does not describe. Lab notebooks, ELN exports, and signed invention assignments are how you answer without a speech. If work happened across labs in the Bay Area of the Pearl River and a US CRO, say which protocol version each site used and where the primary record lives. Cross-border science is normal. Undocumented cross-border science looks like the control problem Washington is arguing about, even when your field is far from GPUs.

Watch the chart-to-record link. Every figure in the data room should point to a run. Every run should name an operator and a date. Every operator should sit on a payroll or contractor agreement that matches the entity story in the China-exposure page. Broken links are where financing stalls: not because the science failed, but because the story of the science cannot be audited. Auditors and investors become stricter when the macro conversation is about who may transfer advanced capability where. Strictness shows up as document requests, not as compliments for your poster.

Train the team before the roadshow, not during it. A one-hour session on what “financing-grade” means: no backdated pages, no unnamed “we,” no chart orphaned from its raw file, no protocol drift between the slide and the bench. Founders sometimes postpone this because APEC week feels like a time for meetings, not for binders. Meetings without binders become meetings that request binders urgently after someone remembers the Nvidia hearing. Urgency is more expensive than an afternoon of indexing.

Keep the tone practical. No claim that today’s chip vote predicts your assay outcome. No invented diligence war stories. Just the rule that holds in quiet labs and loud news weeks: if the record cannot show who did what, when, the data room will stall on ownership and method. Put notebook hygiene beside patents on the closing checklist. When Shenzhen hosts leaders and Washington argues about AI chips, the companies that still look financeable are the ones whose science can be found on a path, not reconstructed from memory.

科學家常把筆記本當成私人備忘；投資人和商業夥伴卻當它是證據。本週這個落差更大：APEC 工商領袖在深圳聚集，亞太貿易與投資話題在談中國經濟崛起與大灣區能力；美國標題則把黃仁勳、英偉達與對華人工智能芯片銷售，放在兩黨收緊管制的壓力下。政治談的是半導體；它帶起的盡職習慣——誰擁有甚麼、誰做了甚麼、技術訣竅在哪——會落到每一家尋求美國資本、又與中國有關的技術公司，包括生技。

誰跑了檢測？哪一批試劑？規程是否和投影片一致？操作者受僱於哪個主體？缺口後來會變成權屬之爭和可重現性之爭。在已被調校出「技術轉移焦慮」的新聞周期裏，缺一條有日期的記錄，不是實驗室不便——它是懷疑的合夥人可以講的、關於方法控制權的故事。你不必喜歡那個跳躍；你必須補上讓跳躍變容易的缺口。

第一天不需要完美的電子系統。你需要有日期的記錄、具名的操作者，以及找到圖表背後那次實驗的方法。盡職調查問「能否看原始數據」時，答案應是一條路徑，不是一場翻箱。把筆記本紀律和發明轉讓、專利放在同一清單。它比深圳關於創新的主題演講少華麗，卻常常讓華麗的那頁，能經得起剛在早餐讀完出口管制的美國基金第二次看。

為融資做一份「最低可行」記錄包：一份支撐簡報主張的研究索引，附筆記本編號或檔案路徑；一份關鍵物料的監管鏈說明；一份聲明——知識產權歸哪個法人、產生數據的人受僱於誰（內地營運公司、香港控股、美國子公司，或混合）。峰會週關於區域科技崛起的敘事，替代不了那份聲明；芯片政治關於外洩的敘事，也發明不了權屬。紙可以。

對與中國有關的創辦人，含義很具體。美國讀者會問：技術訣竅會否以股權故事未描述的方式移動。實驗筆記本、電子實驗記錄匯出、已簽署的發明轉讓，是你不用演講就能回答的方式。若工作橫跨珠三角灣區實驗室與美國合約研究機構，說每個場地用哪個規程版本、主記錄在哪。跨境科學很平常；沒有記錄的跨境科學，看起來像華盛頓在爭論的管制問題——即使你的領域離圖形處理器很遠。

留意圖表到記錄的連結。資料室每張圖都應指向一次實驗；每次實驗應具名操作者與日期；每位操作者應落在與「中國風險敞口」頁主體故事一致的薪資或承包協議上。斷連結是融資卡住的地方：不是因為科學失敗，而是因為科學的故事無法被審計。當宏觀對話關乎先進能力可在何處轉移時，審計者與投資人會更嚴——嚴格表現為文件要求，不是對海報的稱讚。

在路演之前訓練團隊，不要在路演之中。一小時講清楚「融資級」是甚麼：沒有倒填日期的頁、沒有無名的「我們」、沒有與原始檔案失散的圖、投影片與實驗台之間沒有規程漂移。創辦人有時推遲，因為 APEC 週感覺像開會的時間，不是整理活頁夾的時間。沒有活頁夾的會議，會變成有人想起英偉達聽證會之後、緊急索要活頁夾的會議。緊急，比一個下午的索引昂貴。

語氣保持實務：不宣稱今天的芯片投票能預測你的檢測結果，不編造盡職戰爭故事。只堅持安靜實驗室與嘈雜新聞週都成立的規則——紀錄若說不出誰在何時做了甚麼，資料室會卡在權屬與方法上。把筆記本紀律和專利一起放進交割清單。當深圳接待領袖、華盛頓爭論人工智能芯片時，看起來仍融得到資的公司，是科學能在路徑上找到、而不是靠記憶重建的公司。
