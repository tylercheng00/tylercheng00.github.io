::: en
Founders fear hearing no. A maybe that lasts three months is worse. Ask what would have to change for a yes. If nothing would, thank them and move the name out of the active column.

Keep a short note of why. Patterns show up: stage, geography, sector, check size. That list improves the next outreach more than another polite follow-up into silence.

::: zh
創辦人害怕聽到 no。拖三個月的 maybe 更糟。問：要變成 yes 需要改甚麼。若沒有，道謝，把名字移出進行中那欄。

短記原因。模式會出現：階段、地域、賽道、支票大小。那份清單對下一次接觸的幫助，大過又一封沒有回音的客氣跟進。
