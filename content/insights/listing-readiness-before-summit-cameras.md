::: en
Listing readiness is a stack of boring pages that must be true at the same time. Cap table that matches the data room. Financials that match the audit trail. China-operating description that matches how cash and IP actually move. Related-party schedule that a stranger can read. Use of proceeds in weeks. When APEC anticipation rises toward Shenzhen and China ADRs have just printed a soft week on public delayed data, those pages get read faster and with less patience. Cameras do not create the gaps. They reduce the time you have to explain them.

Build a readiness checklist that a US banker can score in fifteen minutes. Entity chart with ownership percentages. Board minutes for the last four meetings. Counsel memo on foreign private issuer status if relevant. Export and data notes if any product touches regulated tech or cross-border datasets. Customer concentration table. Litigation and IP assignment index. None of this is glamorous. All of it is what “we are listing-ready” means when geopolitics is the ambient noise. A growth slide about China’s rise does not substitute for the checklist. Growth and readiness answer different questions.

Sequence the work against the summit calendar, not against hope. If leaders week will consume executive attention, finish the checklist items that require the CEO and CFO signatures before that week. Leave during-summit bandwidth for live diligence questions, not for hunting missing exhibits. Soft China ADR closes from 9–13 November 2026 are a reminder that public comps can stay awkward even when the S&P is firm. Awkward comps make bankers more pedantic about file quality. Pedantry is survivable if the file is complete. It is fatal if the file is a pile of good intentions.

Name the US–China issues you will not debate in a kickoff call. You will describe structure. You will describe permissions. You will describe what happens if a licence is delayed. You will not litigate foreign policy. Founders who arrive ready to argue politics often leave having skipped the structure. Structure is what underwriters can put in a document. Politics is what the news already supplies for free this month. Give the room the document.

For companies considering both US and Hong Kong paths, write the dual-track rules now. Which disclosures differ. Which financial standards. Which governance cleanups must happen either way. Dual-track without rules becomes dual-confusion under summit coverage, when every outsider assumes your timing is political. Your timing should be gated by file readiness and cash runway. State those gates to the board in writing before Monday.

Biotech issuers should add CMC and clinical site appendices to the readiness pack. Method transfer status, stability calendar, site contracts, and who can host a remote quality call during a travel-heavy week. Listing processes stall on manufacturing sentences as often as on market windows. Summit week only makes the stall more visible because everyone is already looking at China for other reasons.

Close the loop with a single owner. Someone — usually finance or counsel coordinated by the CEO — marks each checklist line green, amber, or red by a date before APEC noise peaks. Amber needs a named fix. Red needs a decision: slip the process, bridge, or cut scope. Readiness is not a vibe you announce after watching Nvidia and China ADRs. Readiness is a scored list. Score it while anticipation is still cheaper than crisis.

上市就緒是一疊必須同時為真的無聊頁面。與資料室相符的股權表。與審計軌跡相符的財務。與現金和知識產權實際流向相符的中國營運描述。陌生人讀得懂的關聯方表。按星期寫的資金用途。當對 APEC 的預期移向深圳，而中概股剛在延遲公開數據上印出偏軟一週，這些頁會被讀得更快、耐心更少。鏡頭不會製造缺口，只會縮短你解釋缺口的時間。

做一份美國銀行家十五分鐘能打分的就緒清單。帶持股比例的主體圖。最近四次董事會紀錄。如適用，關於外國私人發行人地位的律師備忘。產品若涉及受規管科技或跨境數據集，附出口與數據說明。客戶集中度表。訴訟與知識產權轉讓索引。沒有一樣好看。在地緣政治成為環境噪音時，這些才是「我們已準備上市」的意思。關於中國崛起的增長投影片替代不了清單。增長與就緒回答不同問題。

按峰會日曆排工作，不要按希望排。若領袖週會吃掉管理層注意力，需要行政總裁與財務總監簽名的清單項，在那週之前做完。峰會期間的帶寬留給現場盡職問題，不要留給找失踪附件。2026年11月9日至13日中概偏軟的收市，提醒你：即使標普堅挺，公開可比公司仍可彆扭。彆扭的可比會讓銀行家對檔案質素更迂腐。檔案齊全，迂腐活得過；檔案是一堆好意，迂腐是致命的。

點名開場電話裏不辯論的美中議題。你會描述結構、許可、牌照延誤時會怎樣。你不會審理外交政策。帶着辯論政治的心情進門的創辦人，離開時往往跳過了結構。結構是承銷商能放進文件的東西。政治是這個月新聞已免費供應的東西。把文件交給房間。

考慮美國與香港雙軌的公司，現在就寫雙軌規則。哪些披露不同。哪些財務準則。哪些治理清理橫豎都要做。沒有規則的雙軌，在峰會報道下會變成雙重混亂，每個外人都假設你的時機是政治的。你的時機應由檔案就緒與現金跑道把門。星期一前用書面把關口告訴董事會。

生技發行人應在就緒包加入 CMC 與臨床場地附錄。方法轉移狀態、穩定性日曆、場地合約，以及出行密集的一週誰能主持遠程質量電話。上市流程卡在製造句子上，與卡在市場窗口上一樣常見。峰會週只是讓停滯更顯眼，因為所有人已因其他理由看着中國。

用單一負責人收口。通常由財務或律師在行政總裁協調下，在 APEC 噪音見頂前的日期，把清單每行標成綠、黃或紅。黃的要有具名修復。紅的要有決定：流程滑期、橋接，或砍範圍。就緒不是看完英偉達與中概股後宣布的感覺。就緒是打過分的清單。趁預期仍比危機便宜，先打分。
