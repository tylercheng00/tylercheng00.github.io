"We are a platform" is one of the most common sentences in a biotech first meeting. It means the technology can, in principle, produce many products. It is often true. It is also often a way to avoid naming the one product the company is actually going to make first, with this team, on this money.

A platform that has not chosen is a set of options. Investors do not fund a set of options at the same price as a plan.

### The first product is a management decision

The science may support several directions. The company can only walk down one of them properly. Choosing the first product is not a claim that the others are worthless. It is a claim about sequence. Which patient, which indication, which formulation, which partner, and which risk you are willing to stare at for the next two years.

If the answer is "all of them, because the platform is the value", ask who is spending Tuesday on which one. A platform with three equal programmes and one team is three programmes done slowly. Say the order.

### What has to be true for the second product to be real

A platform earns the name when the second product is cheaper, faster or more likely because the first one existed. Name that inheritance. A manufacturing method. A delivery particle. A data package a regulator has already seen. An assay the team no longer has to invent.

If the second product would start from a blank page, you may still have a good technology. You do not yet have a platform story. You have a product, and a hope. Those can be funded. They should not be described as the same thing.

### Kill a direction on paper

Write down one application you are not pursuing, and why. Not forever. For this round. The act of killing something is what makes the remaining choice believable. A slide with six logos of possible markets, and no sentence about which one you refused, reads as a menu. Menus are for people who have not decided what the company is for.

### Keep the option, lose the fog

You can protect the wider intellectual property. You can mention the second and third shots in an appendix, with the evidence you actually have, which may be thin. What you should not do is let the main narrative hover above all of them so that no single failure can ever count. A story in which nothing could falsify the platform is a story in which nothing is being tested.

Choose. Then let the platform be the reason the next choice will be better, not the reason this choice can wait.

「我們是一個平台」是生物科技第一次會議裏最常見的句子之一。它的意思是，這項技術原則上可以做出很多產品。這常常是真的。它也常常是一種逃避：逃避說出這家公司、這個團隊、用這一筆錢，實際上會先做的那一個產品。

一個還沒有選擇的平台，是一組期權。投資者不會用計劃的價格，為一組期權出錢。

### 第一個產品是管理決定

科學也許支持幾個方向。公司只能好好走其中一條。選擇第一個產品，不是宣稱其他的沒有價值。它是關於次序的宣稱。哪一個病人、哪一個適應症、哪一種製劑、哪一個夥伴，以及未來兩年你願意盯着的是哪一個風險。

如果答案是「全部，因為價值在平台」，問星期二誰在做哪一個。一個平台、三個同等的項目、一個團隊，就是三個做得慢的項目。把次序說出來。

### 第二個產品要成真，甚麼必須是真的

平台配得上這個名字，是當第二個產品因為第一個存在而更便宜、更快，或更有可能。把這份繼承寫出來。一個生產方法。一顆遞送粒子。一套監管機構已經看過的數據。一個團隊不用再發明的檢測。

如果第二個產品要從白紙開始，你也許仍有好技術。你還沒有一個平台故事。你有一個產品，和一個希望。這些可以被投。它們不該被說成同一件事。

### 在紙上殺掉一個方向

寫下一個你不追求的應用，以及為甚麼。不是永遠。是這一輪。殺掉一件事，剩下的選擇才可信。一頁投影片有六個可能市場的標誌，卻沒有一句你拒絕了哪一個，讀起來是一張菜單。菜單是給還沒決定公司是為了甚麼的人的。

### 留下期權，去掉迷霧

你可以保護更廣的知識產權。你可以在附錄提到第二、第三個機會，並附上你真正有的證據，那可能很薄。你不該讓主線故事懸在所有機會之上，以致沒有任何一次失敗算數。一個沒有任何事情能證偽平台的故事，是一個沒有東西正在被檢驗的故事。

做出選擇。然後讓平台成為下一個選擇會更好的理由，而不是這一次選擇可以再等的理由。
