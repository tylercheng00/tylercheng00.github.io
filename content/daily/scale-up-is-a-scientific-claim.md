::: en
A lab preparation and a batch are not two sizes of one object. Heat leaves differently. Mixing takes longer. A filter that was a detail becomes the step that fails. Impurities that were invisible become the thing a specification has to name. When a company says "scale-up is straightforward", they are making a scientific claim, whether or not they meant to.

The claim is: the product at the new scale is the same product, in every way that matters, and we know which ways matter.

### Sameness has to be defined before you scale

You cannot discover what "the same" means after the large batch looks a bit different. Decide, while the process is still small, which properties are the product. Size, if it is a particle. Potency, if it is a biologic. A residual you already know is toxic. A release rate. Write the short list, and write how each will be measured. The large batch then has something to be guilty of, or innocent of. Without the list, every difference becomes a negotiation about whether it counts.

### The first scale-up is an experiment, so fund it like one

Treat the first larger batch as a study with a hypothesis, not as the start of supply. Hypothesis: these properties stay inside these bounds. Budget the batch so that a negative result is affordable. Teams get into trouble when the first large batch is already promised to a partner, an animal study and a photograph. Then a difference cannot be reported cleanly, because too many people need it not to be a difference. Promise the batch after it has passed, not before it has been asked to.

### Change one thing

Going from a beaker to a larger vessel while also changing the supplier, the order of addition and the person in charge is not a scale-up. It is four experiments sharing a bad day. If you must change more than scale, say so, and do not attribute the outcome to scale alone. The record should let a later hire see what moved. A later hire is the reader who matters. You will not remember.

### Tell investors which claim you are not making

There is an honest version of early manufacturing. We can make it at this scale, this many times, with these properties controlled, and we have not yet shown the next scale. That sentence is fundable. The sentence "manufacturing is solved" is not a gift to the round. It is a hostage. The first time a batch drifts, the company has to walk back a word it used to sound finished. Better to have sounded unfinished and precise.

Scale is not a favour the universe does for a method that worked once. It is a new claim, and it needs its own evidence.

::: zh
實驗室的一次製備，和一批生產，不是同一件東西的兩個尺寸。熱的離開方式不同。混合更久。一個本來是細節的過濾，變成失敗的那一步。原本看不見的雜質，變成質量標準必須點名的東西。當一家公司說「放大是直接的」，他們是在做一個科學主張，無論他們有沒有這個意思。

主張是：新規模下的產品仍是同一個產品，在每一個要緊的方面，而我們知道哪些方面要緊。

### 相同必須在放大之前被定義

你不能在大批看起來有點不同之後，才發現「相同」是甚麼意思。趁製程還小的時候決定：哪些性質就是這個產品。如果是粒子，就是大小。如果是生物藥，就是效力。一種你已經知道有毒的殘留。一個釋放速率。寫下這張短清單，並寫下每一項如何量度。大批之後就有東西可以是有罪的，或是清白的。沒有這張清單，每一個差異都變成一次關於它算不算數的談判。

### 第一次放大是一個實驗，就照實驗來撥款

把第一次較大的批次當成一個有假設的研究，而不是供應的開始。假設：這些性質停留在這些界限內。為這一批做預算時，讓一個負面結果是負擔得起的。團隊出事，是當第一個大批已經答應了給一個夥伴、一個動物研究和一張照片。然後一個差異就無法被乾淨地報告，因為太多人需要它不是一個差異。在批次通過之後才承諾它，而不是在它被質問之前。

### 一次只改一件事

從燒杯走到更大的容器，同時換供應商、改加入次序、換負責人，這不是放大。這是四個實驗共用糟糕的一天。如果你必須改的不只是規模，就說出來，不要把結果只歸給規模。記錄應該讓後來入職的人看見甚麼動了。後來入職的人才是要緊的讀者。你不會記得。

### 告訴投資者你沒有在做哪個主張

早期生產有一個誠實的版本。我們可以在這個規模做出來，做這麼多次，這些性質受控，而下一個規模我們還沒有顯示。這句話是可以被投的。「生產已經解決」不是給這一輪的禮物。它是一個人質。第一次批次漂移，公司就要收回一個用來顯得完成的詞。更好是聽起來未完成，但精確。

放大不是宇宙對一個成功過一次的方法的恩惠。它是一個新的主張，它需要自己的證據。
