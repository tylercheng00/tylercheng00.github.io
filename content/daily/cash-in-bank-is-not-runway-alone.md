::: en
Gross cash looks comforting. Net of payables, deferred obligations, and committed hires, the number changes. Report the tighter figure to the board. Optimism belongs in the plan, not in the cash line.

A company that surprises itself on payables will surprise its investors next.

::: zh
總現金看起來安慰。減應付、遞延義務、已承諾的招聘之後，數字會變。向董事會報告更緊的那個。樂觀放在計劃裏，不要放在現金那一行。

在應付上驚嚇自己的公司，下一步也會驚嚇投資者。
