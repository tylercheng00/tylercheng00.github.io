::: en
Chip-export politics will sit near every China-linked conversation this week whether your product is a model, a drug, a device, or a marketplace. The reason is atmospheric: Nvidia has been a stressed public name on recent delayed data, Huang-centred coverage keeps AI chip sales in the feed, and APEC week in Shenzhen raises the general volume on US–China technology themes. Atmosphere is not a brief for you to orate. Atmosphere is a prompt to show whether your company has a dependency someone else can understand.

Build a one-page exhibit titled Tools, compute, and constrained inputs. List categories: training or inference compute, design software, specialised chips in devices, laboratory instruments with controlled components, cloud regions, and third-party AI APIs. For each line: vendor geography, substitute tested or not, licence or attestation on file, and operational impact if access tightens. Empty lines are allowed. Empty lines that say “none” with a reason are better than omitting the page. Omitting the page invites the reader to invent your stack from the last company they punished.

Keep opinions off the page. You are not scoring White House strategy or Senate banking motives. You are showing homework. Founders who perform geopolitical fluency often skip the homework and then sound surprised when diligence asks for vendor contracts. The contracts are the point. If a biotech CRO pipeline depends on a particular sequencer controller or GPU-backed analysis vendor, say so. If your consumer app only uses ordinary cloud CPU in a US region for US users, say so. Specificity shrinks the policy shadow.

Train the room to use the exhibit. In the first meeting that mentions export controls, open the page within sixty seconds. Read two lines aloud. Offer the underlying purchase orders or cloud invoices into the data room the same day. Speed here signals maturity. Speeches about fairness signal anxiety. Summit week multiplies anxiety in the market; you do not need to add yours.

Connect the exhibit to listing and financing language. Risk factors will be drafted by counsel. Your spoken version should match the exhibit’s nouns. Mismatch — counsel says “may be subject to export restrictions,” founder says “we have no China tech risk” — is how Monday optimism becomes Wednesday cleanup. Align the nouns before the first banker call of APEC week.

Watch second-order diligence. Even companies with clean compute stacks get asked about customers who might be sensitive end users, about employees with dual appointments, and about whether any research collaboration shares model weights or process know-how across borders. Add a second short page if any of those apply: collaboration list, data-sharing rules, approval trail. If none apply, a single negative confirmation from counsel helps more than a shrug.

Assign an owner for monthly refresh. Export rules and vendor catalogues move. A page frozen in November and never touched becomes a liability in a December listing kickoff. The owner does not need to be the CEO. Finance or operations with counsel review is enough. Put the refresh date in the footer so bankers see the hygiene.

Do not trade the politics. Do not advise anyone to buy or sell Nvidia, China ADRs, or semis because of summit headlines. Last week’s public moves are useful only as weather for conversation timing. Your job is the folder. APEC business week will end. Chip-export politics will not. The exhibit you finalise on this quiet Sunday should still be accurate in December. That durability is the standard. If a paragraph would need rewriting after every headline, it is commentary. Replace it with a line item you can still verify when the cameras are gone.

無論產品是模型、藥、器械還是市場平台，本週每場中國相關對話附近都會坐着芯片出口政治。理由是氣氛：英偉達在近期延遲數據上是承壓的公開名字，圍繞黃仁勳的報道讓人工智能芯片銷售留在新聞流，深圳 APEC 週又抬高美中科技主題的總音量。氣氛不是讓你演說的簡報，而是提示你展示：公司有沒有一份別人看得懂的依賴說明。

做一頁附件，標題叫「工具、算力與受限制投入」。列出類別：訓練或推理算力、設計軟件、設備內特殊芯片、帶管制部件的實驗室儀器、雲區域、第三方人工智能接口。每一行寫：供應商地理、替代是否測過、檔案中有無牌照或證明、准入收緊時的營運影響。空白行可以；寫「無」並附理由的空白行，好過整頁省略。省略整頁，等於邀請讀者用上一家被他們懲罰過的公司來發明你的技術棧。

意見不要上頁。你不是在給白宮策略或參議院銀行動機打分，你是在出示功課。表演地緣政治流利的創辦人，往往跳過功課，然後對盡調要供應商合約感到意外——合約才是重點。若生技合約研究組織流水線依賴某款測序控制器或基於圖形處理器的分析供應商，說出來；若消費應用只在美國區域為美國用戶使用普通雲中央處理器，說出來。具體性會縮小政策影子。

訓練會議室使用這份附件。第一場提到出口管制的會議，六十秒內打開這一頁，大聲讀兩行，當日把採購單或雲發票放進資料室。這裏的速度信號是成熟；關於公平的演說信號是焦慮。峰會週會放大市場焦慮，你不必再加自己的。

把附件接到上市與融資語言。風險因素由律師起草；你口頭版本的名詞應與附件一致。不一致——律師說「可能受出口限制」，創辦人說「我們沒有中國科技風險」——就是星期一樂觀變成星期三清理的路徑。APEC 週第一次銀行家電話前，先對齊名詞。

留意二階盡調。即使算力堆疊乾淨的公司，也會被問到客戶是否可能是敏感最終用戶、員工有沒有雙重任命、有沒有研究合作跨境分享模型權重或工藝訣竅。若有任何一項適用，加第二頁短文：合作清單、數據分享規則、批准軌跡。若都不適用，律師的一句否定確認，比聳肩有用。

指定每月刷新的負責人。出口規則與供應商目錄會動；十一月凍結、之後從不碰的一頁，在十二月上市開場會變成負債。負責人不必是行政總裁，財務或營運加上律師審閱已夠。把刷新日期放在頁腳，讓銀行家看見衛生。

不要交易政治。不要因為峰會標題建議任何人買賣英偉達、中概或半導體。上週公開波動只適合作為對話時機的天氣；你的工作是資料夾。APEC 商務週會結束，芯片出口政治不會。你在這個安靜星期日定稿的附件，到十二月仍應準確——耐用才是標準。若一段文字每條標題都要重寫，那是評論；換成鏡頭離開後你仍能核實的一行。
