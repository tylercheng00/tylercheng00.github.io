The hard part starts after the term sheet難的部分，從條款清單之後才開始

People photograph the signed term sheet. Three weeks later the money is not there, and nobody can say which line is stuck.條款清單簽了，大家拍照。三個星期後錢還沒到，沒有人說得出卡在哪一行。

A signed term sheet is a good day. Then three weeks pass and the wire has not left, and the status on the group chat is still "almost". The excitement was real. The list was not.

I treat the signature as permission to start the annoying part.

Write the closing list the same day. Each row is a document or a consent, with a person's name and a date. Board approval. A shareholder waiver. The IP assignment from the early employees that nobody ever chased. A bank account that can actually receive the money. If a row says "the lawyers", it is not a row yet. A name is the person who will chase it on Tuesday.

The list will be wrong in places. Fine. A wrong list can be fixed. A round with no list gets fixed by panic, one email at a time.

Read the conditions as if you wanted an excuse to walk away. "Subject to diligence" means someone is still allowed to leave. Go and close the gaps you could use yourself, before the other side finds them. This is not about being clever. It is about seeing your own round the way a cautious person sees it.

Company counsel, investor counsel and the accountant will otherwise run three calendars. I want one short call, twice a week, list on the screen. What moved. What date you will actually commit to. If something has not moved, say so in the first minute. "We're hopeful" is not a status.

When diligence turns up a fact you forgot, send it, with what you propose to do. Waiting in the hope it will not matter is how the week between signing and closing goes sour. Most facts are survivable. A fact that arrives from their lawyer, late, is a different fact.

The round is closed when the last condition is met and the money moves. Everything between the photo and that moment is the job.

簽條款清單那天心情很好。然後三個星期過去，錢沒有動，群裡的進度還是「快了」。興奮是真的。清單不是。

我把那個簽名看成：可以開始做討厭的部分了。

當天就把成交清單寫下來。每一行是一份文件或一個同意，旁邊是人名和日期。董事會。某個股東的放棄。早期員工那份一直沒人追的知識產權轉讓。一個真的收得到錢的銀行戶口。一行寫「律師」，就還不是一行。名字是星期二會去追的那個人。

清單有些地方會錯。沒關係。錯的可以改。沒有清單的那一輪，只能靠驚慌來改，一封郵件一封郵件。

把先決條件讀成你想找藉口走開。「以盡職審查為準」的意思是，還有人可以走。你自己用得上的那些缺口，在對方找到之前補上。不是為了聰明。是用一個謹慎的人的眼睛看自己這一輪。

不然公司律師、投資者律師、會計師各走各的日曆。我要的是每星期兩次短會，清單投在螢幕上。甚麼動了。你肯承諾的下一個日期是哪天。沒動就第一分鐘講。「我們有希望」不是進度。

盡職審查翻出一件你忘了的事，連同你建議怎麼處理，一起送出去。乾等，希望它不要緊，是簽字和匯款之間變味的方式。大部分事實都扛得住。對方律師很晚才發出來的那件，是另一種事實。

最後一項條件做完、錢動了，這一輪才結束。拍照和那個時刻之間，就是工作。
