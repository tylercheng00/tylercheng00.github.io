Roadshow dates feel like progress. Flights, hotels, a sequence of rooms timed for “after APEC.” On 16 November 2026 from Hong Kong, that temptation is loud: pre-APEC Shenzhen week is building, US–China capital tension is the market’s default language, Nvidia–Huang chip politics already shapes what US counsel asks first, and mid-month China filings windows make every founder want a calendar that looks busy. The work that decides whether those rooms waste everyone’s time is quieter: a folder that opens, with names that match, and numbers that survive a second look next to an export-control headline.

I treat listing readiness as a set of files before I treat it as a story — especially in a mid-month window when filings language and roadshow language collide. Cap table that reconciles. Financials that match the last board pack. Contracts that say who owns what. A China-exposure description written in ordinary words, not borrowed from a summit talking point. If any of those are missing, the first serious buyer will ask for them on day one of the post-summit tour. You can either have them, or spend the roadshow promising to send them later while the tape is still arguing about chips and diplomacy.

“Later” is expensive under capital tension. It turns a meeting into a chase. It also tells the room that the company is still assembling itself while claiming readiness for a US process. Some buyers will wait. Many will move to the next name on their list — especially buyers whose compliance teams are already overloaded with China-linked packs this month.

A useful readiness page on a website is only a map. The real object is the folder. Can counsel open it without you on the call. Can a banker find the latest version without asking which WhatsApp thread holds it. Can a New York associate answer “where does the IP sit” and “which step touches controlled tech or compute” from documents, not from your memory of a Shenzhen preview dinner. If the answer is no, fix the folder before you fix the deck design, and before you book the rooms that assume APEC week will “unlock” interest.

Build for the diligence habits this news cycle actually produces. Site map: process step, city, entity, quality system. Related-party list with daylight. Cap table that matches the corporate chart. Method-transfer or CRO pages if you claim manufacturing readiness. A one-page China-operating map: cash, IP, data, permissions. None of that is a politics seminar. It is how you stop summit atmosphere from becoming a week of follow-up emails.

You do not need perfection to start conversations in mid-November. You need a baseline that does not collapse under ordinary questions asked by people who have just read chip-sale politics. Build that first. Then book the rooms. The sequence sounds slower while everyone else is previewing Shenzhen forums. It usually finishes sooner — because the folder, not the flight, is what turns a soft circle into a dated process.

If you already booked travel against the summit calendar, freeze new meetings until the feared files are in place. Send counsel the paragraphs that mention bay-area supply chains, AI-adjacent partnerships, or export footnotes. Update the spoken script to match the redlines. Then reopen the calendar. Pre-APEC buildup rewards people who look prepared, not people who look booked.

This is listing-readiness hygiene for a noisy mid-November, not investment advice and not a prediction about APEC or capital-market outcomes.

路演日期感覺像進度。機票、酒店、一連串對齊「APEC 之後」的房間。以香港時間計，2026 年 11 月 16 日，這種誘惑很響：深圳 APEC 週前在預熱，美中資本緊張是市場預設語言，黃仁勳—英偉達芯片政治已塑造美國律師先問甚麼，月中中國相關申報窗口又讓每個創辦人都想要一本看起來很忙的日曆。決定那些房間會不會浪費所有人時間的工作更安靜：一個打得開的資料夾，名字對得上，數字在出口管制標題旁邊仍經得起第二次看。

我先把上市準備度當一組檔案，再當故事——尤其在月中窗口，申報語言與路演語言撞在一起時。對得上的股權表。和上次董事會材料一致的財務。寫明誰擁有甚麼的合同。用普通話寫的中國風險描述，不是從峰會談話要點借來的。缺任何一項，認真的買家會在峰會後巡迴的第一天就要。你要麼已有，要麼整趟路演都在答應稍後補寄，而行情仍在爭論芯片與外交。

在資本緊張下，「稍後」很貴。它把會議變成追討。也告訴房間：這家公司一邊主張已為美國流程就緒，一邊仍在組裝自己。有些買家會等。很多人會轉去名單上下一個名字——尤其合規團隊這個月已被中國相關材料包塞滿的買家。

網站上的準備度頁面只是地圖。真正的物件是資料夾。律師能不能在你不在電話上時打開它。銀行家能不能找到最新版本，而不必問哪個 WhatsApp 對話裏。紐約助理能不能從文件——而不是你對深圳預告晚宴的記憶——回答「知識產權在哪」和「哪一步碰觸受管制科技或算力」。答案是否定的，先修資料夾，再修投影片設計，也先於預訂假設 APEC 週會「解鎖」興趣的房間。

按這個新聞周期實際產生的盡職習慣來建。場地圖：工藝步驟、城市、主體、質量體系。有日光的關聯方清單。與架構圖一致的股權表。若你主張製造就緒，放方法轉移或外包研究頁。一頁中國營運圖：現金、知識產權、數據、許可。這些都不是政治研討會。它是阻止峰會氣氛變成一週追問郵件的方法。

在十一月中旬開始對話不需要完美。你需要一個在剛讀完芯片銷售政治的人所問的普通問題下不會塌的底線。先建那個。再訂房間。當其他人都在預告深圳論壇時，順序聽起來比較慢。通常反而比較早結束——因為把意向變成有日期流程的，是資料夾，不是機票。

若你已按峰會日曆訂了出行，凍結新會議，直到害怕的檔案到位。把提到灣區供應鏈、人工智能相鄰合作或出口腳註的段落寄給律師。口頭講稿跟紅字對齊。然後再打開日曆。APEC 前預熱獎勵看起來準備好的人，不是看起來行程滿的人。

這是嘈雜十一月中旬的上市準備衛生，不是投資建議，也不是對 APEC 或資本市場結果的預測。
