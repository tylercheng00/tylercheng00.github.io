::: en
Most milestone tables are calendars in disguise. "Complete formulation work." "Hire the team." "File the patent." They can all be ticked without the company becoming less risky in any way an outsider could name. Activity is not a milestone. A retired risk is.

I want each row to survive one rude question: if this is done, what are you no longer afraid of?

### Four cells, and no fifth for decoration

For each milestone, four things. The risk it is meant to retire, in one sentence. The evidence that would count as retiring it. What it costs. How long it takes if the evidence is negative as well as if it is positive. The negative case matters. A milestone that only has a plan for success is not a plan. It is a hope with a budget.

Leave out the column called "strategic importance". If the risk sentence is clear, importance is obvious. If it is not, an adjective will not save it.

### Evidence you could show a stranger

"Positive data" is not evidence. "Three independent batches inside this specification, made by someone other than the inventor" is evidence. "A signed term sheet" is not evidence of demand. "A second purchase order from a customer who paid for the first" is getting closer. Write the evidence as a thing that can sit in the data room, not as a feeling the founder will report.

The row should be draftable by the scientist and readable by the investor without a translator in either direction. That is the whole point of the table.

### One milestone has to be allowed to fail

If every row must succeed for the company to be alive, you do not have milestones. You have a single bet broken into chapters. That is sometimes honest, and you should say so. More often, a company pretends each step is independent and then, in the appendix, reveals that step three is impossible unless steps one and two were both perfect. Show the dependency. A board can fund a dependent plan. It cannot fund a plan that pretends not to be one.

### Revisit the row when you slip

You will slip. When you do, do not edit the date and leave the risk sentence untouched, as if the same risk simply moved to next quarter. Ask whether the slip changed the risk. Sometimes it did. A delay that teaches you the assay is unstable is new information, and the table should absorb it. A table that only ever moves to the right, with the words unchanged, stops being a management tool and becomes a record of optimism.

Build the table before the round, and bring the same table to the board afterwards. If you need a different table for investors and for yourselves, one of them is for show.

::: zh
多數里程碑表是偽裝的日曆。「完成製劑工作。」「聘好團隊。」「提交專利。」這些都可以打勾，而公司並沒有以任何外人說得出的方式變得更不危險。活動不是里程碑。退掉的風險才是。

我要每一行都受得起一個無禮的問題：這件事做完，你不再害怕甚麼？

### 四格，不要第五格來裝飾

每一個里程碑，四件事。它打算退掉的風險，一句話。怎樣的證據才算退掉了。要花多少。如果證據是負面的，要多久；如果是正面的，又要多久。負面的情況要緊。一個只有成功計劃的里程碑，不是計劃。它是一個有預算的希望。

不要那一欄叫「策略重要性」的。如果風險那句話清楚，重要性是明顯的。如果不清楚，一個形容詞救不了它。

### 一個陌生人看得到的證據

「正面數據」不是證據。「三批獨立生產、落在這個質量標準內、而且不是發明人自己做的」才是證據。「簽了條款清單」不是需求的證據。「一個為第一張單付了錢的客戶，開出第二張訂單」比較接近。把證據寫成可以放進資料室的東西，而不是創辦人會報告的一種感覺。

這一行應該科學家寫得出、投資者讀得懂，兩個方向都不需要翻譯。這就是這張表的全部意義。

### 必須允許一個里程碑失敗

如果每一行都必須成功公司才活得了，你沒有里程碑。你有一個被拆成章節的單一賭注。這有時是誠實的，你應該這樣說。更常見的是，公司假裝每一步互相獨立，然後在附錄裏露出：除非第一步和第二步都完美，否則第三步不可能。把依賴畫出來。董事會可以為一個有依賴的計劃撥款。它不能為一個假裝沒有依賴的計劃撥款。

### 延誤的時候，重看那一行

你會延誤。延誤時，不要只改日期，風險那句話原封不動，好像同一個風險只是搬到下一季。問這次延誤有沒有改變風險。有時有。一次讓你知道檢測不穩定的延誤，是新資訊，表應該把它吸進去。一張只會往右移、文字從不改變的表，不再是管理工具，而是樂觀的記錄。

融資之前就把表建好，之後帶同一張表去董事會。如果你們自己用一張，給投資者看另一張，其中一張是演出。
