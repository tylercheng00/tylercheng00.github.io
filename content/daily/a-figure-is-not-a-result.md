::: en
A nice figure does a lot of work in a biotech deck, and it hides a lot. I want to know what would have made it unimpressive before I am impressed by it. What was the control. What result would have killed the claim. Did you actually run that, or did the condition appear after the data looked good.

This is not a request for more statistics on the slide. It is the sentence under the figure. We would have dropped this if we had seen X. We didn't. We ran it this many times, starting over, not just remeasuring the same tube.

Repeating a reading on the same sample is not the repeat that matters. A new day, a new batch, someone else's hands, if you can. If you can't, say which kind you did and which you haven't. The honest version is easier to fund than the decorated one, because the next person will ask anyway.

If one condition failed, put it in the back and label it. A deck with only the winning panel makes me wonder where the rest went. Showing the edge, it works here and falls apart there, is the start of a development plan. The winning panel alone is a poster.

Keep the measurement and the conclusion apart. "The particle reached the tumour" is a measurement, if you measured it. "So it will work in patients" is a different sentence, and it needs the missing steps named. The figure can stay. The title of the figure should not do more work than the experiment did.

When you present, walk one figure slowly. What you did. What you compared. What is still a hope. The investors who are right for a science company would rather understand one result than clap for six.

::: zh
生物科技簡報裡，一張漂亮的圖很能做事，也藏很多。我被它打動之前，想知道怎樣才不會被打動。對照是甚麼。怎樣的結果會把這個說法打死。那個條件是真的做了，還是數據好看之後才補上的。

我不是要投影片上多幾個 p 值。我要圖下面那句。如果看到 X，我們會放棄。我們沒看到。重做了這麼多次，是重新開始，不是同一管再量一次。

同一樣品上重複讀數，不是要緊的那種重複。做得到的話，新的一天、新的一批、另一雙手。做不到，就講你做了哪一種、哪一種還沒有。誠實的版本比較容易被投，因為下一個看的人反正會問。

有一個條件失敗了，放在後面並標明。一份只有贏的那格的簡報，我會想其餘的去了哪裡。標出邊界，這裡行、那裡散，才是開發計劃的開頭。只有贏的那格，是海報。

量到的和推下去的，分開寫。「粒子到了腫瘤」是量度，如果你真的量了。「所以病人會有效」是另一句，中間缺的步驟要寫出來。圖可以留。圖的標題不該比實驗做更多工作。

講的時候慢慢走完一張圖。做了甚麼。跟甚麼比。哪部分還只是希望。適合科研公司的投資者，寧願弄懂一個結果，不要為六個鼓掌。
