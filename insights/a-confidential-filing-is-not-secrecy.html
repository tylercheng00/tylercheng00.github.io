A confidential filing is not secrecy from yourself保密遞交，不是對自己保密

The public is not reading the draft yet. Your lawyers, your auditors and the regulator's staff are. Confidential does not mean unfinished.公眾還沒在讀那份草稿。你的律師、核數師和監管機構的人在讀。保密不是還沒寫完。

US capital markets美國資本市場10 Nov 20262026年11月10日3 min read閱讀約 3 分鐘

Founders hear that a foreign company can first send a draft registration statement without the world reading it, and they relax. Half of that is right. The public isn't reading it yet. Everyone else who matters is. Treating the confidential draft as a private sketch is how you waste the only advantage the process gives you.

The pages that hurt are the ones to write early. Related parties. What the offshore company actually owns. Revenue sitting in two customers. A weakness the auditor already mentioned in a private letter. Risks that are yours, not copied from a peer and lightly edited. Put them in while the comments are still private. Leave them out "to keep it clean" and the comments arrive later, louder, closer to the day you wanted to print a prospectus.

Confidential is not a waiver of arithmetic. The financials, the share count, the use of proceeds and the business section have to describe one company. If the management accounts and the audit tell two stories, don't file one of them and hope a response letter will explain the gap. Write the explanation until it is short enough to live in the document.

Everything in that draft should be something you can imagine on a website. Wording will change. The fact will not become secret again. If there is a fact the company cannot bear to see in print, that is a problem for the listing, not a drafting problem. Say it in the room before it shows up as a comment.

The useful gift is time without a share price and without headlines. Spend it closing audit gaps, signing the contracts the document assumes, making the legal chart match the operating one. Don't spend it on sixteen rounds of adjectives.

It is a dress rehearsal with a serious audience. Come with the play written. The privacy is so you can rewrite in peace, not so you can walk on with an outline.

創辦人聽說外國公司可以先交註冊聲明草稿，世界還讀不到，就放鬆了。這件事對了一半。公眾暫時不在讀。其他要緊的人都在讀。把保密草稿當成私人素描，等於把這個程序唯一的好處浪費掉。

痛的那些頁，要早寫。關聯方。境外公司實際擁有甚麼。收入坐在兩個客戶身上。核數師已經在私人信裡提過的弱點。屬於你的風險，不是從同業抄來改兩個字的。意見還是私密的時候放進去。為了「保持乾淨」留空，意見會來得更晚、更大聲，而且更接近你想印招股書的那天。

保密不是算術可以不算。財務、股數、資金用途和業務，要講同一家公司。管理賬和審計講兩個故事，不要遞交其中一個，指望回覆信解釋缺口。先把解釋寫到短得可以放進文件裡。

草稿裡的每件事，都應該是你可以想像出現在網站上的。措辭會改。事實不會再變回秘密。有一件事是公司無法忍受印出來的，那是上市的問題，不是起草的問題。它變成一條意見之前，先在房間裡講。

真正的禮物是一段沒有股價、也沒有標題的時間。拿去補審計缺口、簽文件假設的那些合約、讓法律圖和營運圖對上。不要拿去改十六輪形容詞。

這是一場有認真觀眾的彩排。劇本帶着來。私隱是讓你安靜地改，不是讓你拿着大綱上台。
