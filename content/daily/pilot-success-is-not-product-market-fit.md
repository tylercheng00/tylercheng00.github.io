::: en
Teams announce PMF after one friendly site says the demo worked. Save the phrase. Ask whether they will pay, renew, and expand without you in the room. Until then, call it a pilot with a learning agenda.

Investors have heard PMF used loosely. Specific usage and revenue language will travel further than the acronym.

::: zh
團隊在一個友善場地說演示有效後就宣布 PMF。先別用這詞。問他們會不會付費、續約、在你不在場時擴展。在那之前，叫它帶學習議程的試點。

投資者聽過被用得很鬆的 PMF。具體的使用與收入語言，比這個縮寫走得更遠。
