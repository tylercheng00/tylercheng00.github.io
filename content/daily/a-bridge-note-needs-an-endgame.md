::: en
Bridges are tools. They are not strategies. Before you sign, write what the bridge is for: which milestone, which process, which date you expect to reopen the round. If the only answer is we need more time, you are buying calendar without a destination.

Investors who provide bridges will ask what changed since the last raise. Have that sentence ready. Cap table cleanup, a customer that paid, a study that finished — something that makes the next conversation different. A bridge that only funds the same deck again trains everyone to expect another bridge.

::: zh
橋接是工具，不是策略。簽名之前，寫下這筆橋為了甚麼：哪個里程碑、哪段流程、你預期何時重開這輪。若唯一答案是我們需要更多時間，你是在買日曆，沒有目的地。

提供橋接的投資者會問：距上次融資改變了甚麼。準備好那句話。股權表清理、付費的客戶、完成的研究——任何讓下一次對話不同的東西。只為同一份簡報再供一次電的橋，會訓練所有人期待下一座橋。
