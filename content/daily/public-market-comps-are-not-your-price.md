::: en
Decks borrow comps because they look objective. They are not your price. Liquidity, scale, disclosure, and history sit between that company and yours. Use comps to show the category, then say why your near-term number is different.

Buyers who know the space will discount a lazy comps page fast. A humble, specific bridge from today to that multiple is more credible than pretending you are already there.

::: zh
簡報借用可比，因為看起來客觀。那不是你的價錢。流動性、規模、披露、歷史，隔在那家公司和你之間。用可比顯示賽道，再說你近期的數字為甚麼不同。

認識行業的買家會很快打折一頁懶可比。從今天連到那個倍數的謙虛、具體橋樑，比假裝你已在那裏更可信。
