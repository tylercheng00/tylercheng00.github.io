::: en
A trillion-dollar market, three circles, your logo in the smallest one. Every company claims the prize is large and their aim is modest. Unless the number is built from buyers you can name, units and price and how many of them exist, the slide is borrowed confidence. Use the first market you can actually reach, and the arithmetic of that market only. The big number can live in a sentence if you still love it. It should not be page three.

"Leading", "proprietary", "unique", "AI-powered". If you delete the adjective and the sentence collapses, there was no fact in it. Say the fact. Patents granted, how many, what they cover. A result with a control. A person on the team who has done this job before. The reader will supply an adjective if the fact earns one.

A roadmap of the whole future is comforting to draw and expensive to defend. Every extra product is a question: who is doing it, what it costs, why it is not a distraction. If the honest answer is "not this round", it does not belong in the main arc. One appendix page, marked as later, is enough. The body of the deck is the company you are this year.

A logo is a claim that a relationship exists. Partners who took a meeting, an accelerator that accepted you, a university where someone once studied. If you would be uneasy forwarding that slide to them, take the mark off. You will look smaller for one page and more reliable for the rest.

Don't put the price on page four. You are asking for a judgment before anyone has agreed what the object is. Leave the ask until the risk, the use of proceeds and the milestone are clear. A reader who has followed you that far can disagree with the number and still respect the file.

Cut until each remaining page would be worth printing if the meeting were fifteen minutes. That is usually the meeting you are going to get.

一個萬億的市場、三個圓、最小的圓裡是你的標誌。每家公司都說獎很大、目標很謙。除非數字是從你叫得出名字的買家算出來的，數量、價格、這樣的人有多少，否則那一頁是借來的信心。用你真正到得了的第一個市場，以及只屬於那個市場的算術。大數字如果你還喜歡，可以活在一句話裡。它不該是第三頁。

「領先」「專有」「獨特」「人工智能驅動」。刪掉形容詞句子就塌了，裡面就沒有事實。把事實說出來。授權了的專利，多少項，覆蓋甚麼。一個有對照的結果。團隊裡一個以前做過這份工的人。事實值得的話，讀者會自己加形容詞。

畫出整個未來的路線圖，畫的時候安慰，辯的時候很貴。每一個額外產品都是問題：誰在做、多少錢、為甚麼不是分心。誠實的答案是「不在這一輪」，它就不屬於主線。附錄一頁，標明以後，就夠。正文是你今年這家公司。

標誌是在聲稱有某種關係。只是見了面的夥伴、錄取你的加速器、某人讀過的大學。把那一頁轉發給他們會不安，就把標誌拿掉。你會在一頁上看起來小一點，其餘的頁更可靠。

不要在第四頁放價格。你是在人家還沒同意那件東西是甚麼之前，請他們判斷一個數字。要求留到風險、資金用途和里程碑都清楚了。跟到那裡的讀者可以不同意數字，仍然尊重這份文件。

剪到每一頁在會議只有十五分鐘時仍然值得印。那通常就是你會得到的會。
