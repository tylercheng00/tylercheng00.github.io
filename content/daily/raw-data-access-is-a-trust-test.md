::: en
Diligence will ask who can pull the raw. If the answer is only the founding scientist, you have key-person risk and version risk. Put files in a shared, permissioned place with names and dates.

This is not bureaucracy for its own sake. It is how a second scientist, a CRO, or a buyer can verify without theatre. Fix it before you need it in a live meeting.

::: zh
盡職審查會問誰能拉出原始數據。若答案只有創始科學家，你有關鍵人風險和版本風險。把檔案放進共享、有權限的位置，帶名字和日期。

這不是為官僚而官僚。而是第二位科學家、CRO 或買家能驗證、而不靠表演。在現場會議需要之前先修好。
