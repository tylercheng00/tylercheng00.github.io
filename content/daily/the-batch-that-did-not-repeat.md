::: en
The first batch worked. The second did not. In a young science company this is one of the most common weeks there is, and one of the most badly reported. The instinct is to say the work is "ongoing" and to hope the third batch rescues the slide. Sometimes it does. The company still learned the wrong lesson.

The batch that did not repeat is not a detour around the result. It is the result, until you can say why.

### Write down what was actually the same

"The same protocol" is rarely the same. A reagent from a new bottle. A column that had been regenerated once too often. A person who did step four from memory. An incubator that ran a degree high over a weekend nobody logged. Before you theorise about the science, list every input that was free to vary. You will not know all of them. The list of the ones you can name is already a better document than "we are optimising".

Put the list in the lab book the same week. Memory is generous to the batch that worked.

### Decide what question the failure answers

A failed repeat answers something. It might answer "we do not yet control this step". It might answer "the first result was the lucky draw". Those are different facts, and they lead to different spends. If you cannot yet tell them apart, the next experiment should be built to tell them apart, not to recreate the pretty figure as fast as possible. Chasing the figure is how teams burn two months and a pile of material to get back to the same ambiguity.

### Tell the board in one paragraph

Investors and directors do not need the chromatography. They need four sentences. What you expected. What you saw. What you think it means. What you will do in the next thirty days, and what you will stop doing. A company that can write that paragraph is under control, even when the science is not. A company that says "minor process issues" for three meetings is not.

Do not wait for the rescue batch to send the paragraph. The rescue batch is a hypothesis. The paragraph is the management.

### Protect the material you still trust

When something fails, people start changing three variables at once because it feels like action. Freeze the last batch you do trust. Keep the samples. Keep the sheet of settings. The comparison is the asset. Without it, a later success is just another anecdote, and you will not know which anecdote to manufacture.

A process that failed in public, and was then explained, is worth more than a process that has only succeeded once, in a notebook nobody else has read.

::: zh
第一批成了。第二批不成。在年輕的科研公司，這是最常見的星期之一，也是報告得最差的之一。本能是說工作「進行中」，希望第三批救回那頁投影片。有時救得回。公司仍然學錯了教訓。

沒有重現的那一批，不是繞過結果的彎路。在你說得出原因之前，它就是結果。

### 寫下究竟甚麼是相同的

「同一個實驗方案」很少真的相同。新瓶子裏的試劑。再生過太多次的管柱。一個憑記憶做第四步的人。一個週末沒有人記錄、高了一度的培養箱。在你開始建立科學理論之前，先列出每一個可以變動的輸入。你不會知道全部。你能說出名字的那張清單，已經比「我們在優化」更好。

同一個星期寫進實驗記錄。記憶對成功的那一批特別慷慨。

### 決定這次失敗回答了甚麼問題

一次失敗的重複，回答了某件事。它可能回答「我們還沒有控制這一步」。它可能回答「第一次是抽中的」。這是不同的事實，導向不同的花費。如果你還分不出來，下一個實驗就應該用來把它們分開，而不是盡快重畫那張漂亮的圖。追那張圖，是團隊燒掉兩個月和一堆材料、回到同一個含糊的方式。

### 用一段話告訴董事會

投資者和董事不需要層析圖。他們需要四句。你預期甚麼。你看到甚麼。你認為這意味着甚麼。接下來三十天你會做甚麼，以及你會停止做甚麼。寫得出這一段的公司，即使科學還不受控，管理是受控的。連續三次會議都說「輕微製程問題」的公司，不是。

不要等拯救的那一批才送出這一段。拯救的那一批是一個假設。這一段才是管理。

### 保護你仍然信任的材料

東西失敗時，人們會一次改三個變數，因為那感覺像行動。把你仍然信任的最後一批凍結。留下樣品。留下參數表。比較本身才是資產。沒有它，後來的成功只是另一則軼事，而你不會知道該把哪一則做成產品。

一個公開失敗、然後被解釋清楚的製程，價值高過一個只成功過一次、而且寫在沒有別人讀過的筆記本裏的製程。
