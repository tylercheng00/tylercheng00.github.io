::: en
Founders think of reproducibility as a scientific virtue, something good teams care about and careless teams skip. Investors, if they are paying attention, think of it as whether the asset survives a person leaving the building. A result that lives in one pair of hands is not a result the company owns.

That makes reproducibility a financing problem. It decides what, exactly, is being sold.

### Write the method for a stranger

The test is uncomfortable and fair. Could someone hired next month, skilled but new, follow the written method and land inside the same range, without a conversation with the inventor? If the honest answer is no, the method is not written. It is remembered. Memory does not transfer in a financing, and it does not survive a disagreement about what the method was.

This is not a demand for a novel's worth of protocol on day one. It is a demand that the steps which actually move the result are on paper: the order, the source of the material, the setting that must not drift, the sign that a run should be abandoned. The rest can be ordinary.

### The second pair of hands is the asset

Budget for someone other than the inventor to run the key experiment before you raise on it. The cost feels like duplication. It is the opposite of duplication. It is the moment the result moves from a person to a company. If the second person cannot do it, you have learned something cheap, relative to learning it in the middle of diligence, with a clock running and a data room already sent.

Put that second run in the use of proceeds if it has not happened yet. Say so. A reader who sees the line "transfer the method off the founder" understands the round better than a reader who sees another line called "R&D".

### Do not raise the price of a single notebook

A valuation built on data that one person can reproduce is a valuation built on that person. Sometimes that is explicit and fair: the round is to keep them, and to fund the transfer. Often it is implicit, and the model assumes the data just exist, the way a factory assumes its machines exist. Ask which one you are doing. If the key experiments have never been repeated by someone who did not design them, the deck should not speak as if the company has a process. It has a person, and people are allowed to be tired, or to leave, or to remember selectively under pressure.

### Make it a milestone with a witness

"Reproducible" should be a row someone can tick. Named operator, named method version, named acceptance range, dated. A witness can be the head of the lab who did not run it, reading the record. The point of the witness is not mistrust. It is that a future investor, or a future you, can see that the word was used in a way that had a test.

You are not financing the best day in the lab. You are financing the company's ability to have that day again.

::: zh
創辦人把可重現性想成一種科學美德，好的團隊在乎，馬虎的團隊跳過。如果投資者留心，他們想的是：這項資產在一個人離開大樓之後還在不在。一個只活在一雙手裏的結果，不是公司擁有的結果。

這使可重現性成為一個融資問題。它決定你究竟在賣甚麼。

### 把方法寫給一個陌生人

這個測試不舒服，而且公平。下個月入職的人，有技術但是新的，能不能跟着寫下來的方法，落在同一個範圍裏，而不需要跟發明人談話？如果誠實的答案是不能，方法就沒有被寫下。它是被記住的。記憶在融資裏不轉移，也活不過一場關於方法究竟是甚麼的爭論。

這不是要求第一天就有一部小說那麼長的實驗方案。這是要求那些真正推動結果的步驟在紙上：次序、材料的來源、不能漂移的那個設定、一次實驗應該放棄的訊號。其餘的可以普通。

### 第二雙手才是資產

在你拿一個結果去融資之前，為發明人以外的人再做一次關鍵實驗做預算。這個成本感覺像重複。它是重複的反面。它是結果從一個人搬到一家公司的時刻。如果第二個人做不到，你學到的東西是便宜的，相對於在盡職審查中間、時鐘在走、資料室已經送出時才學到。

如果這件事還沒發生，把它放進資金用途。並這樣說。一個讀者看到「把方法從創辦人身上轉走」這一行，會比看到另一行叫「研發」的讀者更明白這一輪。

### 不要為一本筆記本抬價

一個建立在只有一個人能重做的數據上的估值，是一個建立在那個人身上的估值。有時這是明確而且公平的：這一輪是為了留下他們，並資助轉移。常常它是隱含的，模型假設數據就在那裏，像工廠假設機器就在那裏。問你在做哪一種。如果關鍵實驗從未被一個不是設計它們的人重複過，簡報就不該說得好像公司有一個製程。它有一個人。人是可以被允許疲倦、離開，或在壓力下選擇性地記憶的。

### 把它做成一個有見證人的里程碑

「可重現」應該是一行有人可以打勾的。被點名的操作者、被點名的方法版本、被點名的接受範圍、有日期。見證人可以是沒有親自做的實驗室負責人，讀那份記錄。見證人的意義不是不信任。是未來的投資者，或未來的你，可以看見這個詞被用在一個有測試的方式上。

你融資的不是實驗室裏最好的那一天。你融資的是公司有能力再有那一天。
