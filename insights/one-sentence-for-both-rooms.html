One sentence has to be true in both rooms有一句話，必須在兩個房間裡都是真的

The lab and the investors can use different words. They cannot believe different facts. Find the sentence both will sign.實驗室和投資者可以用不同的詞。他們不能信不同的事實。找出兩邊都願意簽的那一句。

Biotech生物科技US capital markets美國資本市場13 Nov 20262026年11月13日3 min read閱讀約 3 分鐘

I spend time in two rooms. In one, people argue about whether a result will repeat and whether a batch is the same batch. In the other, people argue about whether a round will close and whether a document can be signed. The manners are different. The facts don't get to be.

The useful habit is to find one sentence that is true in both, and to refuse a story that needs the sentence to change when you walk down the corridor.

Not a slogan. A fact about the size of one breath. We can make this particle at this scale, three times, inside this specification, and we have not shown the next scale. Two customers are most of the revenue, and the round is to make that untrue. The operating company is owned through these contracts, and those contracts are what an investor would actually be buying.

If the sentence embarrasses you in the lab, it is too loose. If it embarrasses you in front of an investor, the deck you were about to show is the problem.

Read the scientists' sentence, without softening, to someone who thinks in money. Read the sentence the investors nodded at back to the person who runs the experiment. Watch which words they will not repeat. Those words are where the story inflated. Cut them. What remains is the version for the slide, the data room and the board pack. One version. The moment you keep two, you have created a job for the person whose work is to catch you.

Facts move. A batch fails. A customer leaves. The sentence should be allowed to change, in public, on purpose. What it should not do is drift, so the deck, the lab book and the last board minute are three generations of the same claim. Date it. When you update it, show the old one.

It is a test for the next decision before it is a line on a website. Does this hire make the sentence more true, or does it decorate the company while the sentence stays fragile? Does this experiment change the sentence, or does it produce a figure beside it? Does this round fund the sentence you have, or a larger one you have not earned?

The two rooms will not speak the same language. They can be asked to sign the same sentence. A company that can do that is surprisingly hard to confuse.

我的時間在兩個房間。一個房間爭一個結果會不會重現、一批是不是同一批。另一個房間爭一輪會不會成交、一份文件能不能簽。禮儀不同。事實不可以不同。

有用的習慣是找出一句兩邊都真的話，並拒絕任何需要你走過走廊時把這句改掉的故事。

不是口號。一句一個呼吸那麼長的事實。我們可以在這個規模做出這種粒子，三次，落在這個標準裡，下一個規模還沒有顯示。兩個客戶佔了大部分收入，這一輪是為了讓這句不再為真。營運公司靠這些合約被擁有，而那些合約才是投資者實際會買到的。

這句在實驗室裡讓你尷尬，它就太鬆。它在投資者面前讓你尷尬，問題在你正要展示的簡報。

把科學家同意的句子，不軟化，讀給一個用錢想事情的人。把投資者點頭的句子，讀回給做實驗的人。看哪些詞他們不願意重複。那些詞就是故事鼓起來的地方。剪掉。剩下的才是投影片、資料室和董事會文件用的版本。一個版本。你留兩個，就為那個工作是抓住你的人創造了職位。

事實會動。一批失敗。一個客戶走了。這句應該可以改，公開地、有意地。它不該自己漂，以致簡報、實驗記錄和上一份董事會記錄是同一個說法的三代。給它日期。更新的時候把舊的也亮出來。

它先是下一個決定的測試，然後才是網站上的一行。這個招聘會讓句子更真，還是句子仍然脆弱時拿來裝飾公司？這個實驗會改變句子，還是在旁邊產出一張圖？這一輪融的是你已有的這句，還是一句你還沒有贏得的更大的話？

兩個房間不會說同一種語言。可以要求它們簽同一句。做得到的公司，出奇地不容易被搞亂。
