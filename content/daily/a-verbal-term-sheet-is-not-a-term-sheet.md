::: en
People say we are aligned and the room moves as if the round were done. A verbal term sheet is useful. It is also where companies start spending like the wire already cleared.

Ask for the open points in writing. Valuation is only one line. Option pool, liquidation preference, board seats, information rights, and what happens if the close slips — those decide whether the verbal yes survives counsel. If those are we can sort it later, you do not have a term sheet. You have a mood.

Keep verbal agreements in the same list as conditional yeses. Owner, date, what document will make it real. Celebrate when signatures land. Until then, the calendar still belongs to diligence, not to the party.

::: zh
有人說我們對齊了，房間就像這輪已完成一樣動起來。口頭 term sheet 有用。公司也常在這裏開始像錢已到賬一樣花錢。

要求把未決點寫下來。估值只是一行。期權池、清算優先、董事會席位、資訊權、交割若延遲怎麼辦——這些決定口頭 yes 能不能活過律師。若都是稍後再弄，你沒有 term sheet，只有氣氛。

把口頭協議和有條件的 yes 放在同一份清單。負責人、日期、哪份文件會讓它變真。簽名落地再慶祝。在那之前，日曆仍屬於盡職審查，不屬於慶功。
