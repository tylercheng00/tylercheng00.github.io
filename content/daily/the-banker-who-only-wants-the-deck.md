::: en
Send the deck can be the start of work. It can also be a way to collect deal flow without committing attention. Watch what happens after you send it. Do they ask for the cap table, the latest financials, the China-exposure paragraph — or do they go quiet until they want another version?

Answer serious requests with a short index of the folder, not only with prettier slides. If the other side never opens the folder, you learned something cheaply. Protect your calendar. A process that never requests documents is not a process. It is a mailing list.

::: zh
把簡報寄來可以是工作的開始，也可以是收集案子卻不投入注意力的方法。看你寄出之後發生甚麼。他們是要股權表、最新財務、中國風險段落——還是安靜到又想要另一版？

回應認真要求時，給資料夾短目錄，不只給更好看的投影片。若對方從不打開資料夾，你便宜地學到一件事。保護你的日曆。從不要求文件的流程不是流程，是郵寄名單。
