::: en
Say whether the pool is pre-money or post, and what percent is unallocated. Hiring plans that assume a pool you do not have create broken offers. Put the pool next to the valuation in every board pack during a raise.

Candidates and investors both read that line. Make it boring and correct.

::: zh
說期權池是募前還是募後，未分配多少百分比。假設有池卻實際沒有的招聘計劃，會產生壞掉的 offer。融資期間每份董事會材料都把池和估值放一起。

候選人和投資者都讀那一行。讓它無聊且正確。
