There is a comforting story about raising a little: keep it light, close it with angels, save the serious process for later. Some small rounds are genuinely light, and should stay that way. Many are not. They carry the complexity of a larger round and the attention of a smaller one. That combination is how a "quick" raise becomes a season.

I would rather a founder respect a small round than romanticise it.

### The documents do not shrink with the cheque

A messy cap table is not tidier because you are only raising a modest sum. A missing IP assignment does not matter less. A convertible note stacked on two earlier notes does not become simple by being called a bridge. The legal work is driven by what already exists, not by the size of the new money. If the history is tangled, a small round can cost as much attention as a large one, for less runway. Sometimes the right small round is the one that pauses to clean the history first, even if that feels like not raising.

### Nobody's model depends on you

In a larger process, someone has a fee, a calendar and a reputation attached to closing. In a small round, the other side is often investing their own money between other jobs. They will get to you. They will get to you after the thing that pays their week. Build that into the timetable. A close that requires five busy people to read a document on the same Friday will miss that Friday. Fewer signatures, a shorter document, and a single person empowered to say yes: that is how small stays fast. Adding "just one more angel" is how small becomes slow.

### Do not import the ceremony of a large round

A data room index with eighty folders, a twenty-page management presentation, and a weekly all-hands update are tools for a process with many readers. For a small round they are a way to feel serious while the wire is not sent. Send the five documents that let a careful person say yes, and talk to them. If a sophisticated investor wants more, they will ask, and you will already have it because you built the room for yourselves. Ceremony is not diligence. It is often a substitute for a decision you have not asked for.

### Know why it is small

Small because the milestone is genuinely cheap is a good reason. Small because the larger round was not available, and nobody has said what this money changes, is a warning. Write the sentence anyway. This amount, by this date, retires this risk. If the sentence is embarrassing, the round is not small. It is unclear, and unclear rounds are the expensive ones, at any size.

有一個令人安慰的故事，關於融少一點：保持輕巧、跟天使投資者成交、把認真的流程留到以後。有些小輪確實輕巧，也應該保持輕巧。很多不是。它們帶着較大一輪的複雜，和較小一輪的注意力。這個組合，是一輪「很快」的融資變成一個季節的方式。

我寧願創辦人尊重一小輪，而不是把它浪漫化。

### 文件不會跟着支票縮小

股權表混亂，不會因為你只融一筆不大的錢就變整齊。缺一份知識產權轉讓，不會變得較不要緊。一張可換股票據疊在兩張更早的票據上，不會因為被叫成過橋就變簡單。法律工作由已經存在的東西驅動，不是由新錢的大小驅動。如果歷史是纏着的，一小輪可以花掉跟一大輪一樣多的注意力，換來更短的跑道。有時正確的小輪，是先停下來把歷史弄乾淨的那一輪，即使那感覺像沒有在融資。

### 沒有人的模型依賴你

在較大的流程裏，有人的費用、日曆和名聲系於成交。在小輪裏，對方常常是在其他工作之間，投自己的錢。他們會處理你。他們會在處理完那件支付他們這個星期的事情之後處理你。把這個寫進時間表。一個需要五個忙碌的人在同一個星期五讀同一份文件的成交，會錯過那個星期五。更少的簽名、更短的文件、一個被授權說好的人：這是小仍然可以快的方式。加上「再多一個天使」，是小變成慢的方式。

### 不要搬進大輪的儀式

一個有八十個資料夾的資料室索引、一份二十頁的管理層演示、每星期一次的全員更新，是給有很多讀者的流程的工具。對小輪來說，它們是在匯款還沒送出時感覺自己很認真的方式。送出那五份讓一個謹慎的人可以說好的文件，然後跟他們談。如果一位老練的投資者想要更多，他們會問，而你已經有，因為你是為自己建資料室的。儀式不是盡職審查。它常常是一個你還沒有開口要求的決定的代替品。

### 知道它為甚麼小

小，是因為里程碑真的便宜，這是好理由。小，是因為較大的一輪不可得，而沒有人說這筆錢改變甚麼，這是一個警告。反正把那句話寫下來。這個金額，在這個日期之前，退掉這個風險。如果這句話令人尷尬，這一輪就不是小。它是不清楚。不清楚的輪次才是貴的，無論大小。
