Diligence questions to expect in summit week峰會週盡調觀察：常見問題其實沒變，變的是時間

APEC business week will not invent new diligence themes. It will compress the time you have to answer familiar ones about China structure, compute, and timing.APEC報道壓縮的是回答窗口；結構、許可、算力與時機，仍是那幾道老題。

Summit week compresses attention. The questions themselves are mostly old. US readers financing or listing China-touching companies will ask about control, cash location, IP ownership, data residency, customer concentration, related parties, and whether any critical tool or reagent sits behind an export-sensitive path. APEC coverage in Shenzhen, plus ongoing chip-export politics, makes those questions arrive earlier in the call and with less small talk. Preparation is a script of short answers backed by exhibits, not a longer speech about the summit.

Expect the structure question first. Which entity do investors buy. Which entity operates in China. How economics move between them. Have the chart on screen in the first ten minutes. If the answer requires a five-minute verbal maze, the chart is not ready. Summit week is a poor time to discover that your corporate story and your banking story disagree. Align them on Sunday; show the aligned version on Monday.

Expect the permission question next. Licences, filings, data-export constraints, human-genetic rules if you are biotech, and any sector permits that renew on a calendar. Say the renewal dates. Say the owner. Say the first operational move if a renewal slips. Investors price a dated dependency. They haircut a vague assurance that “we are fully compliant.” Compliance language without dates sounds like a slogan when the news is already about rules and borders.

Expect the compute-and-supply question even if you are not a chip company. Last week’s public tape put Nvidia in a sharp decline while broader indices rose, and China ADRs were weak. That pattern keeps export-control and AI-tooling themes near the top of feeds. Be ready to say what you use, where it runs, and what you do if a vendor path narrows. If the honest answer is “not applicable,” say why in one sentence and move on. Lingering on Nvidia colour you do not have wastes credibility.

Expect the timing question dressed as geopolitics. “Should we wait until after APEC?” Translate it back into clocks: your runway in weeks, their investment committee calendar, underwriter bandwidth, and whether your file is actually complete. If the file is incomplete, waiting may be rational. If the file is complete and cash is short, waiting for a photo opportunity is not a plan. Put the translation on the table so the room debates the right clock.

Expect the related-party and founder-support question with less patience than usual. Political attention raises the cost of discovering family loans, affiliate sales, or informal IP holding late. Bring the schedule. Bring the unwind plan. Bring counsel’s plain paragraph. Summit week does not create related-party risk. It creates shorter fuses.

Biotech-specific expectables: site access for quality calls during travel-heavy days, method-transfer status, stability pulls that cannot slip because executives are in Shenzhen, and who signs GMP-related statements if the CMO needs a same-day reply. Put names beside each. Diligence threads that bounce between personal WeChat accounts during a summit will look amateur in a US partner meeting the same afternoon.

Rehearse with a timer. Two minutes per answer. Exhibit number spoken aloud. No invented quotes from officials, no private claims about meetings you do not have, no investment advice about ADRs or Nvidia. High-level public weather is enough to acknowledge. Then return to paper. The founders who survive summit-week diligence are not the most eloquent on geopolitics. They are the fastest to the correct exhibit.

峰會週壓縮的是注意力，不是問題本身。為沾中國的公司融資或上市的美國讀者，仍會問控制權、現金位置、知識產權歸屬、數據駐留、客戶集中度、關聯方，以及有沒有關鍵工具或試劑落在出口敏感路徑之後。深圳APEC報道，加上持續的芯片出口政治，只是讓這些問題更早出現在電話裏，寒暄更少。準備宜是一套有附件支撑的短答劇本，而不是一篇更長的峰會演說。

先預料到結構問題：投資人買哪個主體；哪個主體在中國營運；經濟利益如何在其間流動。前十分鐘把圖放上螢幕。若答案需要五分鐘口頭迷宮，圖還沒就緒。峰會週是個糟糕的時間點，才發現公司故事與銀行故事不一致。星期日對齊；星期一出示對齊後的版本。

接着預料到許可問題：牌照、備案、數據出口限制、生技則還有人類遺傳相關規則，以及按日曆續期的行業許可。說續期日，說負責人，說續期延後了的第一步營運動作。投資人給有日期的依賴定價；他們會剃掉「我們完全合規」這種含糊保證。新聞已在談規則與邊界時，沒有日期的合規語言聽起來像口號。

預料到算力與供應問題，即使你不是芯片公司。上週公開行情裏英偉達大跌、大盤上升、中概偏弱。這種格局讓出口管制與人工智能工具主題留在新聞上方。準備好說你用甚麼、在哪裏跑、供應商路徑收窄時做甚麼。若誠實答案是「不適用」，一句話說明為甚麼就轉題。纏着你沒有的英偉達色彩，只會浪費信用。

預料到披着地緣政治外衣的時機問題。「該不該等APEC之後？」把它翻譯回鐘：你以星期計的跑道、他們的投資委員會日曆、承銷精力，以及檔案是否真的齊。檔案不齊，等待可能合理；檔案齊而現金短，等一張合照機會不是計劃。把翻譯放上桌，會議室才會辯對的那座鐘。

預料到關聯方與創辦人支持問題，耐心會比平時少。政治關注升高，晚發現家族借款、關聯銷售或不正式的知識產權持有，成本更高。帶表，帶解除計劃，帶律師的白話段。峰會週不製造關聯方風險，只製造更短的引信。

生技特有的預期：出行密集日子質量電話的場地接入、方法轉移狀態、不能因高管在深圳而延後的穩定性取樣，以及合約生產商需要當日回覆時誰簽優良生產規範相關聲明。每項旁邊寫名字。峰會期間在個人通訊軟件之間彈跳的盡調線，同一天下午在美國合夥人會議上會顯得業餘。

用計時器彩排。每個答案兩分鐘，大聲說出附件編號。不要發明官員引語，不要聲稱你沒有的私人會面，不要對中概或英偉達給投資建議。承認高階公開天氣已夠，然後回到紙。活過峰會週盡調的創辦人，不是地緣政治最會說的人，而是最快找到正確附件的人。
