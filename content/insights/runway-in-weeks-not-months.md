::: en
“We have twelve months of runway” is a sentence that ages badly in a week like 18 November 2026. The APEC CEO and business leaders summit in Shenzhen fills the feed with Asia-Pacific prosperity and bay-area cooperation. Leaders from Korea, Vietnam and Canada add more footage. Jensen Huang, Nvidia and AI chip export-control politics remain a parallel weather system. Founders feel pressure to sound long-term and calm. Boards still need to know how many weeks of cash sit in the account if the round slips.

Months hide burn. Weeks force a conversation about what stops.

Ask for weeks of cash at the current burn, with no new money. Then ask what changes in week six if the close moves — hiring pause, experiment pause, landlord conversation, bridge ask. The summit can improve mood in a dinner room and leave your payroll calendar untouched. US–China capital markets can look “constructive” on television and still take another month to circulate a term sheet. Your model should not assume the dinner becomes the close.

Volatile weeks create optimistic spreadsheets. Someone adds a soft circle from a guest who liked the bay-area slide. Someone assumes chip-policy noise will “clear after the leaders’ meetings,” so the US process restarts on a hopeful date. Someone books a December roadshow as if exclusivity, diligence and counsel redlines will politely wait. Put the assumption under the runway number. If the only way the months look good is a miracle close timed to diplomacy, say that in plain language while there is still time to cut or raise a bridge.

Boards can work with bad news early. They cannot work with a surprise that the company is three weeks from payroll. Put the number in the update. Put the unit in weeks. Put the date of the cash zero next to the date of the next expected close. If those dates are close, the update is not a vibe check about APEC. It is an operating decision memo.

Practical runway hygiene for this week:

1. Recalculate cash runway in weeks at current burn, excluding hoped-for closes and excluding “summit interest.”
2. List the three spend items that stop first if week six arrives without a signed term sheet.
3. Separate mood from money: prosperity speeches and Nvidia headlines may change buyer tone; they do not refill the account.
4. If you grant exclusivity during summit week, price it against weeks of cash, not against the length of the leaders’ agenda.
5. Tell counsel and the board the same week-count. Two different runways is how surprises are born.

Founders sometimes worry that speaking in weeks sounds desperate. In a headline-heavy week, speaking in months without assumptions sounds evasive. Precision is not panic. It is how you keep the right to ask for help before the help has to be emergency help.

None of this is a forecast that APEC will help or hurt cross-border fundraising. It is not a view on chip export controls. It is not investment advice. It is a reminder that volatile news and slow cash often share the same calendar week — and that “twelve months” is a story, while “fourteen weeks at current burn, no new money” is a fact a board can use.

If the round is on track, weeks still help: they show you know the machine. If the round is slipping, weeks are the only honest unit. Say runway in weeks, not in hopeful months — especially while Shenzhen’s summit lights are bright and your burn rate does not care.

「我們有十二個月跑道」這句話，在2026年11月18日這種星期特別容易過時。深圳APEC工商領導人活動用亞太繁榮與灣區合作填滿報道；韓、越、加領導人再加畫面。黃仁勳、英偉達與人工智能芯片出口管制政治仍是另一套天氣。創辦人感到要顯得長遠、冷靜；董事會卻仍需知道：若這輪融資延遲，戶口以星期計還剩多少現金。

用「月」會藏起消耗；用「星期」會逼出：甚麼要停。

先問：以現時消耗、沒有新資金，還有幾個星期現金。再問：若交割延後，第六個星期要改甚麼——暫停招聘、暫停實驗、跟房東談、開口借過橋。峰會可以改善晚宴氣氛，卻動不到你的發薪日曆。美中資本市場可以在電視上看起來「建設性」，卻仍要多一個月才傳出條款清單。你的模型不該假設晚宴等於交割。

波動的星期會製造樂觀試算表。有人把喜歡灣區投影片的嘉賓算進鬆散意向；有人假設芯片政策噪音會「在領導人會議後釐清」，於是美國流程在一個充滿希望的日期重啟；有人訂了十二月路演，彷彿獨家、盡職審查與律師紅線會客氣地等。把假設寫在跑道數字下面。若「月」之所以好看，只因一次配合外交時程的奇蹟交割，趁還有時間削減開支或借過橋，用白話說清楚。

董事會可以早一點消化壞消息，無法消化離發薪只剩三星期的驚喜。把數字寫進更新，單位用星期，把現金歸零日與下一次預期交割日並列。若兩日很近，這份更新就不是關於APEC的氣氛檢查，而是營運決策備忘錄。以現時消耗重算現金跑道時，應排除寄望中的交割，也排除「峰會興趣」；列出若第六個星期仍無已簽署條款清單、最先停的三項支出；把氣氛與金錢分開——繁榮演講與英偉達新聞或可改變買家語氣，卻不會把戶口填滿。若在峰會期間給出獨家，用現金星期數定價，不要用領導人議程長度定價；並告訴律師與董事會同一個星期數——兩套跑道，是驚喜誕生的方式。

創辦人有時擔心用星期計算聽起來像絕望。在新聞很重的星期，用不帶假設的「月」來說，聽起來像迴避。精確不是恐慌，而是你在求助變成緊急求助之前，仍保有求助權利的方式。

以上都不是預測APEC會幫助或傷害跨境融資，也不是對芯片出口管制的看法，更不是投資建議。波動新聞與緩慢現金常常擠在同一星期——「十二個月」是故事，「以現時消耗、沒有新資金還有十四星期」是董事會用得上的事實。

若這輪順利，用星期說仍然有用：表示你認識這台機器。若這輪在滑，星期是唯一誠實的單位。現金跑道要用星期計算，不要用樂觀的月份——尤其當深圳峰會燈火很亮，而你的消耗速度並不在意燈火的時候。
