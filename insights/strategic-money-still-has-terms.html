Strategic money still has terms策略資金仍然有條款

A corporate investor can be worth more than the cheque. That is a reason to read the paper carefully. The dinner will not be in the file in three years.企業投資者的價值可以大於那張支票。所以紙要讀仔細。三年後檔案裡沒有那頓飯。

US capital markets美國資本市場11 Nov 20262026年11月11日3 min read閱讀約 3 分鐘

A strategic investor arrives with a logo, a possible customer, and a feeling that the round is about the relationship. Founders loosen the terms because the money feels friendly. The friendliness may be real. The paper is what a person who was not at the dinner will read in three years.

The investment and the partnership are two contracts, even if one introduction produced both. A right of first look on a licence, a board seat, information that reaches into other customers, a veto on selling to their competitor: that is the commercial deal, wearing the investment as a hat. Write those terms as if the cheque were zero. Then ask whether you would sign that document for the distribution or the purchase order alone. If you wouldn't, don't sign it because a cheque is stapled to the front.

A period in which you may not talk to their competitors is an asset, and you are the one giving it. Name the length. Name what they must do during it, a paid pilot, a named person's time, a date for a decision. Otherwise it is a gift. A small cheque plus "visibility" in exchange for a year of silence is them buying an option with your scarcity. You can sell that. Know the price.

Corporates ask for more information than a financial investor, because they can use it in their own business. Draw the line before the first board pack. What they see as a shareholder. What they never see: another customer's pricing, a programme that competes with them, data a clinical partner did not agree to share. If the line is not written, a well-meaning business-development person will test it just by asking.

They can be warm and still want a different outcome. They may prefer you stay private and supply them. You may need to sell the company, including to their rival, for the technology to matter. Neither of you is wicked for that. The term sheet is where that possible split gets a date. Read it as if the friendship has gone quiet. The people will change jobs. The document will not.

策略投資者帶着標誌、一個可能的客戶，以及一種感覺：這一輪是關係。創辦人把條款放鬆，因為錢感覺友善。友善也許是真的。三年後讀那份紙的人，沒有吃過那頓飯。

投資和合作是兩份合約，即使來自同一次介紹。授權的優先看一看、一個董事席、伸到其他客戶的資訊、賣給他們競爭對手時的否決：那是商業交易，戴着投資的帽子。把這些條款寫成支票是零也說得通。然後問：如果只為了渠道或訂單，你會不會簽。不會的話，就不要因為前面釘了一張支票而簽。

一段你不可以跟他們的競爭對手談話的時間，是資產，而且是你在給。寫長度。寫他們在這段時間必須做的事：一個付費試驗、一個被點名的人的時間、一個決定的日期。否則就是禮物。一張小支票加「曝光」，換你一年沉默，是他們用你的稀缺在買期權。你可以賣。要知道價錢。

企業要的資料比財務投資者多，因為他們用得上。第一份董事會文件之前把線畫好。作為股東他們看到甚麼。永遠看不到甚麼：別的客戶的價格、一個跟他們競爭的項目、臨床夥伴沒同意分享的數據。線沒寫下來，一個善意的業務發展同事問一句，就會試到這條線。

他們可以很溫暖，卻想要一個跟你不同的結果。他們也許希望你繼續私有、供應他們。你也許要把公司賣掉，包括賣給他們的對手，技術才有意義。雙方都不會因此是壞人。條款清單是這種可能的分岔拿到日期的地方。把它讀成友誼已經安靜下來。人會換工作。文件不會。

— 鄭迪珉 Tyler Tik Man Cheng
