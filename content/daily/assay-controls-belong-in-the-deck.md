::: en
Positive and negative controls, replicates, and operator identity turn a figure into evidence. If the slide is too crowded, put controls on the appendix and say they are there.

Serious scientific buyers will ask. Answering from the slide is better than answering from memory.

::: zh
陽性與陰性對照、重複、操作者身分，把圖變成證據。若正頁太擠，把對照放附錄並說明在那裏。

認真的科學買家會問。從投影片回答，好過從記憶回答。
