"We are a platform" shows up in a lot of first meetings. Sometimes it is true: the technology can, in principle, lead to more than one product. It is also a convenient way to avoid naming the product this team will actually make first.

A platform that has not chosen is a set of options. People do not fund a set of options at the same price as a plan.

The science can support several directions. The company can only walk down one of them properly. Choosing is about sequence. Which patient, which formulation, which risk you are willing to stare at for two years. If the answer is "all of them, the platform is the value", ask who is spending Tuesday on which one. Three programmes and one team is three programmes done slowly. Say the order.

The word earns its keep when the second product is cheaper or faster because the first one existed. A method. A particle. An assay you no longer have to invent. If the second product starts from a blank page, you may still have a good technology. You have a product and a hope. Those can be funded. Don't describe them as the same thing.

Write down one application you are not doing this round, and why. Killing something on paper is what makes the remaining choice believable. Six market logos and no sentence about which one you refused is a menu.

You can keep the wider IP, and mention the later shots in an appendix with whatever evidence you actually have, which may be thin. What you should not do is hover above all of them so that no single failure counts. Choose. Let the platform be the reason the next choice is better, not the reason this one can wait.

::: zh
「我們是一個平台」在第一次會議裡很常見。有時是真的：技術原則上不止一條產品。它也很好用，用來避開「這班人先做哪一個」。

還沒選的平台是一組期權。人家不會用計劃的價錢買一組期權。

科學可以支持幾個方向。公司只能好好走一條。選擇是次序。哪個病人、哪種製劑、未來兩年你願意盯着哪個風險。如果答案是「全部，價值在平台」，就問星期二誰在做哪一個。三個項目、一個團隊，就是三個做得慢的項目。把次序說出來。

第二個產品因為第一個而更便宜或更快，這個詞才配。一個方法、一顆粒子、一個不用再發明的檢測。如果第二個要從白紙開始，你也許仍有好技術。你有一個產品和一個希望。這些可以被投。不要把它們說成同一件。

寫下這一輪不做的一個應用，以及為甚麼。在紙上殺掉一件，剩下的選擇才可信。六個市場標誌，卻沒有一句你拒絕了哪一個，那是菜單。

比較廣的知識產權可以留。後面的機會放附錄，附上你真正有的證據，也許很薄。不要讓故事懸在所有機會上面，以致沒有一次失敗算數。先選。平台是下一個選擇會更好的理由，不是這一次可以再等的理由。
