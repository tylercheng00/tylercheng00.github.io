::: en
After a first meeting, send what you promised within the week — even if it is smaller than you hoped. A corrected cap table page, a counsel note, a customer sentence. The second meeting should open on that progress.

If you need more time, say the date. Silence plus the same slides is how processes die politely.

::: zh
第一次會議之後，一週內寄出你答應的東西——即使比你希望的小。更正的股權表頁、律師說明、客戶那句話。第二次會議應從那項進度打開。

若需要更多時間，說出日期。沉默加上同一份投影片，是流程客氣地死去的方式。
