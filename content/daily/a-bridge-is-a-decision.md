::: en
"We are just doing a bridge" is said in a tone that means nothing important is happening. A small instrument, existing investors, money in quickly, the real round later. Sometimes that is exactly right. Often the bridge is where the company quietly resets its promises without writing down the new ones.

A bridge is a decision. It deserves the same sentence a larger round would: what risk dies before this money runs out?

### Name the other side of the bridge

A bridge needs a far bank. Not a season. A fact. The audit signed. The batch released. The customer renewed. The structure a new investor can actually buy. If you cannot name the fact, you do not have a bridge. You have a smaller round that will be followed by another smaller round, and the story you tell in six months will be about resilience rather than about a milestone. Resilience is real. It is a weak reason to have taken dilution.

Write the far bank in the document. "This note is meant to get the company to X." If X is missing, stop and choose it before you choose the discount.

### Existing investors are not a free audience

The people who already own the company can move quickly, which is the point. They can also say yes because saying no is socially expensive, and then resent the terms later. Do not use speed as a substitute for a term sheet they have actually read. A short document, a clear cap or discount, a clear maturity, and a clear statement of what happens if the next round does not come. The last one is the adult sentence. Everyone hopes it will not matter. The document is for the world in which it does.

### Do not spend bridge money like round money

Bridge money is for the path to the named fact, not for the plan you would have funded if the larger round had arrived. Hiring the full commercial team on a bridge, because "we will raise into it", is how bridges become the business. Cut the plan to the milestone. If the milestone does not need a hire, do not make the hire to feel like the company is still expanding.

### Tell the new investors the truth about why

When you later raise the round you wanted, the new investor will ask why there was a bridge. "Timing" is not an answer. The answer is the fact you were buying time to create, and whether you created it. If you did, the bridge becomes a boring footnote, which is the best outcome available. If you did not, say what you learned and what this new money retires instead. A bridge you pretend did not happen is a gap the diligence list will find in an afternoon.

「我們只是做一個過橋」說出來的語氣，像是沒有甚麼要緊的事發生。一份小工具、現有的投資者、錢快點進來、真正的一輪在後面。有時這完全正確。常常，過橋是公司悄悄重設承諾、卻沒有把新承諾寫下來的地方。

過橋是一個決定。它值得跟較大的一輪用同一句話：這筆錢用完之前，哪個風險會死？

### 說出橋的另一邊

過橋需要對岸。不是一個季節。是一個事實。審計簽了。批次放行了。客戶續約了。架構變成新投資者真正買得到的東西。如果你說不出那個事實，你沒有一座橋。你有一輪較小的融資，後面跟着另一輪較小的融資，而六個月後你講的故事會是關於韌性，而不是關於一個里程碑。韌性是真的。它是接受攤薄的一個弱理由。

把對岸寫進文件。「這張票據是為了讓公司到達 X。」如果 X 不在，先停下來選它，再選折扣。

### 現有投資者不是免費的觀眾

已經擁有公司的人可以很快，這正是意義所在。他們也可以因為說不的社交代價很高而說好，然後在條款上事後不滿。不要用速度代替一份他們真正讀過的條款清單。一份短文件、清楚的上限或折扣、清楚的到期、以及清楚寫明如果下一輪不來會怎樣。最後一句是成年人的句子。人人希望它不要緊。文件是為它要緊的那個世界而寫的。

### 不要把過橋的錢花得像一輪正式融資

過橋的錢是為了走向那個被點名的事實，不是為了那個「如果大的一輪到了你就會做」的計劃。在過橋上聘滿整個商業團隊，因為「我們會融進這筆開支」，是過橋變成業務本身的方式。把計劃砍到里程碑。如果里程碑不需要一個招聘，不要為了感覺公司仍在擴張而招聘。

### 告訴新投資者為甚麼會有過橋

當你後來融你真正想要的那一輪，新投資者會問為甚麼有過橋。「時機」不是答案。答案是你用時間去製造的那個事實，以及你有沒有把它製造出來。如果有，過橋會變成一個沉悶的腳註，這是能得到的最好結果。如果沒有，說你學到甚麼，以及這筆新錢改為退掉甚麼。一個你假裝沒有發生的過橋，是盡職審查清單一個下午就會找到的缺口。
