"Send me the deck" is not a yes「把簡報發來」不是答應

Often it is a polite way out of a meeting that has not earned a second one. Ask what would.很多時候這是禮貌地結束一場還沒有贏得下一次的會。問清楚怎樣才會有下一次。

US capital markets美國資本市場9 Nov 20262026年11月9日3 min read閱讀約 3 分鐘

"Send me the deck" feels like progress. A lot of the time it is a way to leave the room without saying no. I don't put it in a pipeline. I treat it as a question I haven't answered yet.

Before you attach the file, ask one thing. If the deck is fine, what happens next, and what would have to be true? A serious person will tell you. The unit economics. An IP opinion. A customer who will take a call. The other answer is "I'll have a look and revert." Both are useful. Only one of them is a next step.

Then send that thing, and the deck if they still want it. A note aimed at the objection they actually named beats sixteen pages sent into silence.

A week later, "just checking you got it" adds nothing. If you have nothing new, wait. If you have a number, a signed contract, a date, send four lines. Don't apologise for existing. The replies go to people who make the reply easy: a yes or a no, a time, a page.

Sometimes the request is genuine and the reader is on a plane, item seven in someone else's Monday meeting. The first two pages have to stand alone. What you do, for whom, why now, what the round is for, what you want from them. If those pages only work while you are presenting, they are speaker notes. They will not survive an inbox.

Keep a line somewhere: who, when, what they asked, what you sent, what they said would happen next. Six weeks later you will not remember, and you will either chase the wrong person or forget the one who asked a real question. A conversation is live when they owe you a specific answer. The rest is a wish with a logo on it.

「把簡報發來」聽起來像有進展。很多時候，這是不想在房間裡說不的離開方式。我不把它放進管道。我把它當成一個我還沒答上的問題。

附檔之前先問一件事。簡報沒問題的話，下一步是甚麼，甚麼必須是真的？認真的人會講。單位經濟、知識產權意見、一個願意接電話的客戶。另一種答案是「我看看再回」。兩個都有用。只有一個是下一步。

然後就送那一件。他們還要簡報，再附上。對準他們親口講的疑慮，好過十六頁掉進沉默。

一星期後「確認你收到沒有」，沒有增加任何資訊。沒有新東西就等。有數字、有簽了的合約、有日期，四行送出。不要為自己的存在道歉。拿得到回覆的，通常是讓人容易回的：是或否、一個時間、某一頁。

有時要求是真的。對方在飛機上，或者是別人星期一會議的第七項。頭兩頁要自己站得住。做甚麼、為誰、為甚麼現在、這一輪做甚麼、你要他們做甚麼。這兩頁只有你在講才說得通，那是講者備註。它過不了收件箱。

找個地方留一行：誰、何時、問了甚麼、你送了甚麼、他們說下一步是甚麼。六星期後你不會記得，然後去煩錯的人，或忘掉那個問了真問題的人。只有對方欠你一個具體回答，對話才算活着。其餘的是一個貼了標誌的願望。
