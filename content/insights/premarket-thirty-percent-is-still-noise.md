Within minutes of the 23 November reinstatement news, Nvidia’s premarket trading showed gains on the order of thirty percent. Taiwan futures later erupted. Chat groups will demand that you “reprice tonight.” Do not. Premarket explosions after governance reversals are liquidity and narrative. They are not a quality-of-earnings review of your company.

Send the same three-number frame you should have used during the rout: public tape, your dated process range, and sensitivity if AI multiples compress again. Consistency across down days and up days is the whole point. If you only reprice on green candles, partners will assume you also panic on red ones.

Bankers may want a refreshed board pack before the U.S. open. Offer a market-observation page that timestamps the Huang news and freezes your ask. Freezing is a strength when the tape is hysterical in either direction.

Inside the company, ban screenshot-driven decision making for twelve hours. Let counsel and finance sleep on any request to change SAFE caps or option board valuations solely because Nvidia’s premarket went vertical. Sleep is a control.

Tomorrow’s cash market will tell a longer story. Your process letter should already tell yours. Keep them in separate folders.

Keep the day hook in the first paragraph and the operating proof in the rest. Investors forgive topical openings; they do not forgive empty middles. Add dates, owners, and artefacts that can survive a partner forward. If a control does not yet exist, name the date it will. If a risk cannot be closed this week, name the mitigation already funded in cash runway weeks. Write as if the next associate will paste your note into an investment committee chat without you in the room.

黃仁勳11月23日復職消息傳出後數分鐘，輝達盤前出現約三成量級的漲幅，台股期貨隨後爆發。對話群會逼你「今晚重定價」。不要。治理逆轉後的盤前爆炸是流動性與敘事，不是對你公司的盈餘品質檢視。

寄出股災時就該使用的同一套三數字框架：公開行情、你帶日期的流程區間、人工智慧倍數若再壓縮的敏感度。下跌日與上漲日保持一致，才是重點。若只在綠K重定價，合夥人會假設你在紅K也會恐慌。

銀行家也許想在美股開盤前刷新董事會包。給一頁註明黃仁勳新聞時間戳、並凍結要價的市場觀察。行情無論朝哪邊歇斯底里，凍結都是力量。

公司內部十二小時禁止截圖驅動決策。任何只因輝達盤前垂直上漲就要求改SAFE上限或期權估值的請求，讓律師與財務睡一覺再談。睡眠也是一種控制。

明天的現貨市場會講更長的故事；你的流程信應早已講完你的故事。請分開放在不同資料夾。
