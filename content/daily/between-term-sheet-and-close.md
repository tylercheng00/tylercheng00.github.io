::: en
A signed term sheet is a good day. People take a photograph. Then three weeks pass and the money is not there, and nobody can quite say which item is blocking it. The excitement was real. The closing list was not.

I treat the term sheet as permission to start the hard part, not as evidence that the hard part is over.

### Write the closing list on day one

Not a slogan. A list. Each row is a document or a consent, with a name next to it and a date. Board approvals. Shareholder waivers. Employment IP assignments that were never signed. A bank account that can actually receive the funds. The amended articles. The disclosure letter.

If a row has no name, it is not a row yet. "The lawyers" is not a name. A person who will chase that item on Tuesday is a name.

The list will be wrong in places. That is fine. A wrong list can be corrected. A round with no list is corrected by panic, one email at a time.

### Conditions are not a formality

Every condition in the term sheet is a place the deal can stop. "Subject to diligence" means someone is still allowed to walk away. Read the conditions as if you wanted to get out of the deal, and ask which ones you could use. Then go and close those gaps before the other side finds them. The point is not to be clever. The point is to see your own round the way a cautious investor sees it.

### One timeline, shared

The company, company counsel, investor counsel and the accountant are often running four calendars. I want one. A short call twice a week, with the list on the screen, beats a long email chain in which everyone is waiting for someone else. On that call the only question that matters is: what moved since last time, and what is the next date you will commit to?

If an item has not moved, say so in the first minute. Hope is not a status.

### Do not renegotiate with silence

When a new fact appears in diligence, deal with it in the open. A changed number, a contract you forgot, a lawsuit you thought was small. Send it with a proposed response. Waiting, in the hope it will not matter, is how trust dies between signing and closing. Most facts are survivable. A fact that arrives late, from the other side's lawyer, is a different fact.

The round is not closed when the term sheet is signed. It is closed when the last condition is actually met, and the wire leaves. Everything between those two moments is the job.

::: zh
簽下條款清單是美好的一天。大家拍照。然後三個星期過去，錢還沒有到，沒有人說得清是哪一項擋住了。興奮是真的。成交清單不是。

我把條款清單看成開始做難事的許可，不是難事已經結束的證據。

### 第一天就寫下成交清單

不是口號，是一張表。每一行是一份文件或一項同意，旁邊有名字和日期。董事會批准。股東放棄權利。從未簽過的僱傭知識產權轉讓。一個真正收得到錢的銀行賬戶。修訂後的章程。披露函。

一行沒有名字，就還不是一行。「律師」不是名字。星期二會去追那一項的那個人，才是名字。

這張表有些地方會是錯的。沒關係。錯的表可以改。沒有表的一輪融資，只能靠驚慌來改，一封電郵一封電郵地改。

### 先決條件不是例行公事

條款清單裏的每一項條件，都是交易可以停下來的地方。「以盡職審查為準」的意思是，仍有人可以走開。把條件讀成你想退出這宗交易，問自己哪幾項用得上。然後在對方發現之前，把那些缺口補上。目的不是耍聰明。目的是用謹慎的投資者的眼睛，看自己這一輪。

### 一張共用的時間表

公司、公司律師、投資者律師和會計師，常常各走各的日曆。我要的是一張。每星期兩次短會，把清單投在螢幕上，好過一條長長的電郵，人人都在等另一個人。那個會上唯一要緊的問題是：上次之後甚麼動了，你下一個肯承諾的日期是哪一天？

如果一項沒有動，第一分鐘就說。希望不是進度。

### 不要用沉默重新談判

盡職審查出現新事實，就公開處理。一個改了的數字、一份你忘了的合約、一宗你以為很小的訴訟。連同你建議的處理方法一起送出。乾等，希望它不要緊，是信任在簽字和成交之間死去的方式。大部分事實都承受得住。一個遲來的、由對方律師發出來的事實，是另一種事實。

條款清單簽了，這一輪還沒有結束。最後一項條件真正滿足、匯款離開，才算結束。這兩個時刻之間的一切，就是工作。
