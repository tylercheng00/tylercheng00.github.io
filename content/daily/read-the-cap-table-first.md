::: en
When a deck and a cap table disagree, believe the cap table. The deck is an argument. The cap table is a claim about the legal world, and someone, eventually, will have to sign next to it.

I now read it before the narrative. Not because the story does not matter. Because the story is about a company, and the table says which company.

### Fully diluted means fully

A cap table that leaves out the option pool, the notes that convert, or the warrants "because they are small" is not a simplification. It is a different company. Small instruments add up, and they add up at the moment that matters, which is the moment new money comes in.

Ask for the table on a fully diluted basis, and ask for the list of everything that can become a share. Then ask which of those items the holders have actually signed. A promise in a WeChat message is not a row. It may still be a problem.

### Who has to agree

The interesting column is not always the percentage. It is the consent right. A holder of eight percent who must approve any new round, any sale, or any budget over a number, is not an eight percent holder in any practical sense. They are a gate.

Founders sometimes describe this as loyalty: "they have always supported us." Support that is required is not the same as support that is chosen. Name the gates. If there are too many, the round is not a conversation with investors. It is a negotiation with your own history, and the new investor can feel that.

### Pools and promises

An option pool that is "to be created" at the round is a dilution event with a friendly name. So is a pool that was granted in conversations and never minuted. Put both on the table before you discuss price. Price discussed on the wrong share count is not a price. It is a misunderstanding you will relive at the lawyers.

I would rather argue about the pool in week one than discover it in the disclosure schedule.

### Make one person able to explain every row

Not the lawyer. A founder. For each row: who this is, why they have it, what they can block, and whether the paperwork exists. If the founder cannot do that, the company does not know its own ownership, and no amount of design on the cover slide repairs it.

The deck can wait half an hour. The table cannot.

簡報和股權表不一致時，相信股權表。簡報是一個論點。股權表是對法律世界的主張，最終會有人在旁邊簽字。

我現在先讀表，再讀故事。不是因為故事不要緊。是因為故事講的是一家公司，而表說明是哪一家。

### 完全攤薄就是完全

一張漏掉購股權池、會轉換的票據，或者「因為很小」而省略的認股權證的股權表，不是簡化。它是另一家公司。小的工具會加起來，而且在要緊的那一刻加起來，就是新錢進來的那一刻。

要一張完全攤薄的表，並列出所有可以變成股份的東西。然後問：這些項目的持有人是否真的簽了字。微信裏的一句承諾不是一行。它仍然可能是一個問題。

### 誰必須同意

有趣的那一欄不一定是百分比。是同意權。持有百分之八、但任何新一輪、任何出售、或者超過某個數的預算都必須他批准的人，實際上不是一個百分之八的持有人。他是一扇門。

創辦人有時把這說成忠誠：「他們一直支持我們。」必須的支持，和選擇的支持，不是同一件事。把這些門寫出來。如果太多，這一輪就不是跟投資者的對話。它是跟你自己的歷史談判，新投資者感覺得到。

### 期權池和口頭承諾

一輪融資時「即將設立」的購股權池，是一件有着友善名字的攤薄。對話裏承諾過、卻從未寫進會議記錄的池，也是。討論價格之前，先把兩者放上桌。在錯誤的股數上討論的價格，不是價格。它是一個你會在律師那裏重溫的誤會。

我寧願在第一週爭論這個池，而不是在披露附表裏才發現它。

### 每一行都要有一個創辦人說得清

不是律師。是一位創辦人。每一行：這是誰、為甚麼有、可以擋住甚麼、文件在不在。如果創辦人做不到，公司就不知道自己是誰的，封面設計再好也補不了。

簡報可以等半小時。這張表不能。
