Decide who speaks before the meeting starts會議開始前，先決定誰說話

Ten minutes in, the lawyer is answering a commercial question and the founder is guessing at the accounting. Nothing is on fire. Everything is slightly worse.十分鐘後，律師在答商業問題，創辦人在猜會計處理。沒有着火。每件事都稍微差一點。

US capital markets美國資本市場11 Nov 20262026年11月11日3 min read閱讀約 3 分鐘

A meeting about a financing or a US listing has too many capable people in it, and all of them want to be useful. The fix is dull. Before you walk in, decide who owns which sentence.

The company owns the facts. What you sell, to whom, what happened last year, what the money is for, what you will not do. Those should come from a founder or the finance lead, in their own words. If they can't, the meeting is early. More advisors will not make it late enough. An advisor can rehearse. An advisor should not perform the company's memory.

Counsel should speak when the question is what the paper says, what it doesn't, and what it would take to change it. They should not be the ones deciding out loud whether a price is acceptable. If they get pulled into that, pull them back, in the room, politely. Investors notice who is allowed to bind the company. If the answer looks like "whoever spoke last", the next draft gets more cautious.

If a number will be audited, the person who will sign should describe its limits. A smooth figure from someone who will not sign it is how a meeting ends with false comfort.

The advisor owns the process. Timing, who has been seen, what is blocked, what the next document is. The moment they start inventing the science or the five-year market, they are spending credibility they don't have. I would rather they say "that one is for the founder" than fill a silence with a plausible paragraph. A silence can be survived. A paragraph the founder then contradicts cannot.

Someone has to be allowed to interrupt. Usually the founder. Their job is not to talk the most. It is to hand the next question to the right mouth, and to stop a small factual argument eating the half hour you needed for the decision.

Four lines, read in the taxi. It feels formal. It is cheaper than repairing a meeting in which four people answered one question four ways.

融資或赴美上市的會，能幹的人太多，而且都想有用。修理的方法很悶。進門之前，決定哪句話屬於誰。

事實屬於公司。賣甚麼、賣給誰、去年發生了甚麼、錢做甚麼、不會做甚麼。這些該由創辦人或財務負責人用自己的話講。他們講不了，會就太早。再多顧問也不會把它變得夠晚。顧問可以排練。顧問不該表演公司的記憶。

問題是文件寫了甚麼、沒寫甚麼、要改它需要甚麼，律師才說話。他們不該當眾決定價格可不可接受。被拉進去，就在房間裡禮貌地拉回來。投資者在看誰可以拘束這家公司。如果看起來是「最後說話的那位」，下一稿會更謹慎。

一個數字會被審計，描述它的界限的人應該是將要簽名的那位。一個不會簽的人送出的光滑數字，會讓會議以假的安慰結束。

顧問管流程。時間、見過誰、卡在哪、下一份文件是甚麼。一旦他們開始發明科學或五年市場，就是在花自己沒有的信用。我寧願他們說「這句是創辦人的」，不要用一段聽起來很像的話填沉默。沉默可以過。一段被創辦人當場否定的話，過不了。

要有人可以打斷。通常是創辦人。工作不是講得最多。是把下一個問題交給對的嘴，並阻止一個小的事實爭議吃掉你用來做決定的那半小時。

四行，的士上讀一次。感覺很正式。它比事後修補一場四個人用四種方式回答同一個問題的會便宜。
