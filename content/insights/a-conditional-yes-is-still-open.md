::: en
Investors often say yes with a hinge. Yes if the numbers hold. Yes if counsel is comfortable. Yes after the next customer reference. Founders hear the yes. The room should hear the if.

A conditional yes is useful. It tells you what would make capital move. It is also a trap if you mark the name as done. The condition is now your job. Put an owner on it. Put a date. Put the evidence you will send. If you cannot name those three, you do not have a path. You have a compliment.

I keep conditional yeses in the same list as open diligence requests. Same column. Same follow-up rhythm. The psychology is different for the founder — it feels warmer than a no — but the work is the same. Someone still has to produce a file, a call, or a clean answer.

When the condition clears, confirm in writing that the yes still stands. People change seats. Memories soften. A week of silence after you send the audit comfort letter is not a close. It is another open item.

Celebrate when money moves. Until then, treat every "yes, if" as unfinished work with a name on it.

::: zh
投資者常帶着鉸鏈說 yes。數字站得住就 yes。律師覺得可以就 yes。下一個客戶推薦之後再 yes。創辦人聽到 yes。房間該聽到 if。

有條件的 yes 有用。它告訴你甚麼會讓資金動起來。若你把這個名字標成完成，它也是陷阱。條件現在是你的工作。放負責人。放日期。放你會送出的證據。三樣說不出來，你就沒有路徑，只有一句恭維。

我把有條件的 yes 和未完成的盡職審查要求放在同一份清單。同一欄。同一套跟進節奏。對創辦人來說心理不同——比 no 溫暖——但工作一樣。仍然要有人交出檔案、電話，或一個乾淨的回答。

條件清掉之後，書面確認那個 yes 仍然成立。座位會換人。記憶會變軟。你寄出審計 comfort letter 之後一週沉默，不是交割。是另一個未完成項。

錢動了再慶祝。在那之前，把每一個「yes, if」當成掛着名字的未完成工作。
