::: en
From Hong Kong this Friday, the calendar is already tilting toward Shenzhen. APEC leaders week is not only a diplomatic diary item. It is a financing atmosphere item. US readers who work China-linked private companies will open next week’s calls with summit framing whether you put APEC on your slide or not. Anticipation itself moves behaviour: bankers ask for cleaner China pages earlier, funds ask whether you will be travelling or reachable, and boards ask whether any announcement window should wait until after the cameras leave.

Treat anticipation as a diligence accelerator, not as a marketing theme. You do not need a summit logo on the deck. You need the artefacts people will request once summit coverage thickens: where the company operates in China, which entity holds cash and IP, which licences renew on which dates, and how a US process would describe those facts in risk-factor English. The founders who scramble those pages during leaders week lose days they could have spent on customers. The founders who finish them while anticipation is still rising walk into the loud week with a quieter folder.

Update travel and authority maps for the week ahead. Who can sign what if the CEO is in Shenzhen for side meetings. Who answers a US counsel email at 9 p.m. Hong Kong time. Who has the latest batch record if a biotech diligence thread opens mid-summit. These sound operational because they are. Summit weeks produce uneven attention and uneven bandwidth. Uneven bandwidth is how small diligence gaps become large stories. Write a one-page coverage roster and send it to the board before Monday.

Watch the interaction between summit anticipation and this week’s public market tape. Indices can rise while China ADRs and Nvidia print stress. That combination trains US desks to separate “global risk on” from “China tech and policy risk.” Your meeting may contain both moods. Do not argue with either mood. Hand over the map. If your company sells into China growth stories that APEC coverage will amplify, keep the growth evidence in customer metrics and contracts. Keep listing and financing readiness in process documents. Mixing them into one patriotic paragraph invites a political reading you cannot control.

Biotech and deep-tech teams should pre-answer supply-chain questions that summit-week politics will revive. What reagents, instruments, or compute you source across borders. What stays domestic. What would slow if export-control talk intensifies around chips and AI even when your product is a therapy or a device. You are not predicting legislation. You are showing you have already sat with the dependency. Anticipation of APEC makes that sitting look timely rather than defensive.

Capital raisers should decide now what not to announce during leaders week. Soft circles, half-agreed valuations, and “we are talking to someone in Shenzhen” updates travel badly when every journalist and every LP is already saturated with geopolitics. Prefer closed facts: signed term sheet, cleared counsel memo, finished stability pull. If you only have atmosphere, keep it off the investor WhatsApp. Atmosphere is free this month. Credibility is not.

Use the weekend to run a short rehearsal. Give a colleague five minutes to play a US partner who just read APEC preview pieces and glanced at China ADR screens. Have them ask only process questions. Record which answers required a hunt through email. Those hunts become your homework list. Summit anticipation is useful precisely because it is still slightly ahead of the loudest coverage. Finish the homework while the feed is only warming up. By the time Shenzhen is everywhere, you want the company to sound prepared, not topical.

這個星期五從香港看，日曆已在向深圳傾斜。APEC 領袖週不只是外交記事，也是融資氣氛記事。做中國相關私人公司的美國讀者，下週開電話時會帶峰會框架，無論你有沒有把 APEC 放上投影片。預期本身就會改行為：銀行家更早要更乾淨的中國頁、基金問你會否出行或能否聯絡、董事會問有沒有公告窗口該等鏡頭離開再開。

把預期當盡職加速器，不要當行銷主題。簡報不需要峰會標誌。你需要的是峰會報道變厚時人們會要的工件：公司在中國何處營運、哪個主體持有現金與知識產權、哪些牌照何日續期、美國流程會如何用風險因素英語描述這些事實。在領袖週期間才趕這些頁的創辦人，會失去本可花在客戶上的日子。預期仍在上升時就做完的人，走進嘈雜的一週時，資料夾較安靜。

為未來一週更新出行與授權地圖。若行政總裁在深圳參加邊會，誰能簽甚麼。香港時間晚上九時誰回美國律師電郵。若生技盡職線在峰會中段打開，誰有最新批次記錄。這些聽起來很營運，因為本來就是。峰會週製造不均勻的注意力與不均勻的帶寬。不均勻帶寬，會讓小的盡職缺口變成大故事。寫一頁值班名單，星期一前送給董事會。

留意峰會預期與本週公開行情的互動。指數可以上升，中概股與英偉達卻印出壓力。這種組合訓練美國交易枱把「全球風險偏好尚可」與「中國科技與政策風險」分開。你的會議可能兩種情緒都有。不要跟任何一種情緒辯論。把地圖交出去。若公司賣進 APEC 報道會放大的中國增長故事，把增長證據留在客戶指標與合約；把上市與融資就緒留在流程文件。混成一段愛國段落，會招來你控制不了的政治解讀。

生技與深科技團隊應預先回答峰會週政治會重新喚醒的供應鏈問題。你跨境採購哪些試劑、儀器或算力。甚麼留在境內。即使產品是療法或器械，圍繞芯片與人工智能的出口管制討論升溫時，甚麼會變慢。你不是在預測立法，你是在證明已與依賴坐在一起。對 APEC 的預期，讓這種「坐過」看起來是合時，而不是防守。

融資的人現在就決定領袖週不公告甚麼。軟性意向、半談妥的估值、「我們在深圳跟人談」這類更新，在每個記者與每個 LP 已被地緣政治灌飽時，傳播得很差。寧可要已關閉的事實：簽了的條款清單、過目的律師備忘、做完的穩定性取樣。若只有氣氛，就別放上投資者通訊軟件。這個月氣氛免費，信用不是。

用週末做一場短彩排。給同事五分鐘，扮演剛讀完 APEC 預告、又瞥過中概螢幕的美國合夥人。只准問流程問題。記下哪些答案要翻電郵才找得到。那些翻找就是你的功課清單。峰會預期之所以有用，正因為它仍略早於最響的報道。趁信息流還在預熱，做完功課。等深圳無處不在時，你要公司聽起來是準備好了，而不是趕話題。
