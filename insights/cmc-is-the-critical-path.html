CMC is where the calendar actually breaks時間表真正斷掉的地方，是 CMC

The science milestone is in large type. The batch slips, and every date that needed material slips with it. That was the plan, showing itself.科學里程碑用大字。批次一延，所有等材料的日期一起延。這就是計劃本來的樣子。

Biotech生物科技9 Nov 20262026年11月9日4 min read閱讀約 4 分鐘

In a lot of biotech plans, manufacturing sits near the bottom, assigned to "CMC" as if that were a vendor. CMC is the work of being able to make the thing on purpose, again, and to show what it is. For plenty of young companies it is the critical path, whether the Gantt chart says so or not.

You cannot start the study, or the partner's test, on a batch you don't have. "It runs in parallel" is only true if a batch you trust already exists. If the batch is the thing you are still trying to achieve, it is first. Draw it that way even if the plan looks less ambitious. A plan that assumes material will appear is a wish with dates on it.

A specification is a decision about what you are willing to call the product. Too wide, and a drifting batch teaches you nothing. Too tight, and you fail batches for differences that don't matter. Someone has to own that choice early and write down why. Changing it later is allowed. Changing it quietly, so a failing batch becomes a passing one, is how the data becomes unusable.

A batch with no record of how it was made is a sample. Who did what, with which lot, against which instruction, and what was off. Early teams hate this because it slows a Tuesday. It is also why a Thursday result can be believed by someone who wasn't there. Start the habit before the batch is precious. The precious one is the one you will most regret not having written down.

When a timeline says the study begins in March, ask when the material has to be released for that to be true, and what "released" means in this company. If nobody can answer, March is decoration. Put the release date on the same page as the scientific milestone, in the same size type.

很多生物科技計劃把生產放在靠近底部的地方，交給「CMC」，好像那是一個供應商。CMC 是這件事：你能夠有意地再做出那件東西，並講得出它是甚麼。對不少年輕公司，它就是關鍵路徑，甘特圖寫不寫都一樣。

你沒有的那一批，研究開不了，夥伴的測試也開不了。「平行進行」只有在你已經有一批信得過的材料時才成立。如果那一批正是你還在做的東西，它就是第一。照這樣畫，即使計劃看起來沒那麼大。假設材料會出現的計劃，是一個帶日期的願望。

質量標準是一個決定：你願意把甚麼叫做這個產品。太寬，批次漂了你甚麼也學不到。太緊，不要緊的差異也判不合格。要有人早點做這個選擇，並寫下原因。以後改可以。悄悄改，讓不合格變成合格，數據就用不了。

沒有記錄的一批，是樣品。誰做的、哪一批料、照哪份指示、哪裡不對。早期團隊討厭這個，因為星期二會變慢。它也是一個不在場的人能夠相信星期四那個結果的原因。在批次還不珍貴的時候養成習慣。珍貴的那批，是你最後悔沒寫下來的。

時間表寫研究三月開始，就問材料要何時放行這句話才為真，而這家公司「放行」是甚麼意思。沒有人答得出，三月就是裝飾。放行日期和科學里程碑放同一頁，字一樣大。
