::: en
Receiving a process letter feels like arrival. It is entry. The hard questions start after. Keep the data room current. Keep answers consistent across buyers.

Companies that relax after the letter often rework the same files under time pressure. Stay dull and ready.

::: zh
收到流程函感覺像到達。其實是入場。難題在之後。保持資料室最新。保持對不同買家的回答一致。

拿到函就鬆懈的公司，常在時間壓力下重做同一批檔案。保持無聊，保持就緒。
