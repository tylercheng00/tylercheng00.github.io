::: en
A larger animal. A harder model. A head-to-head against the drug already on the market. Those experiments have a place. They are often booked before the company can interpret them, because whatever comes back will not mean what the slide needs it to mean.

Before you book one, write two short plans. If it works, next month looks like this. If it fails, next month looks like this. If the two plans are the same, "we will optimise and repeat", the experiment is an activity. It can be interesting and still be the wrong use of the last batch, or of the last quarter of money. It earns the slot when at least one outcome would change what you spend on.

Often the glamorous study is waiting on something dull. An assay that measures what you think it measures. A batch that matches the last batch. A dose you can explain. Running the big study on an assay you don't trust does not make you bold. It makes a result you will spend the next month defending, and then quietly set aside. Do the dull work first, and give it its own name in the plan, so nobody experiences it as a delay to the real science. It is the real science.

A collaborator has a free window. A reagent is about to expire. A student is available. Sometimes you should take the window. More often you should let the reagent expire. A free experiment is not free if it uses up the only people who understand the method and returns a dataset you now have to explain forever.

The experiments you postpone will be asked about, by someone who saw them on an old slide. Keep a line. We didn't do this, because this prior fact was missing, and we will look again when that fact exists. That line is more professional than a study that happened because nobody wanted to say no to a dramatic figure.

更大的動物。更難的模型。跟已經在賣的藥正面比。這些實驗有它們的位置。它們常常在公司還讀不懂結果的時候就被排上，因為無論回來的是甚麼，都不會是投影片需要的那個意思。

排期之前寫兩個短計劃。成功的話下個月這樣。失敗的話下個月這樣。如果兩個計劃一樣，都是「優化然後再做」，這個實驗是一項活動。它可以有趣，卻仍然是最後一批材料、或最後一季錢的錯誤用法。至少有一個結果會改變你把錢花在哪，它才配佔位置。

漂亮的研究常常在等一件悶的事。一個真的量到你以為它在量的東西的檢測。一批跟上一批相符的生產。一個你解釋得了的劑量。在一個你不信的檢測上做大實驗，不會讓你大膽。它讓你下個月忙着辯護，然後悄悄放下。先做悶的，並在計劃裡用它自己的名字，免得大家把它當成真正科學的延誤。它就是真正的科學。

合作方有一個免費空窗。試劑快過期。學生有空。有時該抓住空窗。更多時候該讓試劑過期。免費的實驗並不免費，如果它用掉唯一懂這個方法的人，並留下一套你以後永遠要解釋的數據。

你押後的實驗，會被一個在舊投影片上看過的人問到。留一行。我們沒做，因為缺了這一個先前的事實，那個事實有了再看。這一行，比一個因為沒人想對一張很戲劇的圖說不而發生的研究，更像做事的人。
