::: en
Founders sometimes walk into a US meeting ready for a debate about politics. The meeting is rarely that. The reader has a narrower job. Which parts of this company depend on rules, people, data and money inside China, and what happens to the shares they would buy if one of those shifts.

If you don't describe it, they will describe it from the last company they saw. You will not like that version.

One page. Where the customers are. Where the people are. Where the bank accounts are. Where the IP is owned. Where the data sits. Which permissions the operating company needs in order to do what the deck says it does. Arrows for the money and arrows for the ownership. They are not the same arrows. A speech about globalisation doesn't answer the page. Neither does a speech about how unfair the question feels. The page is a diagram a new partner at the fund can read without you.

Selling to Chinese customers and employing people in Shenzhen is ordinary operating exposure, the way a German company selling in Germany has. Investors who don't own the operating assets, and rely on contracts to get the economics, are a different kind of exposure. Both can be described calmly. Mixing them into one cloud called China risk makes the ordinary look frightening and the structural look vague. Split them. People can price a split. They discount a cloud.

Name the dependency you actually watch. A licence that renews on a date. A rule about data crossing a border. A state-owned customer whose procurement cycle you don't control. Then say the first move if that dependency disappoints you. Not a fantasy of moving the company next month. Which entity still has cash, which contracts still pay, which people you can still employ. A concrete first move makes the map more believable, even if they still dislike the risk.

Counsel will draft the formal risk factor, and they should. The spoken version has to be the founder's. "Our lawyers are comfortable", from a founder who cannot draw the page, tells the room the founder has not sat with the question. Comfort from counsel is a view about the paper. The meeting is about whether you know what the paper is describing.

創辦人有時帶着準備跟美國人辯論政治的心情進門。會議很少是那樣。對方的工作更窄。這家公司的哪些部分靠中國境內的規則、人、數據和錢；其中一項動了，他們要買的股份會怎樣。

你不描述，他們會用上一家看過的公司來描述。你不會喜歡那個版本。

一頁。客戶在哪。人在哪。銀行戶口在哪。知識產權歸誰。數據坐在哪。營運公司要哪些許可，才做得到簡報說的那件事。錢的箭嘴，和所有權的箭嘴。它們不是同一組。一篇關於全球化的演講回答不了這一頁。一篇關於這問題有多不公平的演講也回答不了。這一頁是基金裡一個新合夥人可以不靠你就讀的圖。

賣給中國客戶、在深圳僱人，是普通的營運暴露，就像一家在德國賣東西的德國公司。投資者不擁有營運資產、要靠合約去拿經濟利益，是另一種暴露。兩種都可以平靜地講。混成一團叫「中國風險」的雲，普通的會看起來可怕，結構性的會看起來含糊。分開。分開的東西可以定價。雲會被打折。

點出你真正在看的那項依賴。一個某日要續的牌照。一條關於數據過境的規則。一個你控制不了採購周期的國有客戶。然後講，這項依賴令你失望的話，第一步做甚麼。不是下個月把公司搬走的幻想。哪個主體還有現金、哪些合約還在付錢、哪些人你還僱得到。具體的第一步會讓這張圖更可信，即使他們仍然不喜歡這個風險。

正式的風險因素由律師起草，也該由他們起草。說出來的版本必須是創辦人的。「我們的律師放心」，但創辦人畫不出這一頁，房間聽到的是：創辦人沒有跟這個問題坐在一起。律師的放心是對紙的看法。會議是你知不知道紙在描述甚麼。
