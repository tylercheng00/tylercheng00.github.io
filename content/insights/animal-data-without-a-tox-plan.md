::: en
A good animal result creates pressure to raise on momentum. Ask what the next regulated step is, who runs it, and when the slot exists. If the deck stops at the exciting figure, the process will stop there too when a serious partner arrives.

Put the tox or safety plan on the same slide family as the result. Momentum is useful. A cliff is not.

::: zh
好的動物結果會製造趁勢融資的壓力。問下一步受規管的工作是甚麼、誰做、檔期何時。若投影片停在激動人心的數字，認真夥伴到來時流程也會停在那裏。

把毒理或安全性計劃和結果放在同一組投影片。動能有用。懸崖沒用。
