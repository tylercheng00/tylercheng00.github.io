White House input is not your risk-factor template白宮意見不是你的風險因素範本

Nvidia cited senior U.S. government input for Huang’s return — private issuers still need company-level risk language, not borrowed political comfort.輝達援引美國政府高層意見支持黃仁勳回歸；私人發行人仍需要公司層級風險表述，而不是借來的政治安心。

US capital markets美國資本市場23 Nov 20262026年11月23日7 min read閱讀約 7 分鐘

Nvidia’s 23 November reinstatement statement made “senior levels of the United States government” part of the governance story. President Trump welcomed the decision in public. Founders watching from private companies may be tempted to draft risk factors that lean on political weather. Resist. Your risk factors must describe your contracts, your geographies, your customers, and your cash. Borrowed political comfort expires on the next post.

If government relations actually matter to your business — licenses, grants, regulated buyers — say so with specificity. Name the agency touchpoints and the renewal dates. Do not write “supportive policy environment” because a CEO you do not employ got a favorable signal. Specificity is diligence; vibes are marketing.

Counsel should review any sentence that mentions administrations, elections, or personal leaders. Those sentences age like milk. Product and compliance sentences age like infrastructure. Prefer infrastructure.

Use the Huang day as a teaching moment internally: we do not outsource our risk narrative to cable news. We update the risk narrative when our orders, licenses, or counterparties change. That rule keeps decks stable when Washington is loud.

Partners will still ask what you think the Huang return means. Answer with your backlog and your controls. Leave the White House to the newspapers. Newspapers are not in your syndicate.

Keep the day hook in the first paragraph and the operating proof in the rest. Investors forgive topical openings; they do not forgive empty middles. Add dates, owners, and artefacts that can survive a partner forward. If a control does not yet exist, name the date it will. If a risk cannot be closed this week, name the mitigation already funded in cash runway weeks. Write as if the next associate will paste your note into an investment committee chat without you in the room.

輝達11月23日的復職聲明，把「美國政府高層」寫進治理故事；特朗普也公開歡迎。在私人公司旁觀的創辦人，也許想寫依賴政治天氣的風險因素。請忍住。風險因素必須描述你的合約、地理、客戶與現金。借來的政治安心，會在下一則貼文過期。

若政府關係真的影響你的生意——許可、補助、受規管買家——就寫得具體。點出機關接觸點與更新日期。不要因為你沒雇用的執行長得到有利訊號，就寫「支持性的政策環境」。具體是盡調；感覺是行銷。

任何提到行政當局、選舉或個人領袖的句子，都應經律師過目。那些句子像牛奶一樣易壞；產品與合規句子像基礎設施一樣耐用。請選基礎設施。

用黃仁勳這天做內部教材：我們不把風險敘事外包給電視新聞。只有訂單、許可或對手方改變時，才更新風險敘事。華府很吵時，這條規則讓簡報穩定。

合夥人仍會問你如何看黃仁勳回歸。用在手訂單與控制措施回答。把白宮留給報紙；報紙不在你的財團裡。
