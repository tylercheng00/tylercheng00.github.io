::: en
In a biotech plan, the science milestone is usually in large type. The manufacturing milestone is a line near the bottom, assigned to "CMC" as if that were a vendor rather than a discipline. Then the batch slips, and every date that depended on having material slips with it. The team experiences this as bad luck. It is the plan, revealing itself.

CMC — chemistry, manufacturing and controls — is the work of being able to make the thing, on purpose, again, and to prove what it is. For a lot of young companies it is the critical path, whether or not the Gantt chart says so.

### Material is a gate, not a supply

You cannot start the study, the partner's evaluation, or the next animal work on a batch you do not have. Treating manufacturing as something that "runs in parallel" is only true if a batch already exists that you trust. If the batch is the thing you are still trying to achieve, it is not parallel. It is first. Draw the plan that way, even if it looks less ambitious. An ambitious plan that assumes material will appear is a wish with dates on it.

### Specifications are decisions

A specification is not paperwork you fill in when a regulator asks. It is a decision about what you are willing to call "the product". Too wide, and you learn nothing when a batch drifts. Too tight, and you fail batches for differences that do not matter. Someone has to own that choice early, and write down why. Changing it later is allowed. Changing it silently, so that a failing batch becomes a passing one, is how data becomes unusable.

### The record is the product, legally speaking

A batch without a record of how it was made is a sample, not a product. Who did what, with which lot of material, against which instruction, and what was out of trend. Early teams hate this because it slows a Tuesday. It is also the only reason a Thursday result can be believed by someone who was not in the room. Build the habit before the batch is precious. The precious batch is the one you will most regret not having written down.

### Ask for the date the material is released, not the date the experiment starts

When a timeline says "study begins in March", ask when the material has to be released for that to be true, and what "released" means in this company. If nobody can answer, March is a decoration. Put the release date on the same page as the scientific milestone, in the same size type. The calendar gets quieter, and more honest, which is the kind of quiet a board can fund.

生物科技的計劃裏，科學里程碑通常用大字。生產里程碑是靠近底部的一行，交給「CMC」，好像那是一個供應商，而不是一門學科。然後批次延誤，所有依賴那批材料的日期一起延誤。團隊把這體驗成運氣不好。這是計劃在露出本來的樣子。

CMC，即化學、生產與控制，是這項工作：你能夠有意地、再次地做出那件東西，並證明它是甚麼。對很多年輕公司來說，它就是關鍵路徑，無論甘特圖有沒有這樣寫。

### 材料是一扇門，不是一項供應

你沒有的那一批，無法用來開始研究、夥伴的評估，或下一次動物實驗。把生產說成「平行進行」，只有在你已經有一批信任的材料時才是真的。如果那一批正是你仍在努力做出的東西，它就不是平行。它是第一。照這樣畫計劃，即使看起來沒那麼有野心。一個假設材料會出現的野心計劃，是一個帶日期的願望。

### 質量標準是決定

質量標準不是監管機構來問時才填的表格。它是一個決定：你願意把甚麼稱為「這個產品」。太寬，批次漂移時你甚麼也學不到。太緊，你會因為不要緊的差異而判批次不合格。必須有人早早擁有這個選擇，並寫下原因。以後改是允許的。靜靜地改，讓一個不合格的批次變成合格，是數據變成不能用的方式。

### 從法律上說，記錄就是產品

沒有生產記錄的一批，是樣品，不是產品。誰做了甚麼、用了哪一批材料、依照哪一份指示、甚麼超出了趨勢。早期團隊討厭這個，因為它拖慢一個星期二。它也是星期四的結果能夠被一個不在場的人相信的唯一理由。在批次還不珍貴的時候養成習慣。珍貴的那一批，是你最後悔沒有寫下來的一批。

### 問材料放行的日期，不要只問實驗開始的日期

時間表寫「研究於三月開始」時，問：要讓這句話為真，材料必須何時放行，而在這家公司「放行」是甚麼意思。如果沒有人答得出，三月就是裝飾。把放行日期和科學里程碑放在同一頁，用同樣大小的字。日曆會安靜下來，也更誠實。這種安靜，是董事會願意撥款的那種。
