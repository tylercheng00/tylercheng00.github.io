::: en
Founders talk about reproducibility as a scientific virtue. An investor who is paying attention hears a different question: if that person leaves the building, does the asset stay.

Could someone hired next month, skilled but new, follow the written method and land in the same range, without a conversation with the inventor? If the honest answer is no, the method is remembered, not written. Memory does not transfer in a financing, and it does not survive an argument about what the method was.

You don't need a novel on day one. You need the steps that actually move the result: the order, where the material came from, the setting that must not drift, the sign that a run should be abandoned.

Pay for someone other than the inventor to run the key experiment before you raise on it. It feels like doing the work twice. It is the moment the result moves from a person to a company. If the second person can't do it, you have learned something cheap, compared with learning it in the middle of diligence, clock running, data room already sent. If it hasn't happened yet, put "transfer the method off the founder" in the use of proceeds. A reader understands the round better from that line than from another line called R&D.

A valuation built on data only one person can repeat is a valuation built on that person. Sometimes that is explicit and fair: the round is to keep them and to fund the transfer. Often the model just assumes the data exist, the way a factory assumes its machines exist. If the key experiments have never been repeated by someone who did not design them, don't talk as if the company has a process. It has a person. People get tired, and they leave, and under pressure they remember selectively.

Make "reproducible" a row someone can tick. Named operator, named version of the method, named range, dated. A witness can be the head of the lab who did not run it, reading the record. The point is that a future investor, or a future you, can see the word was used in a way that had a test.

You are not financing the best day in the lab. You are financing the company's ability to have that day again.

::: zh
創辦人把可重現性說成一種科學上的好習慣。留心的投資者聽到的是另一個問題：那個人離開大樓，資產還在不在。

下個月入職的人，有技術但是新的，能不能照着寫下來的方法，落在同一個範圍，而不用跟發明人談話？誠實的答案是不能的話，方法是被記住的，不是被寫下的。記憶在融資裡不轉移，也活不過一場關於方法究竟是甚麼的吵。

第一天不需要一部小說。你需要那些真正推動結果的步驟：次序、材料從哪來、哪個設定不能漂、甚麼訊號代表這次該放棄。

拿這個結果去融資之前，付錢讓發明人以外的人再做一次關鍵實驗。感覺像做兩遍。它是結果從一個人搬到一家公司的時刻。第二個人做不到，你學到的東西是便宜的，比起在盡職審查中間、時鐘在走、資料室已經送出時才學到。還沒發生，就在資金用途寫「把方法從創辦人身上轉走」。讀者從這一行明白這一輪，好過再看一行叫「研發」。

一個建立在只有一個人能重做的數據上的估值，是建立在那個人身上的估值。有時這是明說的，而且公平：這一輪是為了留下他們，並付錢做轉移。更多時候模型假設數據就在那裡，像工廠假設機器就在那裡。關鍵實驗從未被一個不是設計它們的人做過，就不要說得好像公司有一個製程。它有一個人。人會累，會走，壓力下會選擇性地記得。

「可重現」要是一行可以打勾的。被點名的操作者、方法的版本、接受範圍、日期。見證人可以是沒有親自做的實驗室負責人，讀那份記錄。意義是以後的投資者，或以後的你，看得見這個詞被用在一個有測試的方式上。

你融資的不是實驗室裡最好的那一天。你融資的是公司有辦法再有那一天。
