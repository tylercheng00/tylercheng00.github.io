::: en
Animal data is where a young company first feels it has news. It is more persuasive than the dish. It is also the moment the claim gets bigger than the experiment.

The result can be real and still not be about the patient you are raising money to treat. Every model leaves something out. The disease is induced, or it is a piece of a human tumour in an animal whose immune system was changed so the tumour would grow. That doesn't make the work worthless. It makes "it works in animals" too large. Name the model in ordinary words. Say which part of the human problem it resembles, and which part it can't. If you don't know the second half, you are not ready to use the first half in a fundraise. The investor who knows the disease will fill the gap with a less generous version.

Put three facts next to the figure, not in a backup nobody opens. The dose, next to a human-equivalent dose you are not embarrassed by. The route, which is often more convenient in the animal than it will ever be in a clinic. How many animals, and whether the ones that don't fit the story are still on the plot. One dramatic animal is an anecdote you photographed. If that is what you have, say so, and call it a direction.

After the result, one sentence of this shape: this does not show a patient will benefit, because of this specific gap, and the next study that would narrow it is the following, at this cost. Teams hate that sentence. It feels like arguing against yourself. It is how the result survives a sceptical reader. Over-claim it and the whole thing gets thrown out. Fence it and it is still standing at the end of diligence.

Don't let the prettiest chart pick the indication. Sometimes the figure is nicest because the model was the easiest, not because the need is greatest or the path to a trial is shortest. Ask that question after you have looked at the figure, not after you have fallen in love with it.

::: zh
動物數據是年輕公司第一次覺得自己有新聞的地方。它比培養皿有說服力。也是說法開始大過實驗的時候。

結果可以是真的，卻仍然不是關於你正為之融資的那個病人。每個模型都漏了東西。疾病是誘導的，或者是一塊人的腫瘤，長在免疫被改過、好讓腫瘤長出來的動物身上。這不會讓工作毫無價值。它讓「動物有效」這句太大。用普通的話講模型的名字。它像人的問題的哪一部分，哪一部分它不可能像。後半句不知道，就還沒準備好用前半句去融資。懂這種病的投資者會用一個沒那麼客氣的版本把空位填上。

三個事實放在圖旁邊，不要放在沒人開的備份裡。劑量，旁邊放一個你不會尷尬的人體等價劑量。給藥途徑，動物身上往往比診所方便。多少隻，以及不符合故事的那幾隻還在不在圖上。一隻很戲劇的動物，是你拍下來的一則事。如果那就是你有的，就這樣說，叫它一個方向。

結果之後加一句這種形狀的話：這沒有顯示病人會受益，因為有這個具體缺口；能把缺口收窄的下一個研究是這個，成本是這些。團隊不喜歡這句，覺得像在反對自己。懷疑的讀者來推的時候，結果靠這句留得住。你說太大，整份會被扔掉。圍得好，盡職審查結束時它還在。

不要讓最好看的圖替你選適應症。有時圖好看，是因為模型容易，不是因為需要最大，或走到試驗的路最短。看完圖再問這件事，不要等你已經愛上它。
