::: en
This is just the arithmetic of the route. If you don't design for it, it will run the round.

There is a thin window when both sides are properly awake. Treat it as scarce. Use it for decisions and for questions that need a voice. Don't use it to read a document together that one side could have read alone. Send the document the evening before, with the three questions you actually need answered, written at the top. The call then starts at the decision instead of at "can everyone see the screen".

If someone is on a plane and there is no overlap, say so and move the decision. A tired midnight call produces agreements that fall apart at the lawyers the next day.

Five emails across a Hong Kong afternoon arrive in New York as a pile at breakfast, the latest contradicting the first. Send one bundle, at a time you have named, with a list of what changed. It feels slower to the sender. It is faster for the round, because nothing gets answered twice. I like a cutoff. Anything after 4pm Hong Kong waits for tomorrow's bundle. People hate this until they see a week in which nothing was lost.

Half the readers will see the work without you. Number the questions. Say what you want in the first three lines. Make the filename match the file you mention. Mark what is new, so nobody rereads twenty pages to find one changed cell. That is how you buy a same-day reply from someone whose day is already ending.

Someone should own the clock. Which items are waiting on New York, which on Hong Kong, and which have waited so long that the wait itself is now the issue. A shared list, updated once a day, beats the founder who answers everything at 1am and becomes the bottleneck they were trying to avoid.

The ocean is not going to shrink. The round can still move every business day, if you stop asking one email to cross it twice before anything is decided.

這就是這條路線的算術。你不為它設計，它就會支配這一輪。

雙方都真正醒着的時間很薄。把它當稀缺的。用來做決定，和用來問那些需要聲音的問題。不要用來一起讀一份一方本來可以自己讀的文件。重疊之前的那個晚上把文件送出，你真正要在電話裡回答的三個問題寫在最上面。電話就從決定開始，不是從「看得到螢幕嗎」開始。

有人在飛機上、沒有重疊，就說出來，把決定挪走。一次疲倦的午夜電話，會產生第二天在律師那裡散掉的協議。

香港下午的五封郵件，到紐約是早餐時的一堆，最新的一封否定第一封。在一個說好的時間送一捆，並列出改了甚麼。發送的人覺得更慢。這一輪更快，因為沒有事情被回答兩次。我喜歡一個截止。香港下午四時之後的，等明天那一捆。大家討厭這個，直到看到一個甚麼都沒丟的星期。

一半的讀者會在沒有你的時候看這些東西。問題編號。頭三行寫你要甚麼。檔名和你提到的一致。標出新的部分，免得有人為了一個改過的格子重讀二十頁。這是你向一個白天快結束的人買一個當天回覆的方式。

要有人管時鐘。哪些在等紐約，哪些在等香港，哪些等得太久、久到等待本身成了問題。每天更新一次的共用清單，好過凌晨一時親自回一切、然後變成瓶頸的那個創辦人。

海不會變小。只要你不再要求一封郵件在任何決定之前跨越它兩次，這一輪仍然可以每個工作日往前。
