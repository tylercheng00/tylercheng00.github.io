::: en
Founders answer a diligence list as homework. Find the file, send the file, tick the row. Some rows are homework. The list as a whole is graded on whether the company stays the same when someone pulls a thread.

"All customer contracts over this amount" answered with three selected case studies is not a partial answer. It is a different conversation. If you can't produce the full set, say what you are sending, what is missing, and when the rest exists. Partial, labelled, on time, beats complete-looking and curated. The moment a reader feels curated to, every earlier page gets reread in a colder light.

The revenue in the deck, in the management accounts, in the tax filing and in the contracts will not match to the dollar. They should match once you explain the gap: timing, a definition, a customer you account for differently. Write that bridge once and reuse it. A fresh explanation for each audience is how a small gap becomes a question about character.

A true answer in a week is weaker than a true answer in a day. Delay is hard to tell apart from reconstruction. If the person who knows is travelling, say so on day one. Six days of silence and a polished answer on day seven reads as a committee having decided what the truth should be. You may have done nothing of the sort. You will not be given that benefit twice.

If the contract they asked for has a side letter, send the side letter. If the litigation search is clean but a letter arrived last month that might become one, say so. The neighbouring fact is where trust gets built, because they didn't know to ask. Diligence ends when the reader believes the next document will resemble this one. It does not end when the list is ticked.

創辦人把盡職審查清單當功課。找檔、送檔、勾掉。有些行是功課。整張清單在評的是：有人拉一條線的時候，公司還是不是同一家。

對方要超過某個金額的所有客戶合約，你送三個精選案例，這不是部分答案。這是另一場對話。完整的一套交不出，就講你在交甚麼、缺甚麼、其餘何時有。部分的、標明了的、準時的，好過看起來完整、其實挑過。讀者一旦覺得被挑過，前面的頁都會被用更冷的光再看一遍。

簡報的收入、管理賬的收入、報稅的收入、合約裡的收入，不會分毫不差。解釋了缺口之後應該對得上：時間、一個定義、一個入賬方式不同的客戶。這座橋寫一次，以後都用。為每個觀眾換一個解釋，小缺口就會變成品格問題。

一個星期後的真話，弱過一天後的真話。延誤和事後拼湊很難分辨。知道的人在路上，第一天就說。沉默六天、第七天一個打磨過的答案，讀起來像有人決定了真相該是甚麼。你也許完全沒有這樣做。這種懷疑，不會再給你一次好處。

他們要的合約有附帶函，就把附帶函一起送。訴訟搜查是乾淨的，但上個月有一封信可能變成訴訟，就講。旁邊的事實是信任長出來的地方，因為他們不知道要問。讀者相信下一份文件會跟這一份相像，審查才結束。不是清單勾完的時候。
