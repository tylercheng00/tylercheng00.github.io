Assay transfer is where batches break批次常在檢測轉移時斷掉

The method that worked in one lab is not yet the method. Transfer is the experiment investors should hear about before the pretty chart.在一個實驗室成立的方法，還不是方法本身。轉移才是投資者在漂亮圖表之前該聽到的實驗。

Biotech生物科技16 Nov 20262026年11月16日4 min read閱讀約 4 分鐘

A chart from the founding lab looks clean. Same hands, same freezer, same habits. The day you move the assay to a partner, a hospital site, or a larger batch, the chart is no longer the same object. Transfer is not paperwork. It is the next experiment.

I listen for how a team talks about that move. If they only show the in-house curve and a plan to "scale later", the risk is still ahead of them. If they can say what changed when a second operator ran it, what failed, and what they locked in the protocol, I trust the next slide more.

Batches break here for boring reasons. Reagent lots. Incubation times that were never written down. A centrifuge setting everyone "just knew". Those details feel too small for a fundraising deck. They are exactly what makes a later diligence call go quiet.

You do not need a perfect multi-site dataset to raise. You need honesty about where the method has been shown to travel, and where it has not. One successful transfer, described plainly, beats five beautiful runs that never left the room.

Put transfer on the critical path early. Budget time for it. Budget samples for it. When someone asks what could delay the next financing milestone, say the method moving to a second pair of hands — if that is true. Better they hear it from you than from a failed batch report.

創始實驗室的圖看起來乾淨。同一雙手、同一個冰箱、同一套習慣。你把檢測移到合作方、醫院點或更大批次的那天，那張圖就不再是同一個物件。轉移不是文書。它是下一個實驗。

我會聽團隊怎麼談這一步。若他們只展示內部曲線，再加一句「稍後放大」，風險仍在前面。若他們能說第二個操作者跑的時候改了甚麼、失敗了甚麼、規程鎖定了甚麼，我更信下一頁。

批次斷在這裏，原因很無聊。試劑批次。從未寫下的孵育時間。人人「都知道」的離心設定。這些細節對融資投影片來說太小。恰恰是它們讓後來的盡職審查電話安靜下來。

融資不需要完美的多中心數據。你需要誠實：方法在哪裏被證明搬得動，哪裏還沒有。一次成功的轉移，講得明白，勝過五次從未離開房間的漂亮實驗。

早一點把轉移放進關鍵路徑。為它留時間。為它留樣本。有人問甚麼會拖慢下一個融資里程碑時，若事實如此，就說：方法交到第二雙手。最好由你說出，而不是由一份失敗的批次報告。
