::: en
A strong management presentation helps. It does not fix a cap table that does not reconcile. After the talk, the buyers return to the folder. Make sure the folder matches what was said.

Rehearse answers. Also reconcile the numbers the night before.

::: zh
一場強的管理層簡報有幫助。它修不好對不上的股權表。講完之後，買家回到資料夾。確保資料夾和所說一致。

排練回答。也在前一晚把數字對好。
