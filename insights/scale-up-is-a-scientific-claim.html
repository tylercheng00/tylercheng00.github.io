Scale-up is a scientific claim放大生產，是一個科學主張

A bigger batch is not the same object in a larger cup. Saying the scale-up is straightforward is a claim that it is still the same product.更大的一批，不是同一個東西裝在更大的杯子裡。說放大很直接，就是在主張它還是同一個產品。

Biotech生物科技12 Nov 20262026年11月12日3 min read閱讀約 3 分鐘

Heat leaves differently. Mixing takes longer. A filter that was a detail becomes the step that fails. Impurities you couldn't see become the thing a specification has to name. When a company says scale-up is straightforward, they are claiming that the product at the new scale is the same product, in the ways that matter, and that they know which ways those are.

You can't discover what "the same" means after the large batch looks a bit off. While the process is still small, decide which properties are the product. Size, if it is a particle. Potency. A residual you already know is a problem. Write the short list and how each will be measured. Then the large batch has something to pass or fail. Without the list, every difference becomes a negotiation about whether it counts.

Treat the first larger batch as an experiment with a hypothesis, not as the start of supply. Budget it so a bad result is affordable. The trouble starts when that batch is already promised to a partner, an animal study and a photograph. Then a difference cannot be reported cleanly, because too many people need it not to be a difference. Promise the batch after it has passed.

Going from a beaker to a bigger vessel while also changing the supplier, the order of addition and the person in charge is four experiments sharing a bad day. If you must change more than the scale, say so. Don't blame scale alone. The record should let a later hire see what moved. You will not remember.

There is an honest early version. We can make it at this scale, this many times, with these properties held, and we have not shown the next scale. That sentence can be funded. "Manufacturing is solved" is a hostage. The first time a batch drifts, you have to walk back a word you used to sound finished.

熱的離開方式不同。混合更久。一個本來是細節的過濾，變成失敗的那一步。本來看不見的雜質，變成標準必須點名的東西。公司說放大很直接的時候，他們是在講：新規模下它還是同一個產品，在要緊的方面，而且他們知道哪些方面要緊。

大批看起來有點不對之後，你才來發現「相同」是甚麼意思，那就晚了。製程還小的時候，決定哪些性質就是這個產品。粒子的話是大小。效力。一種你已經知道有問題的殘留。寫下短清單，以及每一項怎麼量。大批之後才有東西可以過或不過。沒有清單，每一個差異都變成一次關於它算不算的談判。

第一次較大的批次，當成一個有假設的實驗，不是供應的開始。預算要做到壞結果你負擔得起。麻煩是這一批已經答應了給夥伴、動物研究和一張照片。然後差異就無法乾淨地報告，因為太多人需要它不是差異。通過了再承諾。

從燒杯走到更大的容器，同時換供應商、改加入次序、換負責人，這是四個實驗共用糟糕的一天。你必須改的不只是規模，就說出來。不要只怪規模。記錄要讓後來進來的人看見甚麼動了。你不會記得。

早期有一個誠實的版本。我們可以在這個規模做出來，做這麼多次，這些性質守得住，下一個規模還沒有顯示。這句可以被投。「生產已經解決」是一個人質。批次一漂，你就要收回一個用來顯得做完了的詞。
