::: en
Animal data is where a young biotech company first feels like it has news. Something happened in a living system that is not a dish. The figure is more persuasive than the in vitro work, and the deck starts to speak in the future tense. That is the moment to slow down by exactly one sentence.

The result can be real and still not be about the patient you are raising money to treat.

### Say what the model is a model of

Every animal model leaves something out. The disease is induced, or it is genetic, or it is a piece of a human tumour in an animal whose immune system was altered so the tumour would grow. None of this makes the experiment worthless. It makes the sentence "it works in animals" too large for the experiment.

Name the model in plain words. Say which part of the human problem it resembles, and which part it cannot resemble. If you do not know the second half, you are not ready to use the first half in a fundraise. The investor who knows the disease will fill the gap with their own, less generous, version.

### Dose, route, and how many

Three facts belong next to the figure, not in a backup slide nobody opens. The dose, compared with a human-equivalent dose you are not embarrassed by. The route, which is often more convenient in the animal than it will ever be in a clinic. The number of animals, and whether the ones that do not fit the story are still in the plot.

A single dramatic animal is an anecdote you photographed. Say so if that is what you have, and show it as a direction, not as proof.

### The sentence that should follow

After the result, one sentence of the form: this does not show that a patient will benefit, because of this specific gap, and the next study that would narrow that gap is the following, at this cost. Teams resist this sentence because it feels like arguing against themselves. It is the opposite. It is the only way to keep the result when a sceptical reader pushes on it. A result you over-claim will be thrown out whole. A result you fence correctly will still be standing at the end of diligence.

### Do not let the picture pick the indication

It is tempting to aim the company at whichever disease made the nicest animal figure. Sometimes that is right. Sometimes the figure is nicest because the model was the easiest, not because the patient need is the greatest or the path to a trial is the shortest. Ask the commercial and clinical question after the figure, not before you have fallen in love with it. The data can stay. The strategy has to be chosen by someone other than the prettiest chart.

動物數據是年輕生物科技公司第一次覺得自己有新聞的地方。活的系統裏發生了一件事，不是培養皿。那張圖比體外實驗更有說服力，簡報開始用將來式說話。就是這一刻，應該剛好慢下一句。

結果可以是真的，卻仍然不是關於你正為之融資、要去治療的那個病人。

### 說明這個模型是甚麼的模型

每一個動物模型都省略了一些東西。疾病是誘導的，或是遺傳的，或是一塊人的腫瘤長在一隻免疫系統被改過、好讓腫瘤長出來的動物身上。這些都不會讓實驗毫無價值。它讓「在動物身上有效」這句話，大於實驗本身。

用普通的話寫出模型的名字。說它像人類問題的哪一部分，以及它不可能像的是哪一部分。如果你不知道後半句，你就還沒有準備好用前半句去融資。懂這種病的投資者會用他們自己的、沒那麼慷慨的版本，把空位填上。

### 劑量、途徑、多少隻

三個事實應該在圖的旁邊，而不是在沒有人打開的備份頁。劑量，對比一個你不會尷尬的人體等價劑量。給藥途徑，在動物身上往往比在診所裏方便得多。動物的數目，以及不符合故事的那幾隻是否仍在圖裏。

一隻戲劇性的動物，是你拍下來的一則軼事。如果那就是你有的，就這樣說，並把它顯示為一個方向，而不是證明。

### 應該跟着的那一句

結果之後，一句這種形式的話：這並沒有顯示病人會受益，因為有這個具體的缺口；而能夠把缺口收窄的下一個研究是這個，成本是這些。團隊抗拒這句話，因為感覺像在反對自己。剛好相反。這是懷疑的讀者推你的時候，結果仍然留得住的唯一方法。你過度聲稱的結果，會被整份扔掉。你正確地圍起來的結果，盡職審查結束時還站着。

### 不要讓圖片替你選適應症

公司很容易朝着那張最好看的動物圖所指向的疾病走。有時這是對的。有時圖最好看，是因為模型最容易，不是因為病人的需要最大，或走到臨床試驗的路最短。看完圖之後再問商業和臨床的問題，而不是在你愛上那張圖之前。數據可以留下。策略必須由最漂亮的那張圖以外的人來選。
