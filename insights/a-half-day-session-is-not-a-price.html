A holiday-week trading range is not a valuation signal從美股感恩節週的大起大落看：假期半日市，不是你的估值訊號

U.S. stocks swung hard on 25 November, closed on 26 November for Thanksgiving and shut early on 27 November; founders reading the tape from Hong Kong should separate a thin-liquidity week from a pricing signal.美股11月25日盤中大幅波動，26日感恩節休市，27日提早收市；從香港看盤的創辦人，應把流動性偏薄的一週與真正的定價訊號分開。

U.S. equity markets were closed on Thursday, 26 November 2026, for Thanksgiving and are scheduled to close at 1 p.m. New York time today, 27 November. The week around those closures was volatile by any measure. On Wednesday the S&P 500 fell as much as 3.2% during the session and still finished down only 0.3%. The Nasdaq composite dropped as much as 4.6% before closing 0.6% lower, and the Dow Jones Industrial Average closed 0.3% higher. By Thursday evening in New York, index futures were pointing modestly higher, and the major averages were on course for a weekly gain after a very large move on Monday. From a desk in Hong Kong, that sequence is easy to over-read. For a private company preparing a financing, the more disciplined reading is that a holiday-shortened week is a poor instrument for measuring anything about your own value.

Consider what the reported numbers actually show. A session that falls more than 3% at its worst and closes close to flat tells you that sellers and buyers were both active and that the close was a compromise between them. It does not tell you what a fair multiple is for a clinical-stage company. A half-day session on a Friday adds a further caveat: fewer participants, earlier cut-offs and less depth in the order book. Price moves in those conditions can be larger than the information behind them.

The same caution applies to financing conversations. It is common, in a week like this, for an investor to say that the market feels unsettled and to ask whether a round should be delayed. It is equally common for a founder to hear a strong rebound and conclude that the window has reopened. Both reactions treat the headline index as if it were a quote for your company. It is not. The relevant evidence for a private round is narrower: how recent comparable financings were priced, how comparable public companies are trading over a period longer than a week, and what the specific investor in front of you has said in writing.

So what is worth doing in a short, noisy week? Keep a simple log. Write down the date, the index level, the move that day, and one sentence on what, if anything, changed in your own financing plan. After a month, read the log back. Most founders find that very few entries changed the plan. The habit makes the distinction visible: some days are news, and most days are weather.

Use the quiet days for work that does not depend on the tape. Refresh the data-room index and check that every document is dated and current. Confirm that the cap table and option pool match the latest board minutes. Review the manufacturing and toxicology calendar for slots that fall between mid-December and early January, when Hong Kong, mainland and U.S. counterparts all take time off. A reply that takes five working days in November can take ten in the holiday period, and that delay belongs in the financing timeline.

For teams with Hong Kong or mainland investors, add one more check. Local markets open on Friday while New York is on a short session, so a view formed from U.S. futures overnight may already be stale by the time a call begins. Ask the investor which reference they use and for what period. Agreeing on that in advance saves an argument later.

None of this is a forecast of any index or a comment on any company. It is a reminder that valuation conversations are made of documents, comparables and dated commitments, while a thin-liquidity trading week is mostly a record of how few people were at their desks. This note is a research-desk view for founders, not investment advice.

美國股市在2026年11月26日（週四）因感恩節休市，今天11月27日預定紐約時間下午一時提早收市。夾在這兩次休市之間的一週，無論怎麼看都相當波動：週三標準普爾500指數盤中最多跌3.2%，收市只跌0.3%；納斯達克綜合指數盤中最多跌4.6%，收市跌0.6%；道瓊斯工業平均指數則收升0.3%。到了紐約週四晚上，指數期貨略為偏高，三大指數在週一大幅反彈之後，本週仍有望收漲。坐在香港看這一串走勢，很容易讀過了頭。對正準備融資的私人公司來說，比較審慎的看法是：假期縮短的一週，是量度自己公司價值的差工具。

先看這些報道的數字說明了甚麼。一個交易日盤中最差跌超過3%，收市卻幾乎持平，說明買賣雙方都很活躍，收市價是雙方拉扯後的折衷，並不能告訴你一間臨床階段公司合理的倍數是多少。星期五的半日市還有額外限制：參與者較少、截單時間較早、買賣盤的深度也較淺，在這種情況下，價格的波幅可能大過它背後所含的資訊。

融資對話也是同樣道理。這樣的一週，常有投資者說市況不穩，問要不要把這輪融資延後；也常有創辦人聽到一次強勁反彈，就覺得窗口已經重開。這兩種反應，都是把大市指數當成自己公司的報價。其實不是。私人融資真正有參考價值的證據窄得多：近期可比融資如何定價、可比上市公司在一個星期以上的時間裏如何交易，以及眼前這位投資者曾經用書面講過甚麼。

那麼，在一個短而嘈雜的星期，可以做甚麼？可以簡單記一本日誌：寫下日期、指數水平、當日升跌，再加一句話，說明自己的融資計劃有沒有因此改變。一個月後回頭翻看，多數創辦人會發現，真正改變過計劃的記錄寥寥無幾。這個習慣的好處，是把分別變得清楚：有些日子是新聞，大部分日子只是天氣。

安靜的日子，適合做不看行情的功課。更新資料室目錄，確認每份文件都有日期、內容是最新的；核對股東名冊和期權池，是否與最近一次董事會紀錄一致；翻查生產與毒理的日程，看看有沒有檔期落在12月中至1月初，那段時間香港、內地和美國的對手方都會休假。平常五個工作天的回覆，到了假期可能要十天，這段延誤應該寫進融資時間表。

如果投資者來自香港或內地，還要多核對一點：週五香港市場照常開市，而紐約只開半日，憑隔夜美股期貨形成的看法，在通話開始時可能已經過時。可以先問對方用哪一個參考指標、看多長的時間，事先講好，省得之後爭拗。

以上不是對任何指數的預測，也不是對任何公司的評論，只是提醒：估值對話由文件、可比公司和有日期的承諾組成，而流動性偏薄的交易週，主要記錄的是有多少人不在座位上。本文是研究桌面對創辦人的看法，不構成投資建議。
