A bookbuilding range is a hypothesis, and the order book is the test從韓國Innovo申請KOSDAQ上市、多宗IPO相繼撤回看：定價區間只是假設，認購簿才是考試

Korean AI drug developer Innovo Therapeutics held its IPO briefing on 27 November while other Korean listings were being withdrawn. Its offer terms, float design and use of proceeds are a compact case study in how to read an IPO range in a cautious market.韓國人工智能藥物研發公司Innovo Therapeutics11月27日舉行IPO說明會，同期韓國多宗上市計劃撤回；它的招股條款、流通股設計與募資用途，是在審慎市況下閱讀IPO定價區間的精簡個案。

On Friday, 27 November 2026, Innovo Therapeutics held an initial public offering briefing in Seoul’s Yeouido district. The company develops synthetic drugs using an artificial-intelligence platform and is seeking a KOSDAQ listing. Its terms, as reported by Chosun Biz, Financial News and the Asia Business Daily, are specific. It plans to sell 2.2 million newly issued shares at an indicative range of 20,000 to 24,000 won, which would raise 44 billion to 52.8 billion won and imply a market value of roughly 283 billion to 339 billion won. Institutional bookbuilding began on 26 November and runs to 2 December. Retail subscription is scheduled for 7 and 8 December. Korea Investment & Securities is the lead underwriter. The final price had not been set at the time of the briefing.

The timing is what makes this useful to read from Hong Kong. Financial News reported on 28 November that, amid a series of subscription shortfalls and listing withdrawals, an Alzheimer’s drug developer called Adel had pulled its listing plans the week before, and an aerospace company, ANH Structure, withdrew after finishing institutional bookbuilding, which cancelled its retail offering. Eight companies are set to begin institutional bookbuilding in the week of 30 November. Innovo’s management acknowledged at the briefing that biotech issuers in the recent market have repeatedly received valuations they consider inadequate or have listed after lowering their offer prices.

So how should one read a range in a market like this? Treat it as a hypothesis the company is testing, and look at the design choices around it.

Start with use of proceeds. Management said about 80% of the money, around 35 billion won according to Financial News, would go to clinical development and research, and the company has said it plans further licensing talks with global drug makers from 2028. It pointed to a licensing deal with Daewoong Pharmaceutical signed in May, worth up to 662.5 billion won, for one candidate in inflammatory bowel disease. Note the words “up to”: a headline figure that includes milestones is not cash received, and the briefing itself said Innovo shares development costs through Phase 1. A reader should separate committed payments from contingent ones.

Next, the float. The public offering is about 15% of shares, and 25.46% of shares would be tradable on the first day, including those held by existing holders. The company said that is roughly ten percentage points lower than at other companies listed since 2025, and that later unlock stages are also lower. About 36.5% of shares, including those of all six founders and related parties, carry a three-year lock-up. But the registration statement, as Chosun Biz reported, shows further tranches becoming tradable at one, two, three and six months after listing: 9.37%, 8.90%, 9.60% and 8.70%. A small first-day float can support a price at the start, and a schedule of unlocks can matter later. Both are facts in the document, and both belong in the analysis.

Third, the pipeline. Innovo said it runs nine projects, four in clinical trials, and wants to add one new project each year. It said it needs about ten projects to achieve commercial results consistently because synthetic drug development is inherently unlikely to succeed. That is an honest portfolio argument, and it also means that a single data point will not define the company.

Finally, wait for the order book. The institutional demand figures and the final price were not public as of this writing. A range tells you what the company and its underwriter hoped to test. The order book tells you what investors were prepared to pay.

For a Hong Kong Chapter 18A issuer, the parallels are direct: cornerstone strength, float design, lock-up stages and a clear use-of-proceeds statement carry more weight in a cautious window than a story about the platform. None of this is a view on Innovo’s pricing or its shares. This note is a research-desk view of how to read an offering, not investment advice.

11月27日（星期五），韓國人工智能新藥公司Innovo Therapeutics在首爾汝矣島舉行IPO說明會，計劃在韓國創業板KOSDAQ上市。根據朝鮮Biz、《金融新聞》和亞洲經濟的報道，招股條款相當具體：公司計劃發行220萬股新股，參考價格區間為每股2萬至2.4萬韓元，集資440億至528億韓元，對應的市值約2,830億至3,390億韓元。機構投資者的詢價簿記程序從11月26日開始，到12月2日結束，散戶認購排在12月7日和8日，主承銷商是韓國投資證券。說明會舉行時，最終發行價還沒有確定。

時間點，正是這個個案值得香港讀者留意的地方。《金融新聞》11月28日報道，韓國近期接連出現認購不足和撤回上市的情況：阿爾茨海默症藥物開發商Adel上星期放棄了上市計劃；航空航天企業ANH Structure在完成機構詢價之後也撤回申請，原定的散戶認購一併取消。下星期（11月30日起）另有八間公司展開機構詢價。Innovo的管理層在說明會上也承認，近期生技發行人多次獲得他們認為偏低的估值，或者在調低發行價之後才上市。

在這樣的市況下，應該怎樣看一個定價區間？不妨把它當成公司正在驗證的一個假設，再去看圍繞它的各項設計。

先看募資用途。管理層說，大約八成資金（《金融新聞》指約350億韓元）會用於臨床開發和研究，公司並表示打算由2028年起，與跨國藥廠展開更多授權談判。公司以5月與大熊製藥簽訂的授權協議為例，這項針對炎症性腸病候選藥物的協議最高價值6,625億韓元。請留意「最高」兩個字：包含里程碑付款的總額，並不等於已收到的現金；說明會本身也提到，Innovo須分擔直至一期為止的開發費用。讀者應該把已確定的付款，與視乎條件才會到手的付款分開來看。

再看流通股的設計。公開發售約佔股份15%，上市首日可流通的股份為25.46%（包括現有股東的部分）。公司說，這個比例比2025年以來其他上市公司低約十個百分點，之後各階段解禁的比例也同樣偏低。約36.5%的股份，包括六位創辦人及相關人士持有的股份，已承諾三年禁售。但按朝鮮Biz引述的證券申報書，在上市後一個月、兩個月、三個月和六個月，還會分別有9.37%、8.90%、9.60%和8.70%的股份陸續可以買賣。首日流通量小，開局時可能有利於股價；解禁時間表則在之後可能產生影響。這兩點都寫在文件裏，兩點都應該放進分析。

第三是管線。Innovo表示公司同時推進九個項目，其中四個已進入臨床，並打算每年增加一個新項目。公司認為，合成藥物的研發成功率本來就低，所以要有大約十個項目，才能穩定地取得商業化成果。這是一個坦率的組合論述，也意味着單一數據點不會決定這間公司的命運。

最後，等認購簿。截至本文撰寫時，機構需求的數字和最終發行價都還沒有公布。區間告訴你公司與承銷商希望測試甚麼，認購簿才告訴你，投資者實際願意付多少。

對香港的第18A章發行人來說，情況十分相似：基石投資者的實力、流通股設計、禁售分階段解禁，以及清楚的募資用途聲明，在審慎的市況下，比一個關於技術平台的故事更有分量。以上並不是對Innovo定價或股份的判斷。本文是研究桌面對如何閱讀招股條款的看法，不構成投資建議。
