::: en
Scientific and commercial references are different. A collaborator who co-authored a paper may not want to discuss your manufacturing claims. A paid user may not want to be named. Know which is which before you promise a call in a meeting.

Prepare two or three people who have agreed in advance. Tell them what topics are fair. Tell the investor what they will and will not hear. A reference that surprises everyone on the call is not diligence. It is risk.

If you have no one who will speak, say so and explain why — early stage, confidential pilots, regulated settings. Silence with a reason beats a scramble to find a friendly voice after the meeting ends.

::: zh
科學推薦和商業推薦不同。共同寫論文的合作者未必想談你的製造主張。付費用戶未必願具名。開會承諾電話之前，先分清。

預先準備兩三位已同意的人。告訴他們哪些題目合適。告訴投資者會聽到甚麼、聽不到甚麼。讓所有人措手不及的推薦不是盡職審查，是風險。

若沒有人願意說，就直說並解釋——太早、機密試點、受規管場景。有理由的沉默，勝過會議結束後才去找友善聲音的慌亂。
