::: en
There is a comforting story about raising a little. Keep it light, close it with angels, save the serious process for later. Some small rounds are genuinely light, and should stay that way. Many carry the mess of a larger round and the attention of a smaller one. That is how a "quick" raise becomes a season.

The documents do not shrink with the cheque. A messy cap table is not tidier because the amount is modest. A missing IP assignment does not matter less. A note stacked on two earlier notes does not become simple because you called it a bridge. The legal work follows what already exists, not the size of the new money. If the history is tangled, a small round can cost as much attention as a large one, for less runway. Sometimes the right small round is the one that pauses to clean the history first, even if that feels like not raising.

In a larger process, someone's fee and reputation are attached to closing. In a small round, the other side is often investing their own money between other jobs. They will get to you after the thing that pays their week. A close that needs five busy people to read a document on the same Friday will miss that Friday. Fewer signatures, a shorter document, one person empowered to say yes: that is how small stays fast. "Just one more angel" is how small becomes slow.

An eighty-folder data room and a weekly all-hands update are tools for a process with many readers. For a small round they are a way to feel serious while the wire is not sent. Send the five documents that let a careful person say yes, and talk to them. If they want more, they will ask.

Small because the milestone is genuinely cheap is a good reason. Small because the larger round was not available, and nobody has said what this money changes, is a warning. Write the sentence anyway. This amount, by this date, retires this risk. If the sentence is embarrassing, the round is not small. It is unclear. Unclear rounds are the expensive ones, at any size.

有一個令人安慰的故事，關於融少一點。保持輕、跟天使成交、認真的流程留到以後。有些小輪確實輕，也該保持輕。很多帶着大輪的亂，和小輪的注意力。這就是「很快」變成一個季節的方式。

文件不會跟着支票縮小。股權表亂，不會因為金額不大就變整齊。缺一份知識產權轉讓，不會變得比較不要緊。一張票據疊在兩張更早的上面，不會因為你叫它過橋就變簡單。法律工作跟着已經存在的東西走，不是跟着新錢的大小走。歷史纏着的話，小輪可以花掉跟大輪一樣多的注意力，換更短的跑道。有時對的小輪，是先停下來把歷史弄乾淨的那一輪，即使那感覺像沒有在融資。

大的流程裡，有人的費用和名聲系於成交。小輪裡，對方常常是在其他工作之間投自己的錢。他們會在處理完支付這個星期的那件事之後處理你。一個需要五個忙人在同一個星期五讀同一份文件的成交，會錯過那個星期五。更少的簽名、更短的文件、一個可以說好的人：小仍然可以快。「再多一個天使」是小變成慢的方式。

八十個資料夾和每星期的全員更新，是給很多讀者的工具。對小輪來說，它們是匯款還沒走的時候讓自己覺得認真。送出那五份讓一個謹慎的人可以說好的文件，然後跟他們談。他們要更多，會問。

小，是因為里程碑真的便宜，這是好理由。小，是因為大的那輪不可得，又沒有人講這筆錢改變甚麼，這是警告。反正把那句寫下來。這個金額，在這個日期前，退掉這個風險。這句令人尷尬的話，這一輪就不是小。它是不清楚。不清楚的輪次才貴，無論大小。
