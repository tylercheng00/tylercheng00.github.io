::: en
After a pitch or an intro, send one short follow-up with the link and the ask. If there is no reply, move on. People who want the conversation will resurface. People who do not will not be persuaded by volume.

Your reputation is part of the product. Protect it by not filling inboxes with hope.

::: zh
自薦或引薦之後，寄一封短跟進，帶連結和請求。沒回覆就往前走。想談的人會再出現。不想談的人不會被數量說服。

名聲是產品的一部分。別用希望塞滿收件箱，來保護它。
