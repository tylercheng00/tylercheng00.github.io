::: en
Nvidia’s board said it took senior U.S. government input before reinstating Jensen Huang, and that it saw no national-security risk in bringing him back. President Trump publicly welcomed the decision. That political cover helped the stock and calmed a frightened AI complex on 23–24 November. It did not repeal export-control policy, congressional curiosity, or the company’s own promise to keep investigating foreign-intelligence targeting while standing up stronger compliance. House and Senate calendars already point to early-December conversations with Huang and Nvidia counsel about export controls and China. Founders who treat “Huang is back” as “China risk is closed” will walk into diligence with last month’s slides.

Rewrite the China page as an operating map, not a slogan. Where do you manufacture, assemble, support, and invoice. Which entities touch controlled items or dual-use know-how. Which customers require end-use assurances. Which resellers you actually use — not the ones you wish you used. If you sell into cloud or systems integrators that serve mixed geographies, say how you segment SKUs and refuse orders. After Huang’s drama, partners will assume informal channels are under renewed heat. Your materials should show you assumed the same.

Add dates. “We monitor export rules” is weaker than “counsel briefed the board on [month], the last denied-party screen ran on [date], and the next scheduled review is [date].” Nvidia’s creation of a National Security and Export Control Committee and a chief compliance officer search is a template language investors now recognize. You do not need Nvidia’s budget. You need an owner and a cadence that would survive a headline week. If the owner is only the founder’s memory, you have key-person risk stacked on regulatory risk.

Taiwan supply-chain voices said U.S. controls on AI chip sales are more likely to tighten than loosen after the reinstatement, and that some gray paths through third countries will face fuller shutdown. Whether or not every rumor lands, the diligence implication is stable: do not bank a financing model on a channel you cannot describe to counsel under oath. Prefer revenue mixes you can defend in a clean memo. If a material slice of pipeline depends on policy discretion, show the sensitivity table — bull, base, and a “license delayed six months” case — instead of a single hockey stick.

Keep the Huang news in the opening paragraph of your update note so partners know you are awake, then spend the rest of the page on your controls. Markets can roar when a CEO returns with White House applause. Capital still prices the statute, the license, and the reseller you cannot name. Write those three clearly this week, before December’s hearing noise refreshes every China question in every AI-adjacent data room.
Founders selling into AI data-center buildouts should also update the customer FAQ. Expect a question like: “If a key GPU vendor’s leadership is contested again, or if a license batch slips, what happens to your delivery dates?” Answer with lead-time buffers and alternate qualification paths, not with optimism. Optimism is not a mitigation. A dated second-source plan is. If you have no second source, say the concentration plainly and show the cash runway that absorbs a six-month slip. Honesty about concentration beats a vague claim that “the industry will sort itself out,” especially in a week when the industry sorted itself by putting one person back in the chair.

Finally, keep China language consistent across the deck, the data room, and the verbal pitch. Mismatches — a soft China sentence in the pitch and a hard denial in the appendix — are how trust breaks after Huang-week headlines make every associate google your reseller list. Align the words. Then sleep better when December hearings refresh the news cycle.

輝達董事會表示，恢復黃仁勳職務前聽取了美國政府高層意見，並認為復職不構成國安風險；特朗普公開歡迎。這層政治掩護推升了股價，也在11月23日至24日安撫受驚的人工智慧板塊。但它並未廢除出口管制、國會的好奇心，以及公司自己「調查繼續、合規加硬」的承諾。參眾議院行程已指向十二月初與黃仁勳及輝達法務就出口管制與中國議題的對話。若把「黃仁勳回來了」讀成「中國風險結案」，你會帶著上個月的投影片走進盡調。

把中國頁改寫成營運地圖，而不是口號。你在哪裡製造、組裝、支援與開票；哪些實體碰到受控物項或兩用技術；哪些客戶需要最終用途保證；你真正使用的經銷商是誰——不是你希望使用的那些。若你賣進服務多地區的雲端或系統整合商，要說明如何切割料號、如何拒單。黃仁勳風波之後，合夥人會預設非正式渠道正被重新加熱；你的材料應顯示你也作了同樣預設。

加上日期。「我們關注出口規則」弱於「律師於某月向董事會簡報、最近一次拒絕往來名單掃描在某日、下次排程檢視在某日」。輝達成立國安與出口管制委員會、並尋找法遵長，已成為投資人認得的範本語言。你不需要輝達的預算，但需要一個能撐過頭條週的負責人與節奏。若負責人只是創辦人的記憶，那是把關鍵人風險疊在監管風險上。

台灣供應鏈人士指出，黃仁勳復職後，美國對人工智慧晶片銷售的管制更可能收緊而非放寬，部分經第三國的灰色路徑也會被更完整關閉。無論每則傳聞是否落地，盡調含義穩定：不要把融資模型押在你無法在宣誓下向律師描述的渠道上。寧可要能用乾淨備忘錄辯護的營收結構；若管線中有實質一塊依賴政策裁量，就出示敏感度表——樂觀、基準，以及「許可延誤六個月」情境——而不是一條孤零零的曲棍球棒。

更新說明的首段可以點出黃仁勳新聞，讓合夥人知道你醒著；其餘篇幅請留給你的控制措施。執行長在白宮掌聲中回歸，市場可以咆哮；資本仍在為法條、許可證，以及你叫不出名字的經銷商定價。趁十二月聽證會噪音刷新每間人工智慧周邊資料室的中國問題之前，這週就把這三樣寫清楚。

賣進人工智慧資料中心建設的創辦人，也該更新客戶問答。預期會被問：「若關鍵圖形處理器供應商的領導層再起爭議，或一批許可延誤，你的交期怎麼辦？」請用交期緩衝與備援認證路徑回答，而不是樂觀。樂觀不是緩解措施，有日期的第二來源計畫才是。若沒有第二來源，就坦白寫出集中度，並出示能吸收六個月延誤的現金跑道。集中度的老實話，勝過「產業自己會搞定」的含糊——尤其在產業靠把一個人送回座位來「搞定」的這一週。

最後，讓簡報、資料室與口頭簡報的中國表述一致。簡報講得軟、附錄否認得硬，這種落差最容易在黃仁勳週的頭條讓每位助理開始搜你的經銷商名單之後，把信任拆掉。先對齊用詞，十二月聽證會刷新新聞週時，你才睡得著。
