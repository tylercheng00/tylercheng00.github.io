Privacy policy私隱政策

What this website collects, why, where it goes, and how to ask for it to be deleted. Last updated 4 November 2026.本網站收集甚麼資料、用途、資料去向，以及如何要求刪除。最後更新：2026 年 11 月 4 日。

This website (tylercheng00.github.io and www.tylerfuture.com) is run by Tyler Tik Man Cheng (鄭迪珉), who decides how the information described here is used. Questions or requests: info@tylerfuture.com.

1. What is collected

  • Message form (Contact page): your name, e-mail, and if you choose to give them, company, phone / WeChat / WhatsApp, topic, preferred meeting time and your message.
  • Meeting bookings (Contact page, provided by Cal.com): your name, e-mail, the time you choose and anything you write in the booking notes.
  • E-mail: whatever you include if you write to me directly.
  • Visit statistics (GoatCounter): page views, the referring site, browser type, screen size and approximate country. GoatCounter uses no cookies and does not store IP addresses, so visits cannot be linked to you.

The site does not ask for sensitive personal information (for example ID numbers, financial account details or health data). Please do not send it through the form.

2. Why it is used

Only to reply to your enquiry, arrange and hold the meeting you asked for, and keep a record of our correspondence. It is not sold, not rented, not shared for advertising, and not added to a mailing list without your separate agreement.

3. Your consent

Your details are processed because you choose to send them and tick the consent box (or confirm a booking). You can withdraw your consent at any time by e-mail; this does not affect anything done before you withdrew it.

4. Service providers and where your data goes

The site uses these services to work. They handle your data on my behalf and under their own privacy policies:

  • Web3Forms: passes form messages to my inbox. web3forms.com ↗
  • Cal.com: booking calendar and meeting confirmations. Privacy policy ↗
  • GoatCounter: anonymous visit statistics. goatcounter.com ↗
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website. Privacy statement ↗
  • E-mail providers that deliver and store messages sent to info@tylerfuture.com.
  • YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode) and TradingView: a video and market charts are loaded from them. The YouTube player only loads after you press play.

Cross-border transfer. These providers store data outside mainland China, mainly in the United States. If you are in mainland China, sending the form or making a booking means your information is transferred abroad for the purposes above; the consent box on the form covers this transfer. If you do not agree, please do not use the form or booking calendar.

5. How long it is kept

Messages and booking details are kept while we are in contact and for up to 24 months after our last exchange, then deleted, unless you ask for earlier deletion or the law requires longer.

6. Your rights

Wherever you are, you can ask to see the information I hold about you, correct it, delete it, receive a copy, get an explanation of how it is used, or withdraw consent. These include your rights under China's Personal Information Protection Law and, where they apply, US state privacy laws. E-mail info@tylerfuture.com; I will reply within 15 working days. I do not sell or share personal information as defined by California law.

7. Security

The site is served over encrypted HTTPS, and access to the inbox and service accounts is protected with strong passwords. No system is completely secure, so please keep confidential documents for a later, agreed channel.

8. Children

This site is meant for professional enquiries and is not directed at children. Please do not send information about anyone under 14.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

一、收集甚麼資料

  • 留言表單（聯絡頁）：你的姓名、電郵，以及你自願提供的公司、電話／微信／WhatsApp、查詢類別、方便的會面時間和留言內容。
  • 預約會面（聯絡頁，由 Cal.com 提供）：你的姓名、電郵、所選時間，以及你在預約備註中填寫的內容。

  • 訪客統計（GoatCounter）：瀏覽頁面、來源網站、瀏覽器類型、螢幕大小及大約所在國家。GoatCounter 不使用 cookie，也不儲存 IP 地址，因此無法辨識個別訪客。

本網站不會要求你提供敏感個人信息（例如身份證號碼、金融帳戶資料或健康資料），請不要經表單發送這類資料。

二、資料用途

只用於回覆你的查詢、安排及進行你要求的會面，以及保存我們的往來記錄。資料不會出售、不會出租、不會用作廣告，也不會在未經你另行同意下加入任何郵寄名單。

三、你的同意

我們處理你的資料，是因為你自行提交並勾選同意（或確認預約）。你可以隨時以電郵撤回同意；撤回不影響撤回前已進行的處理。

四、服務供應商及資料去向

本網站使用以下服務運作，它們代我處理資料，並受其各自的私隱政策約束：

  • Web3Forms：把表單留言轉發到我的郵箱。web3forms.com ↗
  • Cal.com：預約日曆及會面確認。私隱政策 ↗
  • GoatCounter：匿名訪客統計。goatcounter.com ↗
  • GitHub Pages：網站寄存。私隱聲明 ↗
  • 傳送及儲存 info@tylerfuture.com 郵件的電郵服務供應商。
  • YouTube（私隱強化模式）及 TradingView：網站會從它們載入影片及行情圖表；YouTube 播放器只會在你按下播放後才載入。

跨境傳輸。以上服務供應商在中國內地以外（主要是美國）儲存資料。如你身處中國內地，提交表單或預約即表示你的個人信息會為上述用途傳輸至境外；表單上的同意選項涵蓋此項傳輸。如你不同意，請不要使用表單或預約日曆。

五、保存期限

留言及預約資料會在我們保持聯絡期間保存，並在最後一次往來後最多保存 24 個月，之後刪除；如你要求提早刪除或法律要求保存更久，則按該要求處理。

六、你的權利

無論你身在何處，都可以要求查閱我持有的你的資料、更正、刪除、取得副本、要求解釋處理方式，或撤回同意。這包括你根據《中華人民共和國個人信息保護法》，以及（如適用）美國各州私隱法律享有的權利。請電郵 info@tylerfuture.com，我會在 15 個工作天內回覆。按加州法律的定義，我不會出售或分享個人信息。

七、資料安全

網站以加密 HTTPS 傳輸，郵箱及各服務帳號均以高強度密碼保護。但任何系統都無法保證絕對安全，機密文件請在雙方約定的其他渠道再提供。

八、兒童

本網站供專業查詢使用，並非以兒童為對象。請不要提交任何未滿 14 歲人士的資料。

九、政策更新

如本政策有更改，新版本會在本頁發佈，並註明更新日期。
