::: en
We have twelve months of runway often means a spreadsheet that assumes the round closes on time and hiring pauses politely. Ask for weeks of cash at the current burn, with no new money. Then ask what changes in week six if the close moves.

Boards can work with bad news early. They cannot work with a surprise that the company is three weeks from payroll. Put the number in the update. Put the assumption under it. If the only way the months look good is a miracle close, say that in plain language while there is still time to cut or raise a bridge.

::: zh
我們有十二個月跑道常常是一張表，假設融資準時交割、招聘也會客氣地暫停。先問：以現在消耗、沒有新錢，還有幾週現金。再問：若交割延後，第六週要改甚麼。

董事會可以早一點消化壞消息。無法消化離發薪只剩三週的驚喜。把數字寫進更新。把假設寫在下面。若月之所以好看只因奇蹟式交割，趁還有時間削減或借橋，用白話說清楚。
