::: en
A diligence list looks technical. Contracts, taxes, employment, insurance, litigation, cap table, IP chain. Founders answer it as homework: find the file, send the file, tick the row. Some rows are homework. The list as a whole is a trust test, and it is graded on consistency more than on completeness.

The reader is asking a quieter question. If I pull on this thread, do I find the same company you described?

### Answer the question that was asked

A request for "all customer contracts over a threshold" that comes back as three selected case studies is not a partial answer. It is a different conversation. If you cannot produce the full set, say what you are producing, what is missing, and when the rest will exist. Partial, labelled, on time, beats complete-looking and curated. Curation is the thing diligence is designed to see through. The moment a reader feels curated to, every previous page gets re-read in a colder light.

### Numbers have to survive contact with each other

The revenue in the deck, the revenue in the management accounts, the revenue in the tax filing and the revenue implied by the customer contracts are one fact seen from four places. They will not match to the dollar. They should match once you explain the gap: timing, a definition, a customer you gross up differently. Write that bridge once, keep it, and use it every time the question returns in a new form. Inventing a fresh explanation for each audience is how small gaps become character questions.

### Speed is part of the answer

A true answer in a week is weaker than a true answer in a day, not because speed is a moral good, but because delay is indistinguishable from reconstruction. If a number takes a week, it may simply be that the person who knows is travelling. Say that on day one. Silence for six days and a polished answer on day seven reads as a committee having decided what the truth should be. You may have done nothing of the sort. You will not be given the benefit of that doubt twice.

### Volunteer the neighbouring fact

If the contract they asked for has a side letter, send the side letter. If the litigation search is clean but you received a letter last month that might become one, say so. The neighbouring fact is where trust is built, because the reader did not know to ask. A company that offers the neighbour looks like it has nothing arranged for the visit. A company that answers only the literal line looks like it has walked the room and turned the photographs to the wall.

Diligence ends when the reader believes the next document will resemble this one. It does not end when the list is ticked.

盡職審查的清單看起來很技術。合約、稅務、僱傭、保險、訴訟、股權表、知識產權鏈。創辦人把它當成功課：找出檔案、送出檔案、勾掉那一行。有些行是功課。整張清單是一個信任測試，評分看的是一致性，多於完整性。

讀者在問一個更安靜的問題。如果我拉這條線，我會不會找到你所描述的同一家公司？

### 回答被問的那個問題

對方要「超過某個門檻的所有客戶合約」，你送回三個精選案例，這不是部分答案。這是另一場對話。如果你交不出完整的一套，就說明你在交甚麼、缺甚麼、其餘的何時會存在。部分的、標明了的、準時的，好過看起來完整、其實是挑選過的。挑選，正是盡職審查要看穿的東西。一旦讀者感覺被挑選過，之前的每一頁都會被用更冷的光線重讀。

### 數字要受得起互相接觸

簡報裏的收入、管理賬裏的收入、報稅的收入、客戶合約暗示的收入，是一個事實從四個地方被看見。它們不會分毫不差。一旦你解釋了缺口，它們應該對得上：時間、一個定義、一個你用不同方式入賬的客戶。把這座橋寫一次，留着，每次問題以新的形式回來都用它。為每一個觀眾發明一個新解釋，是小缺口變成品格問題的方式。

### 速度是答案的一部分

一個星期後的真話，弱過一天後的真話。不是因為速度是道德上的好，而是因為延誤和事後重建無法分辨。如果一個數字要一個星期，也許只是知道的人在路上。第一天就這樣說。沉默六天、第七天一個打磨過的答案，讀起來像一個委員會決定了真相應該是甚麼。你也許完全沒有這樣做。這種懷疑的利益，你不會被給第二次。

### 把旁邊的事實也交出來

如果他們要的合約有一封附帶函，把附帶函一起送。如果訴訟搜查是乾淨的，但你上個月收到一封可能變成訴訟的信，就說出來。旁邊的事實是信任建立的地方，因為讀者不知道要問。一家主動交出鄰居的公司，看起來像沒有為這次到訪佈置過。一家只回答字面那一行的公司，看起來像已經走過房間、把照片轉過去貼牆。

盡職審查在讀者相信下一份文件會和這一份相像時結束。它不在清單被勾完時結束。
