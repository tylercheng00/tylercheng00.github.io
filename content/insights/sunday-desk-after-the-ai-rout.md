::: en
The week ending 20–21 November 2026 taught public markets a blunt lesson: Anthropic’s debut could not hold the AI complex, Nvidia’s leadership vacuum kept pricing fear, and the Nasdaq posted one of its worst weeks since early COVID. Both the S&P 500 and the Dow had already slipped into correction territory on the Friday close. Sunday is when founders usually polish decks. This Sunday, polish the process note instead.

Open with three facts your partners already saw in headlines, then separate them from your operating numbers. Fact one: public AI names sold off hard even where earnings were strong. Fact two: circular financing and data-center build costs are back in every associate’s vocabulary. Fact three: key-person risk at Nvidia made the whole ecosystem look single-threaded. None of those facts, by themselves, change your September cohort retention or your December payroll. Say that. Then show what did change: meeting density, diligence questions about China exposure, and any customer who asked for a contingency sentence.

Do not move your indicated range because a weekend chat group forwarded the Nasdaq candle. Ranges move when diligence milestones move. If your data room is the same as Thursday morning, your range should be the same as Thursday morning, with a dated footnote that you are watching public multiples as sentiment, not as a substitute for quality of earnings. Bankers who push an overnight reprice are telling you how they underwrite. File that information.

Use the Sunday desk to assign owners. Who updates the China page. Who refreshes the customer concentration table. Who drafts the one-paragraph answer to “what happens if a GPU vendor’s leadership stays contested.” Put names and dates. A Sunday note without owners is just anxiety with formatting. Anxiety does not close rounds; owners do.

If you must rewrite slides, rewrite the risk factors and the use-of-proceeds calendar first. Vision pages aged well enough. What aged badly this week was any sentence that treated AI infrastructure demand as politically and organizationally frictionless. Friction is now priced. Your job is to show you already budgeted for friction — in cash runway weeks, in second-source language, and in board cadence — before the rout made friction fashionable.

Close the note with one ask for the coming five business days: a diligence call, a customer reference, or a counsel review. Do not ask for a new term sheet on Sunday night after a rout. Adults schedule work. Opportunists schedule moods. This weekend belongs to the adults who keep the ledger while the tape screams.

Partners will also ask whether you are raising into a colder tape on purpose. Answer with runway weeks and a milestone map, not with bravado. If you can wait sixty days without damaging hiring or experiments, say so and show the cash. If you cannot wait, say what diligence you will finish in fourteen days to justify staying in market. Silence reads as denial. Denial reads as risk. The AI rout week punished denial in public markets; do not import it into your private process.

One more Sunday hygiene item: freeze screenshot culture. Associates will paste Nvidia and Anthropic candles into chat and ask for instant reactions. Reply once with the three-number frame — tape, process range, sensitivity — and refuse to renegotiate by emoji. If a serious partner wants to reopen price, schedule a call with the process owner and counsel on the line. Weekend chat is for logistics. Price is for minutes.

截至2026年11月20日至21日的這一週，公開市場上了一堂直白的課：Anthropic的亮相撐不住整個人造智能板塊，輝達的領導真空持續定價恐懼，那斯達克寫下新冠疫情初期以來最差週表現之一；標普500與道瓊也在星期五收盤滑進回檔。星期日本來是創辦人打磨簡報的日子。這個星期日，請改打磨流程短箋。

先用合夥人已在頭條看過的三個事實開場，再把它們與你的營運數字分開。事實一：即使財報強勁，公開市場的人工智慧名字仍重挫。事實二：循環融資與資料中心興建成本，重新進了每位助理的詞彙。事實三：輝達的關鍵人風險，讓整個生態系看起來像單線。這些事實本身，都不會改變你九月群組留存或十二月薪資。請寫清楚。然後寫真正變了的：會議密度、關於中國暴露的盡調問題，以及有沒有客戶要你補上應變句。

不要因為週末對話群轉發了那斯達克K線就改指示區間。區間隨盡調里程碑而動。若資料室與星期四早上相同，區間就該與星期四早上相同，並加註：公開倍數只當情緒參考，不替代盈餘品質。若銀行家逼你隔夜重定價，那是他們如何承保的資訊，請存檔。

用星期日桌面指派負責人：誰更新中國頁、誰刷新客戶集中度、誰起草「若圖形處理器供應商領導層持續受爭議」的一段回答。要名字與日期。沒有負責人的星期日短箋，只是排好版的焦慮。焦慮結不了案，負責人才能。

若必須改投影片，先改風險因素與資金用途日曆。願景頁還撐得住；撐不住的是任何把人工智慧基礎建設需求寫成政治與組織都零摩擦的句子。摩擦已被定價。你的工作是證明：在股災讓摩擦變時髦之前，你已在現金跑道（以週計）、第二來源語言與董事會節奏裡預算過摩擦。

短箋結尾只提未來五個工作天的一個請求：一場盡調電話、一個客戶推薦，或一次律師檢視。不要在股災後的星期日晚上要新的條款清單。成年人排工作，投機者排情緒。這個週末屬於一邊聽行情咆哮、一邊仍記賬的成年人。

合夥人也會問：你是否故意在更冷的行情裡融資。用現金跑道（以週計）與里程碑圖回答，而不是用豪語。若你能等六十天而不傷害招聘或實驗，就說出來並出示現金；若不能等，就說十四天內會完成哪些盡調，以合理化續留市場。沉默讀起來像否認，否認讀起來像風險。人工智慧股災週在公開市場懲罰了否認；別把它輸進私人流程。

還有一項星期日衛生：凍結截圖文化。助理會把輝達與Anthropic的K線貼進對話，要你秒回。用三數字框架——行情、流程區間、敏感度——回一次，拒絕用表情符號重談。若認真合夥人要重開價格，就排一場有流程負責人與律師在線的電話。週末聊天管后勤；價格留給會議紀錄。
