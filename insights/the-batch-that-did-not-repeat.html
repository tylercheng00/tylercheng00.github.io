The batch that did not repeat is the result沒有重現的那一批，才是結果

The first batch worked. The second didn't. Calling it "ongoing" and hoping the third one saves the slide is how the lesson gets lost.第一批成了，第二批不成。說「進行中」，指望第三批救回那頁投影片，教訓就丟了。

Biotech生物科技9 Nov 20262026年11月9日4 min read閱讀約 4 分鐘

This is one of the most common weeks in a young lab, and one of the worst reported. The instinct is to say the work is ongoing and wait for a rescue batch. Sometimes the third one works. The company still learned the wrong thing.

Until you can say why it didn't repeat, that failed batch is the result.

"The same protocol" is rarely the same. A new bottle. A column regenerated once too often. Someone who did step four from memory. An incubator a degree high over a weekend nobody logged. Before you invent a theory, list every input that was free to move. You won't know all of them. The ones you can name are already a better note than "we are optimising". Write it down the same week. Memory is generous to the batch that worked.

A failed repeat answers something. Maybe you don't control the step. Maybe the first result was the lucky one. Those are different, and they cost different money. If you can't tell them apart yet, the next experiment should be built to tell them apart, not to redraw the pretty figure as fast as possible. Chasing the figure is how two months and a pile of material disappear, and you are back in the same fog.

The board does not need the chromatography. Four sentences. What you expected. What you saw. What you think it means. What you will do in the next thirty days, and what you will stop. A company that can write that is under control even when the science isn't. "Minor process issues" for three meetings in a row is not. Send the paragraph before the rescue batch. The rescue batch is a guess. The paragraph is the management.

When something fails, people change three variables at once because it feels like action. Freeze the last batch you still trust. Keep the samples and the sheet of settings. The comparison is the thing you own. Without it, a later success is just another story, and you won't know which story to manufacture.

年輕實驗室裡，這是最常見的星期之一，也是報告得最差的之一。本能是說還在做，等一批來救。第三批有時真的成。公司還是學錯了。

在你講得出為甚麼沒重現之前，失敗的那批就是結果。

「同一個方案」很少真的相同。新瓶子。再生太多次的管柱。憑記憶做第四步的人。週末沒人記、高了一度的培養箱。先別編理論，把可以變的輸入列出來。你不會知道全部。叫得出名字的那些，已經比「我們在優化」好。同一個星期寫下來。記憶對成功的那批特別客氣。

失敗的重複回答了某件事。也許這一步你還沒控制住。也許第一次是抽中的。這兩件不一樣，花的錢也不一樣。還分不出來的話，下一個實驗就用來把它們分開，不是盡快把漂亮的圖畫回來。追那張圖，兩個月和一堆材料會不見，你回到同一團霧。

董事會不要層析圖。四句。你以為會怎樣。你看到甚麼。你覺得這是甚麼意思。三十天內做甚麼、停甚麼。寫得出這四句，科學還沒受控，管理是受控的。連續三次會議都說「輕微製程問題」，就不是。拯救的那批還沒出來，先把這段送出去。那一批是猜測。這段才是管理。

東西失敗時，人會一次改三個變數，因為那樣像在做事。把你還信的最後一批凍起來。樣品和參數表留下。比較本身才是你擁有的東西。沒有它，後來的成功只是另一則故事，你不會知道該把哪則做出來。
