::: en
A beautiful figure does a lot of work in a biotech deck. It also hides a lot. The bars are tidy, the stars are where you hope, and the room nods. Then someone asks how many times you ran it, and the answer is a pause.

A figure is the last sentence of a result. It is not the result.

### Start from the failure condition

Before I am impressed by a plot, I want to know what would have made it unimpressive. What was the control. What result would have killed the claim. Did that condition actually get run, or was it designed after the data looked good. A result that cannot say how it would have failed is a picture. Pictures can still be true. They are just not yet evidence.

This is not a request for more statistics on the slide. It is a request for the sentence underneath: we would have abandoned this if we had seen X, and we did not see X, across this many independent runs.

### Independent means a new start

Repeating a measurement on the same sample is not a replicate in the sense that matters. A new day, a new batch, a new pair of hands, ideally. Early companies often cannot afford the ideal. They can still say which kind of repeat they did, and which kind they have not done yet. The honest version is more fundable than the decorated one, because the next person to diligence the figure will ask anyway.

### Show the ugly panel

If one condition did not work, put it in the appendix and label it. A deck with only the winning panel trains the reader to wonder where the rest went. A deck that shows the boundary — it works here, it falls apart there — teaches the reader that you know the edge of your own claim. That is the beginning of a development plan. The winning panel alone is a poster.

### Separate what you measured from what you concluded

"The particle reached the tumour" is a measurement, if you measured it. "Therefore the drug will work in patients" is a conclusion, and it needs a chain. I like those two sentences kept apart, with the missing steps named. The figure can stay. The title of the figure should not do more work than the figure did.

When you present, walk through one figure slowly. What was done. What was compared. What is still only a hope. The investors who are right for a science company would rather understand one result than applaud six.

::: zh
一張漂亮的圖，在生物科技簡報裏做很多工作。它也藏很多東西。柱子很整齊，星號在你希望的地方，房間裏的人點頭。然後有人問你做了幾次，答案是一次停頓。

圖是結果的最後一句。它不是結果。

### 從失敗條件說起

我被一張圖打動之前，想知道甚麼會讓它不打動人。對照組是甚麼。怎樣的結果會殺死這個主張。那個條件是真的做了，還是數據好看之後才設計的。一個說不出自己如何會失敗的結果，是一張圖片。圖片仍可能是真的。它只是還不是證據。

這不是要求投影片上有更多統計。這是要求圖下面的那句話：如果我們看到 X，就會放棄這件事；我們沒有看到 X；獨立做了這麼多次。

### 獨立，是指重新開始

在同一個樣品上重複量度，不是要緊的那種重複。理想是新的一天、新的一批、新的一雙手。早期公司常常負擔不起理想。他們仍然可以說：做了哪一種重複，哪一種還沒有做。誠實的版本比裝飾過的更值得被投，因為下一個審查這張圖的人反正會問。

### 把不好看的那一格也放上

如果有一個條件不成功，放進附錄並標明。一份只有勝出那一格的簡報，會訓練讀者去想其餘的去了哪裏。一份標出邊界的簡報——這裏有效，那裏散掉——讓讀者知道你明白自己主張的邊緣。那是開發計劃的開始。只有勝出的那一格，是一張海報。

### 把量到的和推斷的分開

「粒子到達了腫瘤」是一個量度，如果你真的量了。「因此藥物會在病人身上有效」是一個結論，它需要一條鏈。我喜歡這兩句分開，並把中間缺的步驟寫出來。圖可以留下。圖的標題不該做比圖本身更多的工作。

演示的時候，慢慢走完一張圖。做了甚麼。跟甚麼比。哪一部分仍只是希望。適合科研公司的投資者，寧願弄懂一個結果，而不是為六個結果鼓掌。
