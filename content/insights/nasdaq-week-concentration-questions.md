::: en
By the weekend of 21 November 2026, the Nasdaq had carved out a roughly ten-percent weekly loss, its worst stretch since the early pandemic, while Nvidia’s governance crisis sat at the center of the AI selloff story. Concentration stopped being a footnote. It became the plot. Private companies that sell into a short list of cloud buyers, or that depend on a short list of accelerated-compute suppliers, should assume Monday’s first question is: “Show me the percentage, the names you can name, and the plan if one hub pauses.”

Write the concentration page as a table, not as poetry. Top five customers by trailing twelve-month revenue. Top five suppliers by critical path. Revenue that would slip if a single strategic delayed orders ninety days. Cash runway in weeks under that case. If you cannot fill the cells without counsel, that is the weekend’s real work. Markets just demonstrated how fast a hub can look contested. Your table should assume contestability.

Avoid the false comfort of “everyone is exposed.” Diligence does not grade the industry; it grades your disclosure. Two companies in the same AI tooling niche will get different terms because one arrived with a concentration memo and the other arrived with a shrug. The shrug is now expensive. The memo is cheap by comparison.

If your largest customer also sits on the cap table, say so and schedule the related-party daylight before someone else discovers it in a side letter. Circular edges that felt clever in a bull tape look like hidden leverage in a rout week. Huang-week and Anthropic-week together taught public investors to hunt circles. Private investors will copy the hunt.

Close with a diversification calendar that is honest. “We will add two logos next year” is not a calendar. “Pilot A decision by date, Pilot B security review by date, backup GPU qualification started on date” is a calendar. After a Nasdaq week like this, calendars beat adjectives. Bring the calendar to the first call of the new week, even if the logos are still unpaid pilots. Motion beats mystique when concentration is the fear.

Boards of private companies should ask for the same table this weekend. If your directors only see logo walls, they are not governing concentration; they are governing marketing. Send the table before the next board pack, even if the numbers are uncomfortable. Uncomfortable numbers that arrive early can be mitigated. Uncomfortable numbers that arrive in a partner meeting become terms.

For China-touching revenue or tooling, add an end-use line under each major customer. The Nvidia drama put export-control vocabulary back on the front page. Your concentration memo should show you already speak that vocabulary in operations, not only in risk-factor boilerplate. One honest line per customer beats a page of general compliance poetry.

Treat the weekend as a documentation sprint, not as a strategy offsite. Strategy offsites during rout weeks tend to produce adjectives. Documentation sprints produce artefacts partners can forward: a concentration table, a process-owner line, a comps method note, and a dated cash runway. Forwardable artefacts are how you stay in market when the tape is loud and unkind. Keep the sprint short. Ship by Sunday night. Begin diligence Monday with paper, not with vibes.

到2026年11月21日的週末，那斯達克已留下約一成的週跌——疫情初期以來最差之一——而輝達治理危機坐在人工智慧拋售故事的正中央。集中度不再是腳註，而成了情節。賣給少數雲端買家、或依賴少數加速運算供應商的私人公司，應預設星期一第一個問題是：「給我看比例、能說的名字，以及若一個中樞暫停時的計畫。」

把集中度頁寫成表格，而不是詩。過去十二個月營收前五大客戶；關鍵路徑前五大供應商；單一戰略方延遲九十天訂單會滑落的營收；該情境下以週計算的現金跑道。若沒有律師就填不了格子，那才是週末的真工作。市場剛演示中樞看起來「受爭議」有多快；你的表應預設可爭議性。

別用「大家都暴露」自我安慰。盡調不打產業分數，打的是你的揭露。同一人工智慧工具利基裡的兩家公司，會因為一家帶著集中度備忘、另一家聳肩，而拿到不同條款。聳肩現在很貴；備忘相對便宜。

若最大客戶也在股權表上，就寫明，並在別人於附帶協議裡發現前，安排關聯交易見光。牛市行情裡顯得聰明的圓邊，在股災週看起來像隱藏槓桿。黃仁勳週與Anthropic週一起教會公開投資人去找圓圈；私人投資人會複製這場狩獵。

結尾給一份誠實的分散日曆。「明年加兩個標誌」不是日曆；「試點A於某日決策、試點B於某日完成資安審查、備援圖形處理器認證於某日啟動」才是。經歷這樣的那斯達克週之後，日曆勝過形容詞。即使標誌仍是未付費試點，也把日曆帶進新週第一通電話——恐懼是集中度時，動作勝過神秘。

私人公司董事會這個週末也該要同一張表。若董事只看到標誌牆，他們治理的不是集中度，而是行銷。即使數字刺眼，也在下一次董事會包前寄出。早到的刺眼數字可以緩解；合夥人會議上才出現的刺眼數字，會變成條款。

對有中國接觸的營收或工具，在各大客戶下加一行最終用途。輝達風波把出口管制詞彙送回頭版；你的集中度備忘應顯示你在營運裡已會說這套詞彙，而不只在風險因素套話裡。每位客戶一行老實話，勝過一頁空泛的合規詩。

把週末當成文件衝刺，而不是戰略閉關。股災週的戰略閉關容易只生形容詞；文件衝刺生出合夥人轉發得了的成品：集中度表、流程負責人行、可比方法註、帶日期的現金跑道。行情又吵又兇時，靠轉發得了的成品留在市場。衝刺要短，星期日晚上交件，星期一日以紙而不是感覺開始盡調。
