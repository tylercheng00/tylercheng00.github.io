::: en
On 24 November 2026 the U.S. Food and Drug Administration approved Bacfenssi, a doxycycline hyclate paste applied directly at the surgical site, to reduce infections after colorectal surgery. The agency lists it among its notable approvals as the first locally applied treatment for that purpose. In the phase 3 SHIELD II trial reported by trade press on 25 November, surgical-site infections occurred in 3.8% of patients who received the paste on top of standard perioperative care and in 9.5% of those who received standard care alone. Read from Hong Kong on 27 November, with the biotech tape busy and attention on financings, the approval is a useful reminder for private teams that the route of delivery can be the product, and that diligence on such products happens partly in the operating theatre.

Begin with the numbers, because the framing matters. A fall from 9.5% to 3.8% is described in some coverage as about a 60% reduction. That is the relative figure. The absolute difference is 5.7 percentage points, which means roughly one fewer infection for every seventeen or eighteen patients treated. Both figures are correct, and both belong in a document. A partner or investor who is used to oncology survival curves may find the absolute figure modest. A hospital pharmacist who counts the cost of readmission may find the same figure significant. A founder should present both and say which audience each is for.

Then consider the route. A paste placed in the wound is not a pill. It has to be stored, handled by the surgical team, applied at the right moment and documented. The effect depends partly on the chemistry and partly on whether the procedure is followed. That is why regulators treat such products as drugs while also paying attention to how they are used. For a private company with a similar profile, the diligence file should therefore include an instruction sheet written for operating-room staff, a training plan, a record of deviations in the pivotal study and a description of how the product behaves if the surgeon applies less or more than intended.

Manufacturing is a separate question. A semi-solid formulation raises its own control points: viscosity, homogeneity, container-closure performance, sterility assurance and shelf-life under real storage conditions. Those items often cause more delay than the clinical package. If your Asia-based story involves a contract manufacturer in a different jurisdiction from your trial sites, put the batch-release chain on one page and show who signs at each step.

Next comes the buyer. Many hospital products are bought by committees that look at cost per case avoided, not at price per vial. If a colorectal surgery programme performs a few hundred cases a year, the committee will want to know how many infections the product would prevent, what an infection costs in extra bed days, and what additional work the surgical team must absorb. A founder who can answer those questions in a short table is better prepared than one who offers only a hazard ratio.

There is also a point about trial design that applies beyond this product. SHIELD II compared the paste plus standard care with standard care alone, which is the comparison a hospital faces in practice. When you design a pivotal study, ask whether your control arm reflects what the real decision-maker would otherwise do. A trial that wins against a weak comparator can be harder to defend in a pricing conversation than a trial with a smaller win against the real alternative.

None of this is a view on the commercial prospects of the approved product or on the company behind it. It is a checklist drawn from a label that appeared this week. When the product is delivered locally, the data room should show the pharmacology, the procedure, the factory and the hospital buyer on the same table. This note is a research-desk view for founders, not investment advice and not medical guidance.

2026年11月24日，美國食品藥物管理局（FDA）批准了Bacfenssi，這是一款直接塗抹於手術部位的多西環素糊劑，用來減少結直腸手術後的感染。該機構把它列入重要批准名單，指它是同類用途中首個局部使用的治療。據行業媒體11月25日報道，在第三期SHIELD II試驗中，在標準圍手術期護理之上加用這款糊劑的患者，手術部位感染率為3.8%，只接受標準護理的患者則為9.5%。香港11月27日讀來，在生技行情熱鬧、各方都關注融資的時候，這項批准對私人公司是個有用的提醒：給藥途徑有時本身就是產品，而這類產品的盡職調查，有一部分是在手術室裏做的。

先看數字，因為怎樣表述很重要。從9.5%降到3.8%，部分報道稱為「減少約六成」，這是相對數字；絕對差距是5.7個百分點，也就是大約每治療十七至十八名患者，少一宗感染。兩個數字都正確，也都應該寫進文件。習慣看腫瘤存活曲線的合作夥伴，可能覺得絕對差距不算大；要計算再入院成本的醫院藥劑師，卻可能認為同一數字相當可觀。創辦人宜兩者並列，並說明各自是給哪類讀者看的。

再看途徑。放進傷口的糊劑不是藥丸，要儲存、由手術團隊處理、在正確時間塗抹並記錄。療效一部分來自化學成分，一部分取決於手術流程有沒有照做。所以監管機構既把這類產品當藥物審批，也關注它如何被使用。類似的私人公司，盡職文件裏就應該有一份寫給手術室人員的使用說明、一份培訓計劃、一份關鍵性研究中的偏差紀錄，以及外科醫生塗得較少或較多時，產品會有何表現的說明。

生產是另一個問題。半固體製劑有自己的控制點：黏度、均勻度、容器密封性能、無菌保證，以及在真實儲存條件下的保質期。這些項目拖慢進度的情況，常比臨床資料還多。如果你的亞洲故事涉及的合約生產商，與試驗地點不在同一個司法管轄區，請把放行鏈放在同一頁，寫明每一步由誰簽字。

接着是買家。很多醫院產品是由委員會採購的，他們看的是每避免一宗感染的成本，而不是每支藥的價錢。如果一個結直腸外科項目每年做幾百宗手術，委員會會問：這產品可以預防多少宗感染？一宗感染多佔用幾個住院日？手術團隊又要多做甚麼？創辦人能夠用一張簡短的表格回答這些問題，比只拿出風險比的人準備得更充分。

還有一點關於試驗設計，並不限於這個產品。SHIELD II比較的是「糊劑加標準護理」與「單用標準護理」，正是醫院在現實中面對的選擇。設計關鍵性研究時，要問自己：對照組是不是真正決策者原本會做的事？對着一個弱的對照組取勝的試驗，到了定價討論時，可能比對着真實替代方案、勝幅較小的試驗更難辯護。

以上不是對這款獲批產品或其背後公司商業前景的看法，只是從本週出現的一份標籤整理出來的清單。當產品是局部給藥時，資料室應該把藥理、手術流程、工廠和醫院買家擺在同一張桌子上。本文是研究桌面對創辦人的看法，不構成投資建議，也不是醫療指引。
