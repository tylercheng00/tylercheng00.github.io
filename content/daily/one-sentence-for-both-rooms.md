::: en
I spend my time in two rooms. In one, people talk about whether a result will repeat, whether a batch is the same batch, whether a model shows what it claims. In the other, people talk about whether a round will close, whether a structure can be owned, whether a document can be signed. The rooms have different manners. They do not get to have different facts.

The useful discipline, at the end of a month of thinking about both, is to find one sentence that is true in each room, and to refuse any story that needs the sentence to change when you walk down the corridor.

### The sentence is a fact, not a slogan

Not "we are transforming healthcare". A fact at the size of a single breath. We can make this particle at this scale, three times, inside this specification, and we have not yet shown the next scale. Two customers are most of the revenue, and the round is to make that untrue. The operating company is owned by the holding company through these contracts, and those contracts are what an investor would actually be buying.

If the sentence embarrasses you in the lab, it is too loose. If it embarrasses you in front of an investor, it is too honest for the deck you were about to show, which means the deck is the problem.

### Read it aloud in the other room

Take the sentence the scientists agreed and read it, without softening, to someone who thinks in money. Take the sentence the investors nodded at and read it back to the person who runs the experiment. Watch which words they will not repeat. Those words are where the story has inflated. Cut them. What remains is the only version you should put on a slide, in a data room, and in a board pack. One version. The moment you keep two, you have created a job for the person whose work is to catch you.

### When the sentence changes, say it changed

Facts move. A batch fails. A customer leaves. A structure you thought was clean needs a new agreement. The sentence should be allowed to change, in public, on purpose. What it should not do is drift, so that the deck, the lab book and the last board minute are three generations of the same claim. Date it. When you update it, show the old one. People trust a company that amends a sentence more than a company that never seems to need to.

### It is a management tool before it is a message

The sentence is not a tagline for the website. It is the test for the next decision. Does this hire make the sentence more true, or does it decorate the company while the sentence stays fragile? Does this experiment change the sentence, or does it produce a figure beside it? Does this round fund the sentence, or a larger sentence you have not earned?

I do not think the two rooms will ever speak the same language. I think they can be asked to sign the same sentence. That is enough. A company that can do it is surprisingly hard to confuse, which is a more useful advantage than it sounds.

::: zh
我的時間花在兩個房間。一個房間裏，人們談一個結果會不會重現、一批是不是同一批、一個模型有沒有顯示它聲稱的東西。另一個房間裏，人們談一輪融資會不會成交、一個架構能不能被擁有、一份文件能不能簽。兩個房間有不同的禮儀。它們不可以有不同的事實。

想了兩種工作一個月之後，有用的紀律是：找出一句在每個房間都是真的話，並拒絕任何需要你走過走廊時把這句話改掉的故事。

### 這句話是事實，不是口號

不是「我們正在改變醫療」。一句一個呼吸那麼長的事實。我們可以在這個規模做出這種粒子，三次，落在這個質量標準內，而下一個規模我們還沒有顯示。兩個客戶佔了大部分收入，而這一輪是為了讓這句話不再為真。營運公司透過這些合約由控股公司擁有，而那些合約才是投資者實際會買到的東西。

如果這句話在實驗室裏讓你尷尬，它就太鬆。如果它在投資者面前讓你尷尬，它對你正要展示的簡報來說就太誠實，那表示問題在簡報。

### 在另一個房間大聲讀出來

把科學家同意的句子，不軟化，讀給一個用錢思考的人聽。把投資者點頭的句子，讀回給做實驗的那個人。看哪些詞他們不願意重複。那些詞就是故事膨脹的地方。把它們剪掉。剩下的，才是你應該放上投影片、放進資料室、放進董事會文件的唯一版本。一個版本。一旦你保留兩個，你就為那個工作是抓住你的人創造了職位。

### 句子改變時，說它改變了

事實會動。一批失敗。一個客戶離開。一個你以為乾淨的架構需要一份新協議。這句話應該被允許改變，公開地、有意地。它不該漂移，以致簡報、實驗記錄和上一份董事會記錄是同一個主張的三代。給它日期。更新的時候，把舊的也亮出來。人們信任一家會修訂一句話的公司，多過一家看起來永遠不需要修訂的公司。

### 它先是管理工具，然後才是訊息

這句話不是網站的標語。它是下一個決定的測試。這個招聘會讓句子更真，還是在句子仍然脆弱時裝飾公司？這個實驗會改變句子，還是在旁邊產出一張圖？這一輪融資的是這句話，還是一句你還沒有贏得的更大的話？

我不認為兩個房間會說同一種語言。我認為可以要求它們簽同一句話。這就夠了。做得到的公司，出奇地不容易被搞亂。這個優勢比聽起來更有用。
