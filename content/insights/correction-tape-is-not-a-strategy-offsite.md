By 22 November 2026, the story of the prior sessions was clear enough for a Sunday desk: major U.S. indexes had fallen into correction territory, the Nasdaq had suffered a historic bad week, and AI leaders from Nvidia to newly public Anthropic had taught investors how quickly narrative premium can vanish. Founders love to respond with offsites. Offsites feel like control. They rarely produce the artefacts diligence needs.

Cancel the optional brainstorm. Keep the mandatory documentation. Concentration table. Cash runway in weeks. Customer reference list with dates of last contact. China or export sentences with owners. Comps method with timestamps. That packet is how you stay raise-ready when the tape is a correction. Vision boards can wait until the packet exists.

Strategy language during corrections tends to inflate. Everyone discovers discipline, focus, and “back to fundamentals” in the same forty-eight hours. Investors have heard the chorus. They have not always seen the checklist. Be the company that sends the checklist first and the adjectives second.

If you must gather the team, gather them to assign owners for each artefact with Friday deadlines. Meeting notes should read like a manufacturing traveler, not like a manifesto. Manifestos leak into decks as slogans. Travelers become data-room folders.

End the day with one outbound email to the lead partner: here is what we are shipping this week; here is what the public tape does not change in our operating plan. That email is the opposite of an offsite. It is also what corrections reward.

Keep the day hook in the first paragraph and the operating proof in the rest. Investors forgive topical openings; they do not forgive empty middles. Add dates, owners, and artefacts that can survive a partner forward. If a control does not yet exist, name the date it will. If a risk cannot be closed this week, name the mitigation already funded in cash runway weeks. Write as if the next associate will paste your note into an investment committee chat without you in the room.

到2026年11月22日，前幾個交易時段的故事對星期日桌面已經夠清楚：美國主要指數滑進回檔，那斯達克度過歷史級差週，從輝達到新上市的Anthropic都在教投資人——敘事溢價消失有多快。創辦人愛用閉關回應。閉關感覺像掌控；卻很少生出盡調需要的成品。

取消可選的腦力激盪，留下必做的文件：集中度表、以週計的現金跑道、附上次聯絡日期的客戶推薦名單、有負責人的中國或出口句子、有時間戳的可比方法。行情處於回檔時，靠這包材料保持可融資；願景板等這包存在再談。

回檔期間的戰略語言容易膨脹。所有人在同一四十八小時發現紀律、聚焦與「回到基本面」。投資人聽過合唱，卻未必見過清單。做先寄清單、後寄形容詞的那家公司。

若必須把團隊叫來，就為每件成品指派負責人與星期五期限。會議紀錄應像製造流程卡，而不是宣言。宣言會漏進簡報變成口號；流程卡會變成資料室資料夾。

一天結束時給首席合夥人一封外寄信：本週交什麼；公開行情不改變我們營運計畫的哪裡。那封信是閉關的反面，卻也是回檔會獎賞的東西。
