::: en
Dual-track sounds sophisticated. It fails when one track is real and the other is a rumour. Assign owners, document requests, and decision dates for both. Tell the board which track is ahead each week.

Buyers and underwriters notice when one story is a prop. Use dual-track as discipline, not as decoration.

::: zh
雙軌聽起來高明。若一條是真、一條是傳聞，就會失敗。兩邊都要有負責人、文件要求、決策日。每週告訴董事會哪條在前。

買家和承銷商看得出哪條故事是道具。把雙軌當紀律，不當裝飾。
