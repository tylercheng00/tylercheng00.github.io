::: en
Teams celebrate the best in-house run. Investors quietly ask whether anyone else can run it. Budget samples and time for a transfer early, even if the partner is only a CRO or a hospital lab.

Document what broke. That list is more valuable than another perfect curve from the same hands. When you later say the method is ready, you will mean ready for other people — which is what manufacturing and clinics require.

::: zh
團隊慶祝最好的內部實驗。投資者安靜地問：別人能不能跑。早一點為轉移留樣本和時間，即使夥伴只是 CRO 或醫院實驗室。

記下斷在哪裏。那份清單比同一雙手的又一條完美曲線更有價值。你之後說方法就緒時，意思會是：對其他人就緒——這才是製造和臨床需要的。
