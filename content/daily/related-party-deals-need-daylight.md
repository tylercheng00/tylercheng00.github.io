::: en
Family loans, founder leases, sales to a relative's company — these are common in young firms and toxic when they appear late. Put them in a schedule. Say what market terms would look like. Say what you will unwind before a listing or a close.

Buyers care less about the existence of a related-party item than about surprise and control. A one-page note from counsel beats a defensive meeting after someone else discovers the invoice.

::: zh
家族借款、創辦人租約、賣給親戚公司——年輕公司裏常見，出現得晚就有毒。列成表。寫市場條款會是怎樣。寫上市或交割前你會解除甚麼。

買家在乎的往往不是關聯交易存在，而是驚喜和控制權。律師的一頁說明，勝過別人發現發票之後的防衛會議。
