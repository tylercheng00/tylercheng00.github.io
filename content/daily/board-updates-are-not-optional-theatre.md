::: en
Founders sometimes skip board notes because nothing big happened. That is usually when the board most needs a line: what moved, what did not, what you need from them.

Keep it boring on purpose. Cash runway in weeks. One hiring or contracting decision. One diligence item that is stuck. One ask. If you only write when there is a win, the board learns that silence means trouble.

A weekly or biweekly note also protects you. When a later debate starts about what we knew when, you have a dated trail. Theatre is a polished deck with no numbers. A plain email with three facts is work.

::: zh
創辦人有時因為沒有大事就跳過董事會更新。往往正是董事會最需要一行字的時候：甚麼動了、甚麼沒動、你需要他們做甚麼。

刻意寫得無聊。現金跑道還有幾週。一個人事或外包決定。一項卡住的盡職審查。一個請求。若只在有勝仗時才寫，董事會會學會：沉默等於出事。

每週或隔週的短訊也保護你。後來爭論當時知道甚麼時，你有帶日期的痕跡。表演是沒有數字的精美簡報。三件事實的普通電郵才是工作。
