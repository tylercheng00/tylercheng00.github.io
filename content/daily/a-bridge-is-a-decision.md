::: en
A small instrument, existing investors, money in quickly, the real round later. Sometimes that is exactly right. Often the bridge is where the company quietly changes what it promised, and doesn't write the new promise down.

What risk is supposed to be gone before this money runs out? If you can't name it, you don't have a bridge. You have a smaller round that will be followed by another smaller round, and in six months the story will be about resilience. Resilience is real. It is a weak reason to take the dilution.

Write the far bank in the document. This note is meant to get the company to X. An audit signed. A batch released. A customer renewed. A structure a new investor can actually buy. If X is missing, choose it before you choose the discount.

The people who already own the company can move fast, which is the point. They can also say yes because saying no is awkward, and resent the terms later. Speed is not a substitute for a short document they have actually read. Cap or discount, maturity, and what happens if the next round does not come. Everyone hopes the last sentence will not matter. The document is for the world in which it does.

Don't spend bridge money like round money. It is for the path to the named fact, not for the plan you would have funded if the larger round had arrived. Hiring the whole commercial team on a bridge, because "we'll raise into it", is how the bridge becomes the business.

When you later raise the round you wanted, the new investor will ask why there was a bridge. "Timing" is not an answer. The answer is the fact you were buying time to create, and whether you created it. If you did, the bridge becomes a boring footnote, which is the best available outcome. If you didn't, say what you learned. A bridge you pretend did not happen is a gap diligence finds in an afternoon.

一份小工具、現有的投資者、錢快點進來、真正的一輪在後面。有時這完全對。常常，過橋是公司悄悄改了承諾、又沒把新的寫下來的地方。

這筆錢用完之前，哪個風險該消失？講不出來，你就沒有橋。你有一輪較小的，後面還會再來一輪較小的，六個月後故事會變成關於韌性。韌性是真的。它是接受攤薄的一個弱理由。

把對岸寫進文件。這張票據是為了讓公司到達 X。審計簽了。批次放行了。客戶續了。架構變成新投資者買得到的東西。X 不在，先選它，再選折扣。

已經是股東的人可以很快，這正是意義。他們也可以因為說不很尷尬而說好，然後在條款上事後不滿。速度代替不了一份他們真的讀過的短文件。上限或折扣、到期、以及下一輪不來會怎樣。人人希望最後一句不要緊。文件是為它要緊的那個世界寫的。

不要把過橋的錢花得像正式的一輪。它是為了走向那個被點名的事實，不是為了「大的一輪到了你就會做」的計劃。在過橋上聘一整個商業團隊，因為「我們會融進這筆開支」，過橋就變成了生意本身。

後來融你真正想要的那一輪，新投資者會問為甚麼有過橋。「時機」不是答案。答案是你用時間去做出的那個事實，以及你有沒有做出來。做出來了，過橋會變成一個悶的腳註，這是能得到的最好結果。沒有，就講你學到甚麼。一個你假裝沒發生的過橋，盡職審查一個下午就找到。
