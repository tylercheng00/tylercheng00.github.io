::: en
Founders sometimes talk about a CRO as if the science were parked safely off-site. The assay is "with them". The tox study is "running". That language hides the real object: a slot in another company's schedule, with change orders, sample queues and a project manager who has five other sponsors.

I read a CRO plan the way I read a manufacturing claim. Who owns the method. What happens when a batch fails. How many weeks of buffer exist before your financing story breaks. If the deck only shows a start date and a hopeful end date, you have a wish, not a plan.

Transfer is where time disappears. Your lab's protocol and their lab's protocol are not the same object until someone has run both and written down the differences. Skipping that step to "save a month" often costs a quarter when the first formal run does not match the in-house numbers you already showed investors.

Ask for the queue position in writing. Ask what slips if another sponsor pays for priority. Ask which results you will see weekly, and which you will only see at the end. Then put those answers next to your raise calendar. If the only way the story works is a perfect CRO month, change the story or change the work.

Outsourcing can be the right call. It does not make the experiment someone else's problem. It makes the experiment a contract, a queue and a set of shared definitions. Treat that as science, or the calendar will teach you later.

::: zh
創辦人有時談起 CRO，像科學已經安全地停在外面。檢測「在他們那裏」。毒理「在跑」。這種說法藏起真正的物件：另一家公司日程裏的一個空位，帶着變更單、樣本隊列，以及同時管五個 sponsor 的項目經理。

我讀 CRO 計劃的方式，像讀製造主張。方法誰擁有。一批失敗時怎麼辦。在融資故事破裂之前，還有幾週緩衝。投影片只有開始日和一個樂觀結束日，那是願望，不是計劃。

轉移最耗時間。你實驗室的規程和他們的規程，在有人兩邊都跑過、並寫下差異之前，不是同一個物件。為「省一個月」跳過這步，常常在第一次正式結果對不上你已給投資者看過的內部數字時，賠掉一個季度。

要求書面寫明排隊位置。問另一個 sponsor 加錢插隊時甚麼會延。問每週你會看到哪些結果、哪些要到結束才看得到。然後把這些答案放在融資日曆旁邊。若故事只能靠 CRO 完美的一個月成立，就改故事，或改工作。

外包可以是對的決定。它不會讓實驗變成別人的問題。它讓實驗變成合同、隊列和一組共享定義。把這些當科學看，否則日曆稍後會教你。
