::: en
23 November 2026 will be filed under Nvidia: Jensen Huang reinstated, board reversed, Washington signaled, premarket ripped. Your inbound Slack will fill with takes. None of those takes help a partner forward you inside their firm. What helps is the same artefact that helps on quiet Tuesdays: an eight-to-twelve-line blurb that states who you are, what you sell, why now, what proof exists, and what you want next. Write it today anyway.

Reversal days create a false sense that context replaces introduction. It does not. An associate who spent the afternoon reading Huang statements still needs language that survives a paste into an investment-committee chat. Keep geopolitics out of the blurb unless geopolitics is your product. Keep the Huang sentence, if any, in a separate market note. Mixing them makes you look like a newsletter.

Use the energy of the day to refresh proofs, not adjectives. If a customer call landed, name it. If a cohort metric moved, name it. If nothing moved, say the operating plan is unchanged and attach the concentration and cash pages you should already have from the rout week. Forwardable calm beats topical excitement.

Ask for one concrete next step: a thirty-minute diligence call, a security questionnaire, or a customer reference window. Do not ask for a rewritten term sheet because Nvidia’s board flipped. Term sheets move on your artefacts. Boards of other companies move on their own clocks.

End the blurb with contact details for the process owner you named during the interim week. Continuity across Huang-out and Huang-in is itself a diligence signal. Companies that keep the same clerk when the industry’s hub reverses look adult. Adults get the Tuesday meetings after Monday’s fireworks.

Keep the day hook in the first paragraph and the operating proof in the rest. Investors forgive topical openings; they do not forgive empty middles. Add dates, owners, and artefacts that can survive a partner forward. If a control does not yet exist, name the date it will. If a risk cannot be closed this week, name the mitigation already funded in cash runway weeks. Write as if the next associate will paste your note into an investment committee chat without you in the room.

If you already sent a blurb last week, send a delta cover of five lines instead of a full rewrite: what changed in proof, what did not change in ask, and who owns diligence this week. Partners punish unnecessary rewrites on noisy days because they cannot tell signal from panic. A delta cover shows control.

Also attach the one artefact most likely to be requested after Huang headlines: either the key-person continuity page or the export-owner line. Make the attach optional in the email so mobile readers are not blocked. Optional attaches still signal readiness. Readiness is the point of writing on a reversal day when everyone else is only reacting.

Treat the weekend as a documentation sprint, not as a strategy offsite. Strategy offsites during rout weeks tend to produce adjectives. Documentation sprints produce artefacts partners can forward: a concentration table, a process-owner line, a comps method note, and a dated cash runway. Forwardable artefacts are how you stay in market when the tape is loud and unkind. Keep the sprint short. Ship by Sunday night. Begin diligence Monday with paper, not with vibes.

2026年11月23日會被歸檔在輝達名下：黃仁勳復職、董事會急轉彎、華府表態、盤前暴衝。你的即時訊息會被各種解讀塞滿。那些解讀幫不了合夥人把你轉發進公司內部。有用的仍是安靜星期二也該有的成品：八到十二行簡介——你是誰、賣什麼、為何是現在、有何證據、下一步要什麼。今天照樣寫。

逆轉日會製造錯覺，以為上下文可以取代介紹。不能。助理下午讀完黃仁勳聲明，仍需要一段貼進投資委員會對話也活得下來的文字。除非地緣政治就是你的產品，否則別寫進簡介；黃仁勳句若需要，放在分開的市場短箋。混在一起會讓你像電子報。

用這天的能量刷新證據，而不是形容詞。若有客戶電話，點名；若群組指標動了，點名；若沒動，就說營運計畫不變，並附上股災週就該備好的集中度與現金頁。可轉發的冷靜，勝過合時宜的興奮。

只要求一個具體下一步：三十分鐘盡調、一份資安問卷，或一扇客戶推薦窗。不要因為輝達董事會翻轉就要求重寫條款清單。條款清單跟你的成品走；別家公司的董事會跟他們自己的時鐘走。

簡介結尾放上你在代理週點名的流程負責人聯絡方式。黃仁勳離開與回來之間保持同一書記，本身就是盡調訊號。產業中樞急轉彎時仍留著同一書記的公司，看起來像成年人。成年人能在星期一煙火之後，拿到星期二的會議。

把當日新聞鉤子放在首段，其餘留給營運證據。投資人能原諒有時事的開場，不能原諒空洞的中段。補上日期、負責人，以及經得起合夥人轉發的成品。若控制措施尚不存在，寫出將建立的日期；若風險本週關不掉，寫出已用現金跑道（以週計）預算的緩解。寫的時候假設下一個助理會把你的短箋貼進投資委員會對話，而你不在場。


若上週已寄過簡介，改寄五行程差封面，而不是整份重寫：證據改了什麼、要價沒改什麼、本週盡調由誰負責。嘈雜日子裡，合夥人會懲罰不必要的重寫，因為分不清訊號與恐慌。程差封面顯示你控得住。

也附上黃仁勳頭條後最可能被要的一件成品：關鍵人連續性頁，或出口負責人行。郵件裡標成可選附件，避免手機閱讀被卡住。可選附件仍能傳達準備度；在別人都只會反應的逆轉日，準備度才是寫作目的。

把週末當成文件衝刺，而不是戰略閉關。股災週的戰略閉關容易只生形容詞；文件衝刺生出合夥人轉發得了的成品：集中度表、流程負責人行、可比方法註、帶日期的現金跑道。行情又吵又兇時，靠轉發得了的成品留在市場。衝刺要短，星期日晚上交件，星期一日以紙而不是感覺開始盡調。
