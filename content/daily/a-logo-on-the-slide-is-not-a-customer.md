::: en
Logo walls are easy to build and hard to defend. A pilot, an unpaid pilot, a letter of intent, and a paid contract are four different objects. Investors who have been burned will ask which one you mean within two minutes.

Write the relationship in one sentence on the appendix page, not only in the wall. Paid since which month. What they buy. Whether they can cancel in thirty days. If the answer is they love us with no invoice, the logo is branding, not revenue proof.

You can still show early users. Call them early users. Accuracy here saves a diligence call later, when someone checks the bank and finds the story thinner than the slide.

::: zh
Logo 牆好建、難辯護。試點、未付費試點、意向書、已付費合同，是四種物件。被燙過的投資者兩分鐘內就會問你指哪一種。

在附錄用一句話寫關係，不要只靠牆。從哪個月開始付費。買甚麼。能否三十天取消。若答案是他們很喜歡我們卻沒有發票，logo 是品牌，不是收入證明。

早期用戶仍可展示。就叫早期用戶。這裏寫準，能省掉後來有人查銀行、發現故事比投影片瘦的那通電話。
