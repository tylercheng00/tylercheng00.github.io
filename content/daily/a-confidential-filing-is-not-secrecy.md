::: en
Founders hear that a foreign company can first submit a draft registration statement to the US regulator without the world reading it, and they relax. The relaxation is half right. The public is not reading it yet. Your lawyers, your auditors, your underwriters and, eventually, the regulator's staff are. Confidential does not mean unfinished. It means not yet public.

Treating the confidential draft as a private sketch is how companies lose the only advantage the confidential process gives them.

### The hard pages are the point

The pages that hurt are the ones worth writing early. Related-party transactions. What the offshore company actually owns. Revenue that is concentrated in two customers. A control weakness the auditor has already mentioned in a private letter. Risks that are specific to you, not copied from a peer's filing and lightly edited.

A confidential first draft is the moment to put those pages in, while the comments you receive are still private. If you leave them out to "keep it clean", the comments simply arrive later, louder, and closer to the day you wanted to print a prospectus.

### You still need the numbers to tie

Confidential is not a waiver of arithmetic. The financials, the share count, the use of proceeds and the business section have to describe one company. If management accounts and the audited numbers tell two stories, do not file either story in the hope that the difference can be explained in a response letter. Write the explanation first, until it is short enough to survive being moved into the document itself.

### Decide what you are willing to see in public

Everything in the confidential draft should be something you can imagine on a website. Some details can be negotiated, and some wording will change. The underlying fact will not become secret again. If there is a fact the company cannot bear to see in print, that fact is a problem for the listing, not a drafting problem. Surface it in the room, before it surfaces in a comment.

### Use the privacy to fix the process

The useful gift of a non-public first review is time without a share price and without headlines. Spend it on the things that actually move: closing the audit gaps, signing the contracts the document assumes, aligning the legal chart with the operating chart. Do not spend it on sixteen rounds of adjectives.

A confidential filing is a dress rehearsal with a serious audience. Come with the play written. The privacy is so you can rewrite in peace, not so you can walk on stage still holding an outline.

::: zh
創辦人聽說外國公司可以先向美國監管機構遞交註冊聲明草稿，而世界還讀不到，於是放鬆下來。這個放鬆對了一半。公眾暫時還不在讀。你的律師、核數師、承銷商，以及最終監管機構的人員，在讀。保密不是未完成。它是尚未公開。

把保密草稿當成私人素描，是公司丟掉保密程序唯一優勢的方式。

### 難的那些頁，才是重點

讓人痛的頁，值得早寫。關聯方交易。境外公司實際上擁有甚麼。集中在兩個客戶的收入。核數師已經在私人信件裏提過的監控弱點。屬於你自己的風險，而不是從同業文件抄來、稍微改過的風險。

保密的第一稿，是把這些頁放進去的時刻，因為你收到的意見仍然是私密的。如果你為了「保持乾淨」把它們留空，意見只會來得更晚、更大聲，而且更接近你想印招股書的那一天。

### 數字仍然要對得上

保密不是算術的豁免。財務、股數、資金用途和業務一節，必須描述同一家公司。如果管理賬和經審計的數字講兩個故事，不要遞交其中一個，希望分別可以在回覆信裏解釋。先把解釋寫出來，直到它短得可以搬進文件本身。

### 決定你願意在公開場合看到甚麼

保密草稿裏的每一件事，都應該是你可以想像出現在網站上的。有些細節可以談，有些措辭會改。底下的事實不會再變回秘密。如果有一個事實是公司無法承受印出來的，那是上市的問題，不是起草的問題。在它變成一條意見之前，先在房間裏把它擺上桌。

### 用這段私隱去修流程

非公開的第一輪審閱，真正的禮物是一段沒有股價、也沒有標題的時間。把它花在真正會動的事情上：補上審計缺口、簽下文件所假設的合約、讓法律架構圖和營運架構圖對上。不要把它花在十六輪形容詞上。

保密遞交是一場有認真觀眾的彩排。帶着寫好的劇本來。私隱是讓你能安靜地改寫，不是讓你拿着大綱走上舞台。
