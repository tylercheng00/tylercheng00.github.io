An introduction is a scarce thing. Spend it once.一次介紹很珍貴，只該用一次

The person introducing you is spending their own name. Most founders treat that as if it were free.幫你介紹的人，花的是他自己的名字。很多創辦人把它當成不用錢。

US capital markets美國資本市場Biotech生物科技9 Nov 20262026年11月9日3 min read閱讀約 3 分鐘

A good introduction is someone telling a person they respect: this is worth your time, and I'm willing to be associated with how it goes. That stock is small. I would rather make one that fits than five that are "worth a chat".

The wrong investor, warmly introduced, is worse than no introduction. They take the meeting, they pass, and the person who introduced you now remembers the pass. Warmth doesn't fix a mismatch of stage, cheque size, or what that investor actually knows how to judge.

Before I ask anyone, I want three sentences. Why this investor. What they have backed that looks like this. What we want from the meeting, which is usually a decision or a next step, not "synergies".

Write the email they will forward. Five or six lines. What you do, why now, why them, what you want. No life story. If they have to compose your case, they will delay, or write something vaguer than you would have. Both are on you.

If someone has passed, don't route a second introduction through a different friend and hope the first one is forgotten. It won't be. The circles between Hong Kong, the mainland and the US funds that look at both are smaller than people think. Come back when something real has changed, and say what.

Afterwards, tell the introducer what happened. Two lines. They met us, they passed, for this reason. Or they asked for the model and we sent it Thursday. People who never hear the ending stop introducing. Not out of spite. Because their name went into a hole.

一次好的介紹，是有人對他尊重的人說：這值得你的時間，而且我願意跟結果連在一起。這筆存款很少。我寧願做一次合適的，不做五次「聊聊也好」。

介紹給錯的人，即使很熱心，也比不介紹差。他們見了，然後拒絕。介紹你的人記得的是這次拒絕。階段不對、支票大小不對、或者他根本不懂判斷你這一行，熱心補不了。

我開口之前想要三句。為甚麼是這個人。他們投過甚麼跟你們相像的。這次會要他們做甚麼，通常是一個決定或下一步，不是「協同」。

他會轉發的那封郵件，你自己寫。五六行。做甚麼、為甚麼現在、為甚麼是他、你想要甚麼。不要人生故事。要他幫你組織說法，他就會拖，或者寫得比你更含糊。兩樣都是你的問題。

人家已經拒絕了，不要再經另一個朋友介紹一次，希望第一次被忘掉。不會。香港、內地、和同時看兩邊的美國基金，圈子比你以為的小。等有實在的改變再回來，並講改變了甚麼。

見完告訴介紹人結果。兩行就夠。見了，拒絕了，原因是這個。或者他們要了模型，星期四送了。一直不知道下文的人，會停止介紹。不是記仇。是自己的名字掉進洞裡。

— 鄭迪珉 Tyler Tik Man Cheng
