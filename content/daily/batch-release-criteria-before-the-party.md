::: en
Define pass and fail before the big run. Share it with the partner who makes the material. When a batch sits in limbo, the fundraising story sits with it.

Clear criteria also protect the science team from moving goalposts after the fact. Write them while heads are calm.

::: zh
在大實驗之前定義合格與不合格。和製作物料的夥伴共享。當一批卡在不明狀態，融資故事也會一起卡住。

清晰標準也保護科學團隊，免於事後移動門柱。趁大家冷靜時寫下。
