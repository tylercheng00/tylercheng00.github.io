The data room is the pitch資料室本身就是簡報

I open the folder before I open the deck. If the cap table takes ten minutes to find, I already know how the next month will feel.我會先開資料夾，才開簡報。股權表要找十分鐘的話，後面一個月大概甚麼感覺，我已經知道了。

US capital markets美國資本市場8 Nov 20262026年11月8日4 min read閱讀約 4 分鐘

Founders will spend three weeks on a deck and then send the data room as a zip called final_v7. Half the contracts are missing and there are three cap tables. I open the zip before I read the slides now. The slides are the introduction. The folder is where someone decides whether to book another meeting.

I don't need everything on day one. I need four things, named so a stranger can find them. The cap table, with the options and anything convertible. Two years of numbers, and if they are only management accounts, say that on the first page. The contracts that matter: the big customers, the supplier you can't replace, the IP assignments. And one page showing which company actually does the work.

If one of those is still being fixed, write it at the top. Silence reads as if you hoped I wouldn't ask.

"Contract.pdf" and "Contract (2).pdf" will waste a lawyer's afternoon. Date the file, name the other side, put the old versions in a folder called old. It sounds petty. It is the difference between a question answered on Monday and a question that sits until someone reconstructs what you meant.

Someone inside the company has to own the room. Not the advisor, and not "the team". When the deck and the accounts disagree, that person should have seen it already. They will be asked. The pause is part of the answer.

Most of the reading happens while you are in another meeting. There is no chance to explain the footnote. Get the room into shape before the week you are trying to close. That is the week you discover last year's option grants were never written down.

創辦人可以花三個星期磨簡報，然後資料室是一個叫 final_v7 的壓縮檔。合約缺一半，股權表有三份。我現在先開壓縮檔，才看投影片。投影片是開場。人家決定要不要再約一次，是看那個資料夾。

第一天我不要全部。我要四樣，而且外人找得到。股權表，連購股權和可換股的東西都在。兩年數字，如果只是管理賬，第一頁寫明。真正要緊的合約：大客戶、換不掉的供應商、知識產權轉讓。再加一頁，哪家公司才是在做生意的那家。

其中一樣還在改，就寫在最上面。你不說，我會當成你希望我沒問。

「合約.pdf」和「合約 (2).pdf」會耗掉律師一個下午。檔名寫日期和對方，舊的丟進一個叫 old 的資料夾。聽起來很小氣。分別是星期一有答案，還是要等人把你的意思重新拼出來。

公司裡要有一個人管這個資料夾。不是顧問，也不是「團隊」。簡報和賬對不上的時候，這個人應該早就看過。一定會被問。停頓本身就是答案。

很多閱讀發生在你去開另一個會的時候，沒有機會解釋腳註。想成交的那一週之前就把資料室弄好。那一週你才發現，去年的購股權根本沒寫下來。
