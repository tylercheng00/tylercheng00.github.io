Temperature, customs, and last-mile handling are easy to leave off the science slide. They belong on the risk page and in the budget. One broken shipment can erase a quarter of goodwill with a site.

Say who owns cold chain, what monitoring you use, and what happens when a logger fails. Boring detail here is operational maturity.
