A collaborator is not a customer合作方不是客戶

A famous lab that will "work with you" has not bought anything. The first time you describe traction, that difference matters.一個願意「合作」的著名實驗室，並沒有買東西。你第一次講進展的時候，這個分別就出來了。

Biotech生物科技10 Nov 20262026年11月10日3 min read閱讀約 3 分鐘

A slide says "in discussion with" three hospitals and a global company. Another says a professor is a collaborator. The room hears demand. Often what exists is polite scientific interest, a material transfer that is not signed, and a logo used too early.

Interest is fine. It is not traction.

I want three columns. Who. What they have signed. What they have done. A signed transfer, a paid pilot, a grant with a budget, a letter that names a date: those are events. "They are keen" is a mood. If the honest list is short, leave it short. One paid pilot is a stronger slide than five unnamed discussions, because a reader can finish the sentence.

Don't put an institution's mark on the page before their contracts people would recognise the relationship. The trouble shows up later, when a real agreement is close and someone senior sees their logo on a deck they never approved. Scientific enthusiasm and permission to use the name are different people. If the answer is slow, describe them instead: a public hospital in southern China, a large pharma company. You lose a little shine. You keep the relationship.

A professor may want a paper, a funded student, a reagent. That can be a fair trade, and it can produce real data. It is not revenue, and it is not proof that a buyer with a budget will pay. Report it as science. The moment it sits in the commercial section, a careful reader starts checking every other line in that section.

Ask, gently, what they would lose if you disappeared in six months. A customer loses a supply or a slot they have already scheduled. A curious collaborator loses an interesting meeting. Keep both. Only one of them belongs in the sentence about the market pulling you.

一頁寫着正跟三家醫院和一家跨國公司洽談。另一頁寫某教授是合作方。房間聽到的是需求。實際常常是一份客氣的科學興趣、一份沒簽的材料轉移、一個用得太早的標誌。

興趣可以。它不是進展。

我要三欄。誰。簽了甚麼。做了甚麼。簽了的轉移、付了錢的試驗、有預算的課題、一封寫了日期的信，這些是事件。「他們很積極」是情緒。誠實的清單短，就讓它短。一個付了錢的試驗，好過五個沒有名字的洽談，因為讀者可以把句子讀完。

對方合約的人還不承認這段關係，不要把機構的標誌放上去。麻煩來得比較晚：協議快成的時候，高層看到自己的標誌在一份他們沒批准的簡報上。科學上的熱，和准你用名字，是不同的人。回覆慢，就改用描述：華南一家公立醫院，一家大藥企。少一點光，關係還在。

教授也許想要論文、一個有資助的學生、一種試劑。這可以是公平交換，也可以有真數據。它不是收入，也不證明有預算的人會付錢。把它當成科學來報。一旦放進商業那一節，謹慎的讀者會把那一節其餘的行都看一遍。

可以溫和地問：六個月後你消失，他們失去甚麼。客戶失去供應，或一個已經排了的位置。好奇的合作方失去一次有趣的會。兩種人都留着。只有一種人屬於「市場在拉我們」那句。
