::: en
Complex holding structures are normal for cross-border companies. Mystery is not. One plain sentence on employment, IP ownership, and cash controls prevents a week of confused diligence.

If the chart is messy, clean what you can before the process. What you cannot clean, disclose. Buyers price clarity. They punish discovery.

::: zh
跨境公司的控股結構複雜很正常。神秘不正常。關於僱傭、知識產權權屬、現金控制的一句白話，能避免一週混亂的盡職審查。

若圖很亂，流程前先清理能清理的。清理不了的，披露。買家為清晰定價。他們懲罰被發現。
