::: en
A buyer asks for exclusivity. It can be reasonable. It can also freeze your process while they shop elsewhere slowly. The document should say when exclusivity ends, what they must deliver by then, and what you may do if they miss it.

If the draft has exclusivity and no clock, add the clock before you sign. Your other conversations are the leverage that makes exclusivity valuable to them. Giving that away for free teaches the room that your calendar is cheap.

::: zh
買家要求排他。可以合理。也可以讓你的流程凍結，而他們慢慢去別處逛。文件應寫明排他何時結束、到期前他們要交付甚麼、若他們做不到你可以做甚麼。

若草案有排他卻沒有時鐘，簽名前先加時鐘。你其他的對話，正是讓排他對他們有價值的槓桿。免費送掉，等於告訴房間：你的日曆很便宜。
